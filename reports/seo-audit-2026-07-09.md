# Full SEO Audit — 2026-07-09

Covers technical SEO, on-page content quality, and live ranking visibility (via the SERP Intelligence checker). No ongoing API cost beyond the one bounded SERP check run for this audit (10 keywords, ~$0.05-0.20).

## Summary

The technical foundation is solid — this is not a "rebuild the site" situation. The real problem is that **0 of 10 checked keywords show towwiththeflow.com anywhere in organic results or AI Overviews**, including local city-cost pages that have been live since April/May. The likely causes, in priority order: unoptimized images hurting page speed, a large chunk of legacy content that's thin and poorly linked, and — most simply — competitors who've been indexed longer and have stronger backlink profiles. None of this is unusual for a 3-4 month old site; it's the normal J-curve. The action items below are what shortens it.

## 1. Technical SEO — solid baseline

- ✅ Meta description, canonical URL, Open Graph, Twitter Card tags all present and correctly templated (`themes/twtf/layouts/partials/head.html`)
- ✅ JSON-LD structured data: Organization, WebSite, Article, BreadcrumbList, WebPage schemas all present
- ✅ `robots.txt` and `sitemap.xml` auto-generated by Hugo
- ✅ Site builds with `--minify`
- ❌ **No FAQ schema.** Multiple competitors ranking above TWTF also lack this — it's a real, achievable differentiator. Worth adding `FAQPage` JSON-LD to posts that already have a Q&A-style structure.
- ⚠️ No `LocalBusiness`/`Service` schema. Only add this if TWTF actually represents a bookable local service — if it's a content/referral site, skip it (inaccurate schema is worse than none).

## 2. Page speed — the highest-leverage fix

- **258MB across 816 images**, several single images at 3.6MB, dozens over 500KB — all served at full Pexels resolution with no resizing or compression.
- Images render via plain `<img src>` with `loading="lazy"` but **no Hugo image processing** (no `resize`/`Fill`, no WebP conversion) in `themes/twtf/layouts/_default/single.html` and `list.html`.
- Large Load Contentful Paint (LCP) images are a direct, measurable Google ranking factor. This is very likely costing real ranking positions on every page that has one.
- **Fix:** add Hugo image processing (`.Resize` to a max width matching the display size, e.g. 1200px, plus WebP output) in the two templates above. This doesn't require touching `generate_post.py` — it's a template-level change that would apply to every existing image automatically on next build.

## 3. Content quality — a clean before/after split

The May 17 content-quality update (`eac0681` — internal linking requirement + longer target length) worked. The data splits cleanly:

| | Before 2026-05-17 (152 posts) | On/after 2026-05-17 (141 posts) |
|---|---|---|
| Thin (<400 words) | 87 (57%) | 2 (1%) |
| Fewer than 2 internal links | 151 (99%) | 7 (4%) |

**The fix already worked going forward — the gap is entirely in legacy content.** About 150 older posts are thin and effectively un-linked from the rest of the site, which both weakens them individually and starves the newer, better posts of internal link equity.

**Action:** a backfill pass on the ~150 pre-05-17 posts — bring them up toward the 500-900 word target and add 2-4 internal links each. This is exactly what `generate_post.py`'s internal-linking prompt already does for new posts; it just needs to run against old content too.

Smaller findings:
- **2 pairs of duplicate-titled pages** targeting the same keyword (e.g. `how-much-does-a-tow-truck-cost-in-denver-colorado` and its `-2` sibling) — likely keyword cannibalization, splitting ranking signal across two pages instead of one. Worth merging or clearly differentiating each pair.
- **1 broken internal link** (points to a slug that doesn't exist; a near-miss typo of three other real posts) — minor, one occurrence.
- **12 meta descriptions** run slightly over the 155-char guideline (up to 162 chars) — low severity, Google truncates gracefully, not worth a dedicated pass.
- **41 "towing cost in [city]" pages** exist. Individually each has enough local specificity to avoid being a doorway page, but worth keeping an eye on as the count grows — no action needed now.

## 4. Live ranking visibility (SERP Intelligence check, 2026-07-08)

Checked the 10 highest-priority keywords with live posts (5 local city pages, 5 pain-point scenarios). **Full breakdown: `reports/serp-intelligence-2026-07-08.md`.**

- **0/10 show towwiththeflow.com** in organic results or an AI Overview.
- Pattern across every keyword checked: the pages currently ranking are often *not* better content than TWTF's — several are thin, self-promotional "call us for a quote" pages with no actual pricing, no FAQ, no structured data. What they have that TWTF likely doesn't yet: **longer time indexed and a stronger backlink/domain-authority profile.**
- This reinforces #2 and #3 above rather than pointing at a content-quality problem: fix page speed, backfill legacy content and its internal links, and add FAQ schema where competitors are visibly missing it — then give it time. Ranking movement on a young domain is measured in months, not weeks.

## Priority order

1. **Image optimization** (page speed) — template-level fix, affects every page immediately, no content rewrite needed.
2. **Backfill the ~150 legacy posts** — bring them to current word-count/internal-linking standard.
3. **Add FAQ schema** to Q&A-structured posts — cheap, and directly targets a gap seen in competitors.
4. **Resolve the 2 duplicate-title pairs** — merge or differentiate.
5. Re-run the SERP check in a few weeks (Wednesdays, or manually via `workflow_dispatch`) to see if 1-3 move the needle before concluding anything more is needed.

None of these require new Claude API spend beyond what's already scheduled weekly.
