Use the calculator above to get a realistic range before you go to retrieve a stored or impounded vehicle. It's built from typical national pricing patterns — not a quote from any specific lot — so treat the range as a sanity check, not a guarantee.

## How this estimate works

The calculator adds up a base admin fee, a per-day storage charge for the number of days you enter, and adjustments for lot type and vehicle size. Releasing the vehicle outside normal business hours often adds an extra fee.

None of this is submitted anywhere — every calculation happens in your browser.

## What actually moves the price

- **Days in storage** is usually the single biggest factor — fees accrue daily, and most lots count any part of a day as a full one.
- **Lot type** matters because private lots set their own rates, while police and city impound lots are often regulated with a capped daily rate.
- **Vehicle size** — larger vehicles take up more space and are sometimes billed at a higher daily rate.
- **Release timing** — retrieving the vehicle outside normal business hours often costs extra.

## Common Questions

**Q: How accurate is this storage fee estimate?**
A: It's a planning estimate, not a quote. Storage rates vary widely, especially at private lots — call ahead and ask for the exact daily rate and any release fee before you go.

**Q: Do police or city impound lots charge less than private lots?**
A: Often yes, since many are regulated by local ordinance with a capped daily rate. Private tow companies set their own rates, which can vary more.

**Q: Can I avoid storage fees entirely?**
A: Often yes, if you're present when the vehicle is towed — you can usually request it go directly to a shop or address of your choice instead of a storage lot. Once it's already in storage, the fees have started accruing.
