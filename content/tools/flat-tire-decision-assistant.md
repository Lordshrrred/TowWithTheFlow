Use the questions above to figure out your next step. A flat tire is rarely an emergency, but where you are and what you have with you changes the right answer.

**This is not professional roadside or mechanical advice.** If the vehicle is stopped somewhere unsafe, moving to safety comes before anything else.

## How this tool works

Location comes first — a flat tire in a travel lane is a safety issue regardless of the tire itself. After that, whether you have a spare (and a run-flat versus a standard tire) determines whether driving carefully, changing it yourself, or calling for help is the right call.

None of this is submitted anywhere — every question and recommendation happens in your browser.

## What actually determines the answer

- **Location** — a flat tire in an unsafe spot always comes before deciding what to do about the tire itself.
- **Tire type** — run-flat tires are built to be driven a short distance at reduced speed; standard tires are not.
- **Spare availability** — no spare means roadside assistance or a tow is the practical answer.
- **Your comfort level** — there's no wrong answer between doing it yourself and calling for help.
