> **Quick Answer:** Progressive pays for towing through two separate coverages. Roadside Assistance (an optional add-on) typically covers towing with no set dollar cap but uses Progressive's contracted tow network. Comprehensive or collision coverage reimburses towing if the breakdown is tied to a covered claim. Without one of these add-ons, Progressive pays nothing for a tow.

## How Much Will Progressive Actually Pay for a Tow?

The answer depends on which coverage you have active on your policy. If you have Progressive's Roadside Assistance add-on, they dispatch a contracted tow truck and pay the bill directly, with no mileage cap stated in most standard policies. If your car needs a tow because of a covered accident and you have comprehensive or collision, towing and labor costs are folded into that claim, subject to your deductible.

The critical detail: Roadside Assistance and comprehensive/collision are separate products. You can have one without the other.

## What Is Progressive Roadside Assistance and What Does It Cover?

Progressive Roadside Assistance covers towing, jump-starts, flat tire changes, fuel delivery, and lockout service. When you call, they dispatch through a partner network and pay the tow company directly. You typically owe nothing out of pocket for a basic tow to the nearest qualified shop.

The coverage does not have a published per-incident dollar cap the way State Farm's does (State Farm caps roadside reimbursement at $80 to $100 per event depending on the policy tier). Progressive's model is dispatch-based, meaning they control cost by controlling which tow company responds, not by capping your reimbursement. If you hire your own tow truck outside their network, you may get reimbursed at a lower rate or not at all. Call first, always.

If you want to compare how this stacks up against another insurer's approach, see the breakdown on [State Farm roadside assistance dollar limits](/state-farm-roadside-assistance-dollar-limit-towing/).

## What If I Use a Tow Truck That Is Not in Progressive's Network?

You will likely pay out of pocket, or receive only partial reimbursement. Progressive's roadside product is a dispatch service, not an open reimbursement program. If you call an independent tow company first and then try to file for reimbursement, Progressive may deny the claim or offer a fraction of the actual bill.

If you already called a tow and are wondering whether Progressive will cover it, submit the receipt anyway. Some adjusters will approve it, especially if you can document that the situation was urgent or that no network tow was available in your area. Keep every receipt and take photos of your car's location and condition before the tow.

Out-of-network tows in major markets run $75 to $150 for a local hook, more for flatbed or long distance. ## How Do I Know If I Have Roadside Assistance on My Policy Right Now?

Log into your Progressive account or app and look under your policy coverages for "Roadside Assistance" or "Emergency Road Service." It will show as a line item with a small monthly cost, usually $1.50 to $3.00 per vehicle per month. If it is not listed, you do not have it.

You can add it mid-policy. If you are stranded right now and do not have it, adding it in the moment and then immediately filing a claim is fraud. Pay for the tow now, add the coverage later.

## Common Questions

**Q: Does Progressive Roadside Assistance have a mileage limit on tows?**
A: Progressive does not publish a hard mileage cap for standard Roadside Assistance tows. Their network tows you to the nearest qualified repair facility. Very long tows, say over 100 miles, may require supervisor approval or a separate authorization.

**Q: Can I use Progressive roadside assistance more than once per year?**
A: Yes. Progressive does not impose an annual use limit on roadside assistance calls the way some insurers do. However, frequent use may flag your account and affect renewals at the company's discretion.

**Q: Will filing a roadside assistance tow claim raise my insurance rates?**
A: Generally no. Roadside assistance claims are not at-fault incidents and typically do not affect your premium. Filing a collision claim after an accident is a different story and can raise rates.

**Q: What if the tow bill is more than Progressive is willing to pay?**
A: Get an itemized invoice from the tow company and call Progressive to dispute any shortfall. If the discrepancy is large, ask for a supervisor. If it involves an out-of-network tow, you may be responsible for the difference above what they authorize.

**Q: Does Progressive cover towing for a flat tire or do I need a separate plan?**
A: Roadside Assistance covers tire changes, not towing for a flat unless the spare is already flat or unusable. If you are running on a flat spare and need a tow, that is still covered under Roadside Assistance.
