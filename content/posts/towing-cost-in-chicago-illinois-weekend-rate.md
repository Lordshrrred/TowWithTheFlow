## What Does a Weekend Tow Actually Cost in Chicago?

On a Saturday or Sunday, a private tow company in Chicago will charge you $95 to $175 for a local tow under 5 miles, before any extras. That base price includes the hookup fee and the first few miles. After that, per-mile rates run $4 to $7 depending on the company and how far out you are.

The weekend surcharge is real and it is standard. Most Chicago towing companies treat Saturday and Sunday identically to after-hours weekday calls, meaning the surcharge kicks in regardless of whether it is 10 AM Sunday morning or 2 AM Saturday night. Budget $25 to $50 extra on top of whatever the base rate quote is.

A flatbed, which you want for AWD, 4WD, or low-clearance vehicles, adds another $20 to $40 over a standard wheel-lift tow.

## Does the Neighborhood or Highway You're On Change the Price?

Yes, location matters a lot in Chicago. If you break down on I-90/94 near the Cicero Avenue interchange on the South Side, you are on a city expressway, and the Illinois Department of Transportation contracts specific tow companies for those lanes through the IDOT Emergency Towing program. Those contracted tows have a set rate structure, but you still pay out of pocket if you don't have coverage.

In neighborhoods like Pilsen, Logan Square, or Wicker Park, you have more options for independent towers, which often quote lower base rates but may charge a premium to come out on weekends. In the far northwest suburbs or near O'Hare, some companies tack on a distance fee just to dispatch to your location.

Tows originating from the Stevenson Expressway (I-55) near Midway Airport tend to run higher on weekends because demand from travelers is steady and companies know it. If you're on Lake Shore Drive, be aware that access for tow trucks can be restricted during events, which may increase your wait time and your bill.

## How Do Weekend Rates Compare to Weekday Rates?

A weekday tow during business hours in Chicago typically runs $75 to $125 for the same local distance. Switching to a Saturday or Sunday adds roughly 20 to 35 percent to that baseline. A tow that costs $100 on a Tuesday afternoon will often run $130 to $145 on a Sunday morning.

The gap closes somewhat if you have AAA or another roadside assistance plan, since those plans do not typically apply a separate weekend surcharge on their end. You pay your membership fee and they dispatch. ## What Should I Do Right Now If I'm Stuck in Chicago on a Weekend?

Pull completely off the roadway if possible. On the Dan Ryan, the Eisenhower, or any Chicago expressway, stay in your car with your seatbelt on until help arrives. Turn on your hazard lights immediately.

Call 911 if you are in a dangerous position on an active lane. For a standard breakdown on the shoulder, call a tow company directly or use your roadside assistance number. If you want to find a lower weekend rate, call two or three companies and ask specifically: "What is your weekend hookup fee and per-mile rate?" Get the quote in writing via text before they dispatch.

Avoid accepting a tow from someone who approaches you unsolicited on the expressway. These are sometimes unlicensed operators who charge inflated rates and then hold your car until you pay. Licensed Chicago tow operators are required to provide a written estimate on request.

If you broke down near a major interchange like the Jane Byrne (I-90/94 and I-290), stay aware of your surroundings. That section sees heavy weekend traffic and tow trucks need space to work safely. ## Common Questions

**Q: Is towing more expensive on Sunday than Saturday in Chicago?**
A: Most companies charge the same weekend surcharge on both days. A handful apply a higher rate on Sundays before noon, but this is not the standard. Always ask when you call.

**Q: Can I negotiate the weekend tow rate once the truck is already there?**
A: You can try, but you have very little leverage once the truck is hooked up. Negotiate and get a quoted rate before they dispatch. Once your car is on the truck, you're paying what they say.

**Q: What if the city tows my car on a weekend instead of a private company?**
A: Chicago city tows (typically for parking violations or accident scenes) have their own fee schedule, currently around $150 to $200 for the initial tow plus daily storage fees. Weekends do not reduce those fees, and the storage clock starts immediately.

**Q: Does my credit card roadside assistance work on weekends in Chicago?**
A: Most credit card roadside programs (Visa, Mastercard Platinum, Amex) work 24/7 including weekends. Coverage limits vary widely, from $50 to $150 per incident. Call the number on the back of your card before you call a tow truck.

**Q: How long does a tow truck take to arrive on weekends in Chicago?**
A: Expect 30 to 60 minutes on a typical weekend. During a Bears or Cubs game day, or after a major accident on the expressway, wait times can stretch to 90 minutes or more. Call as early as possible and stay on hold if needed rather than hanging up and starting over.
