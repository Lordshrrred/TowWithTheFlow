> **Quick Answer:** Weekend towing in Phoenix runs $125-200 for the hook-up, plus $3-5 per mile. Expect 25-50% higher rates than weekdays. Long-distance tows (over 10 miles) can hit $300-500 total. Call multiple companies for quotes.

## What To Do

1. **Get multiple quotes immediately** - Prices vary wildly between companies, especially on weekends
2. **Ask for the total breakdown** - Hook-up fee, per-mile rate, and any weekend surcharges
3. **Confirm your exact location** - Phoenix metro is huge, and distance affects your final bill
4. **Check if your insurance covers towing** - Many policies include roadside assistance that covers weekend rates
5. **Ask about payment methods** - Some companies charge extra for card payments on weekends
6. **Get arrival time estimate** - Weekend demand means longer waits, sometimes 2-3 hours

On step one, don't just call one company and accept the price. Call at least three. In a metro as large as Phoenix, the difference between the cheapest and most expensive quote for the same tow can easily be $75-100. Weekend dispatchers know you're stuck and some will price accordingly. If a company won't give you a firm quote over the phone, that's a red flag.

On step four, check before you need it. AAA memberships cover up to 5 miles free on a standard plan and up to 100 miles on premium tiers. Many auto insurance policies include roadside assistance as a low-cost add-on, sometimes as little as $10-15 per year. If you have it, use it. The insurer negotiates flat rates with contracted towers, so weekend surcharges often don't apply the same way.

On step six, waits of 2-3 hours are realistic on Saturday nights, especially after 10 PM or during major events at State Farm Stadium or Chase Field. If you're on a highway shoulder, call 911 first so they're aware of your position, then call a tow company.

## What It Might Cost

**Base weekend rates in Phoenix:**
- Hook-up fee: $125-200 (vs $85-150 weekdays)
- Per-mile rate: $3-5 (same as weekdays)
- Weekend surcharge: $25-50 additional
- After-hours fee (nights): $50-75 extra

**Real examples:**
- Short tow (5 miles): $150-225
- Medium tow (15 miles): $200-300
- Long tow (25+ miles): $300-500

**Premium services cost more:**
- Flatbed vs wheel-lift: Add $25-50
- Motorcycle towing: Add $50-100
- Heavy-duty (trucks/RVs): $200-400 base rate

A few things that catch people off guard on the final bill. Some companies measure from their dispatch location to your car, not just from your car to the destination. Ask explicitly: "Is the mileage rate calculated from my location to the drop-off, or from your yard?" That single question can save you $30-60. Also, if your car needs to be winched out of a ditch, off a curb, or out of a parking structure, expect a winching fee of $50-150 on top of the standard hook-up rate. This is separate from the tow itself and is almost never mentioned upfront unless you ask.

Card surcharges are also common on weekends. If a company adds 3-4% for credit card use and your bill is $300, that's nearly $12 extra for the convenience of not having cash. Knowing this ahead of time lets you plan.

## Common Questions

**Q: Can I negotiate the price with a tow company on a weekend?**
A: Yes, and it works more often than people expect. If you have a competing quote in hand, mention it directly. Companies would rather take a slightly lower fare than lose the job entirely. This works better for local private operators than for large dispatch networks.

**Q: What happens if I can't pay the tow bill upfront?**
A: Most tow companies require payment before releasing your vehicle, and in Arizona they have legal authority to hold your car until the bill is paid. If you're short on cash, call your bank about an emergency credit increase or ask a family member to pay over the phone with a card before the driver unhooks your car.

**Q: Is it always cheaper to wait until Monday for a non-emergency tow?**
A: If your car is parked safely on private property or in a legal spot and doesn't need to move immediately, yes. Waiting until Monday morning can save you $50-100 in weekend surcharges and after-hours fees. Just make sure the vehicle is secure and won't be ticketed or towed by the city before you call.

## Stay Safe

- Stay with your vehicle if safely possible
- Use hazard lights and reflective triangles
- Don't accept rides from unlicensed tow operators
- Verify the company name matches what you called
- Take photos of your car before it gets hooked up
- Get a written receipt with all charges listed
