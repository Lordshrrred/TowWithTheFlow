> **Quick Answer:** Call 911 first if you're stuck in a live lane on I-75, I-94, I-96, or the Lodge Freeway (M-10). If you're safely on a shoulder or side street, call your roadside provider (AAA, your insurer, or your car maker's assistance line). No coverage? Call a local Detroit towing company directly and ask for the price before they dispatch.

## Who Do I Call First When My Car Breaks Down in Detroit?

Call 911 first if your car is blocking traffic or you feel unsafe. Otherwise, call whichever roadside coverage you already have. It's usually free or prepaid, and it beats paying cash.

Check these in order:

1. **Your insurance card or app.** Many auto policies include roadside add-ons.
2. **AAA**, if you're a member. Have your membership number ready.
3. **Your car maker's roadside program.** Newer vehicles often include it for the first few years. Look in the owner's manual or the manufacturer's app.
4. **Your credit card.** Some cards include dispatch or reimbursement.

If none of those apply, search "towing near me" and call a local company directly. Skip the first ad you see. Call two shops and compare. Our [Detroit towing cost breakdown](/towing-cost-detroit-michigan/) shows what a fair price looks like.

## What Do I Do If I Break Down on I-75, I-94, or the Lodge Freeway?

Get as far right as you safely can, turn on your hazards, and call 911 if you can't clear the lane. Detroit freeways have narrow shoulders in stretches, and the sunken sections of I-94 and the Lodge (M-10) near downtown have very little room.

Do this:

- Coast toward the nearest exit ramp or wide shoulder if the car still moves.
- Hazards on. Wheels turned away from traffic.
- Exit on the passenger side, away from the lanes, and stand well behind the guardrail.
- Do not stay in the car on a narrow shoulder with fast traffic beside you, unless it's the only safe spot.
- Don't try to push the car or fix it yourself.

Michigan State Police patrols the freeways here, and 911 will route you to them. For road closures and crashes that affect your wait, the MDOT Mi Drive app and the state's 511 service show live conditions.

## Which Towing Company Should I Trust in Detroit?

Pick a company that quotes a price up front, gives you an arrival window, and tells you their business name when they answer. Detroit has plenty of legitimate operators, and some predatory ones working freeway breakdowns.

Red flags:

- A driver who shows up before you called anyone. Unsolicited tow trucks near a breakdown are a known scam, especially on I-94 and the Southfield Freeway.
- No price, or "we'll figure it out when we get there."
- Pressure to sign

## How Much Will a Tow Cost Me in Detroit?

Expect a base hook-up fee plus a per-mile charge, and expect it to climb at night, on weekends, and in bad weather. Detroit winters push demand hard, and snow or ice on the freeways stretches wait times and prices.

Ask for three numbers: the hook-up fee, the per-mile rate, and the total to your destination. ## Common Questions

**Q: Should I call 911 or a tow company when my car breaks down in Detroit?**
A: Call 911 if you're in a live lane, hurt, or in danger. If you're safely off the road, call roadside assistance or a tow company directly.

**Q: Is it safe to wait in my car on a Detroit freeway?**
A: Only if there's no safe place outside. On a narrow shoulder with fast traffic, get out on the passenger side and stand behind the guardrail while you wait.

**Q: Can I get towed in Detroit if I don't have insurance or AAA?**
A: Yes. Call a local tow company, get the hook-up fee, per-mile rate, and total in writing, and pay by card so you have a record.

**Q: What if a tow truck shows up that I didn't call?**
A: Don't let them hook up your car. Unrequested trucks near breakdowns are a common scam. Wait for the company you called, and confirm the name and plate.

**Q: How long will I wait for a tow in Detroit?**
A: It varies with weather, time of day, and whether it's a freeway or side street. Ask the dispatcher for an arrival window, and check how long you should realistically wait on a highway in [this guide](/how-long-can-you-wait-for-a-tow-truck-on-highway/).
