> **Quick Answer:** With no insurance or roadside plan, you pay the tow company directly, usually by card at drop-off. A local Memphis tow typically runs $75 to $200 depending on distance, time of day, and vehicle type. Highway pickups, nights, and weekends cost more. Get the total quoted before the truck is hooked up, and ask for an itemized receipt.

## How Much Will a Tow Cost in Memphis With No Insurance?

Expect roughly $75 to $200 for a local tow in Memphis, paid by you directly. Insurance doesn't change what the tow company charges. It only changes who pays. Without coverage, the full bill is yours.

Most companies charge a hook-up fee plus a per-mile rate. A short tow from a Midtown or Cordova street costs the least. A long haul from the I-240 loop out to Collierville or Bartlett adds up fast on mileage alone.

Extras that raise the bill:

- **Nights, weekends, holidays:** often a surcharge on top of the base rate.
- **Flatbed required:** all-wheel-drive vehicles, low cars, and anything with transmission trouble usually need one. See [flatbed towing costs in Memphis](/towing-cost-in-memphis-tennessee-flatbed/).
- **Highway pickup:** recovery on a busy interstate can cost more than a surface-street tow.
- **Winching:** if you're in a ditch or off the shoulder, expect an added recovery charge.

For a fuller breakdown of rates, read [towing cost in Memphis, Tennessee](/towing-cost-in-memphis-tennessee/).

## Who Do I Call First If I Have No Insurance or Roadside Plan?

Call a local Memphis tow company directly, and call two if you're safe enough to wait. Asking for a total price over the phone is normal. A reputable dispatcher will give you a number or a tight range.

Ask these before agreeing:

1. What's the total to tow from my exact location to my destination?
2. Is that flatbed or wheel-lift?
3. Are there any fees on top of that quote?
4. What payment do you take? (Many want card or cash on drop-off.)

If you're on the interstate, your first call is 911 if you're in a dangerous spot, then a tow. On I-40 near the Hernando de Soto Bridge or I-240 through Midtown, shoulders are narrow and traffic is fast. If you can't get fully off the road, stay buckled in with hazards on and tell dispatch exactly where you are. More on that in [who to call when your car breaks down on a Memphis highway](/car-broke-down-on-memphis-highway-who-to-call/).

Skip any driver who shows up unrequested. Unsolicited trucks on Memphis interstates and around busy exits are a known source of inflated bills.

## What Are My Cheaper Options If I Can't Afford the Tow?

Your cheapest move is usually the shortest tow to a trusted shop, not the shop you'd ideally pick. Mileage is the biggest lever you control.

- **Tow to a closer shop** and have the car moved later when you have time to shop around.
- **Tow to your home** if the repair can wait. A short tow home beats a long tow to a distant garage.
- **Ask a mechanic you already use** whether they run their own tow truck or partner with a company. Some shops discount towing when you're booking the repair.
- **Check whether the problem is actually a tow.** A dead battery or flat tire may only need a jump or a spare, which costs far less. A [roadside assistance plan](/towing-cost-in-memphis-tennessee-without-roadside-assistance/) is worth pricing for next time, since a membership can cost less than a single tow.

Never skip the tow and push a car with a failing transmission, no brakes, or a leaking coolant system. A $100 tow beats a $2,500 engine.

## Common Questions

**Q: Can a tow company refuse to tow my car if I have no insurance?**
A: No. Towing is a paid service, not an insured one. As long as you can pay, insurance status doesn't matter. Some companies may require payment upfront or on delivery.

**Q: Do I have to pay before the tow truck leaves with my car?**
A: Most Memphis tow companies collect when they drop off your car, though some ask for payment at pickup. Clarify this when you call so there are no surprises at the shop.

**Q: What if I don't have money for the tow right now?**
A: Tell the dispatcher upfront. Some companies will take a card later or work out a short arrangement, but expect storage fees if the car sits at their lot while you sort it out. Storage charges per day can erase any savings.

**Q: Is it cheaper to tow to a shop or to my house?**
A: Whichever is closer. Towing cost follows mileage more than destination, so a short tow home often costs less than a long haul to a far-off shop.

**Q: Will my regular car insurance cover towing after the fact if I don't have roadside coverage?**
A: Usually not. Towing is typically an add-on, not part of basic liability. Check your policy before you assume. Details are in [does car insurance cover roadside assistance if I add it](/does-car-insurance-cover-roadside-assistance-if-i-add-it/).
