> **Quick Answer:** Sometimes. GEICO's Emergency Road Service add-on covers towing for breakdowns, and GEICO can arrange a tow after a crash, but who actually pays depends on your policy. Collision coverage typically covers towing a car that was damaged in an accident, subject to your deductible and policy terms. Check your declarations page or the GEICO app to see what you carry.

## Does GEICO Roadside Assistance Cover Towing After an Accident?

GEICO's Emergency Road Service (ERS) is built for breakdowns, and whether it applies after a crash depends on how your policy is written. ERS is an optional add-on, so first confirm you have it. It covers things like towing, jump-starts, flat tire changes, and lockouts.

An accident tow is more commonly handled under your collision coverage, which pays to repair your car, and towing to a shop is typically part of that claim. Some policies also include towing and labor coverage. The details vary by state and policy, so read your declarations page or ask GEICO directly.

## How Do I Get a Tow From GEICO Right Now?

Open the GEICO app or call the number on your insurance card, and tell them you were in an accident and need a tow. Do it from a safe spot, not standing in a lane.

Do this first:

1. **Call 911 if anyone is hurt** or the car is blocking traffic.
2. **Get everyone out of the car and away from traffic.** On a highway, that means behind the guardrail.
3. **Photograph the scene** before the car moves: all four corners, the damage, the other vehicle, the plates.
4. **Open a claim with GEICO** and ask whether they dispatch the tow or reimburse you.
5. **Ask where the car is going.** Get the shop name and address in writing or in the app.

Don't let a random tow truck that shows up at the scene hook up your car. Police-called tows and uninvited "chasers" often take cars to lots that charge storage by the day. If police order a tow because the car is blocking the road, you can still ask to have it moved to a shop of your choice afterward.

## Who Pays If the Accident Wasn't My Fault?

The at-fault driver's insurance is ultimately responsible for your towing, but you may have to cover it upfront and get reimbursed. Waiting on the other insurer can take days, and storage fees pile up in the meantime.

Many drivers use their own collision coverage to get the car moving, and GEICO then pursues the other insurer through subrogation. If you pay a deductible, you can often get it back once the other side is found at fault. We break down the details in [who pays for towing after a car accident that wasn't your fault](/who-pays-for-towing-after-a-car-accident-not-my-fault/).

Keep every receipt. If you pay a tow driver out of pocket, get an itemized invoice showing hookup, mileage, and any storage.

## What If I Only Have Liability Coverage?

Liability-only policies generally do not pay to tow or repair your own car. Liability covers damage you cause to others, not damage to your vehicle. If that's all you carry, GEICO won't typically pay for your tow unless

## How Far Will GEICO Tow My Car, and What Does It Cost Me?

For a covered tow, distance limits depend on the coverage. Collision-based towing usually goes to a repair facility or a storage location tied to the claim. ERS towing has its own mileage or dollar caps that vary by policy, so check yours. Our guide on [how far insurance will tow your car for free](/how-far-will-insurance-tow-my-car-for-free/) covers typical limits.

If you pay out of pocket, expect a local tow to run roughly $75 to $150 for the hookup and first few miles, plus a per-mile rate after that. Flatbeds cost more, and a car with all-wheel drive or frame damage usually needs one. Post-accident cars often need a flatbed because of bent axles, damaged steering, or locked wheels.

Storage is the real budget killer. A car sitting at an impound or tow yard can accrue a daily fee. Get it moved to a shop fast. See [what impound lots charge per day](/how-much-does-impound-lot-charge-per-day/).

## Common Questions

**Q: Does GEICO cover towing if I'm at fault?**
A: Usually yes, if you carry collision coverage, since towing to a repair shop is typically part of the claim. You'll owe your deductible. Liability-only drivers typically aren't covered for their own car.

**Q: Do I need to add Emergency Road Service to get a tow after a crash?**
A: Not always. Collision coverage may handle an accident tow without ERS. ERS is the add-on aimed at breakdowns, so verify on your policy documents which one applies.

**Q: Can I choose which tow company or shop GEICO uses?**
A: You can usually request a specific shop, but GEICO may dispatch its own tow provider. Ask before the truck hooks up, and tell the driver the destination you've confirmed with GEICO.

**Q: Will using GEICO roadside assistance raise my rates?**
A: Roadside service claims generally aren't treated like at-fault accident claims, but an accident claim itself can affect your rates depending on fault. Ask GEICO how your specific situation is handled.

**Q: What if the tow truck damages my car?**
A: Document the damage with photos before and after, get it noted on the invoice, and file a complaint with the tow company and its insurer. See [what happens if a tow truck damages your car](/what-happens-if-tow-truck-damages-my-car/).
