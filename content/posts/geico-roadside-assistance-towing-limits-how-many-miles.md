> **Quick Answer:** GEICO roadside assistance covers towing up to 15 miles per incident with most policies. You pay the full cost for any distance beyond 15 miles. This limit resets with each new breakdown, and coverage typically includes up to 5 service calls per policy period.

## What GEICO Covers for Towing

GEICO's standard roadside assistance provides towing coverage up to 15 miles from your breakdown location. This distance limit applies to each individual towing incident, not annually.

The 15-mile limit covers the actual towing distance, not the straight-line distance between pickup and drop-off points. If your mechanic is 12 miles away but the tow truck driver takes a 16-mile route due to traffic or road conditions, you pay for the extra mile.

Coverage includes towing to the repair facility of your choice within the 15-mile radius. You can request towing to a dealership, independent mechanic, or your home garage as long as it falls within the distance limit.

## What You Pay Beyond 15 Miles

When your preferred repair shop sits beyond the 15-mile coverage limit, you pay the additional distance at the tow company's standard per-mile rate. Most tow companies charge $3 to $7 per mile beyond the covered distance.

For example, if your chosen mechanic is 25 miles away, GEICO covers the first 15 miles. You pay for the remaining 10 miles at the tow company's rate, typically $30 to $70 extra.

The tow truck driver will inform you of the additional charges before exceeding your coverage limit. You can choose to go to a closer facility within the 15-mile radius to avoid extra costs.

## How Often You Can Use Towing Coverage

GEICO roadside assistance typically allows up to 5 service calls per policy period. Each towing incident counts as one service call, regardless of the distance towed within your coverage limit.

The 15-mile limit resets with each new breakdown. If you need towing twice in one month, you get 15 miles of coverage for each incident, not 15 miles total.

## When GEICO Won't Cover Towing

GEICO roadside assistance excludes towing in certain situations. Coverage doesn't apply to commercial vehicles, motorcycles, or vehicles used for ride-sharing or delivery services during business hours.

The service won't cover towing from private property without the owner's permission, such as apartment complex parking lots or shopping centers where you don't have authorization to be there.

Pre-existing mechanical problems that you knew about before the policy began aren't covered. If your transmission has been slipping for weeks and finally fails, GEICO might deny the claim.

Keep your preferred mechanic's address handy and measure the distance from common locations where you drive. If your regular shop is 18 miles from your workplace, factor in the extra cost when deciding whether to use GEICO's service or pay for private towing.

## Common Questions

**Q: Does the 15-mile towing limit apply per breakdown or per year?**
A: The 15-mile limit applies per individual breakdown incident, not per year. Each time your car breaks down, you get a fresh 15 miles of towing coverage, up to 5 total service calls per policy period.

**Q: What happens if the tow truck takes a longer route than the straight-line distance to my mechanic?**
A: GEICO covers the actual miles driven by the tow truck, not the straight-line distance between two points. If a longer route pushes the trip past 15 miles, you pay the tow company's per-mile rate, typically $3 to $7 per mile, for every mile over the limit.

**Q: Can I ask to be towed to my home driveway instead of a repair shop?**
A: Yes, GEICO allows you to choose your tow destination, including your home garage, as long as it falls within the 15-mile coverage limit. If your home is farther than 15 miles from the breakdown location, you will pay out of pocket for the extra distance.
