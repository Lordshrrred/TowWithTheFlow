> **Quick Answer:** In Nashville, expect a hookup fee of roughly $75 to $150 plus about $3 to $7 per mile after the first few miles included. A 10-mile tow often lands between $100 and $200. Flatbeds, nights, and weekends cost more. These are typical ranges, not guaranteed quotes, so always get the total price before the driver hooks up.

## How Much Per Mile Does Towing Cost in Nashville?

Most Nashville tow companies charge about $3 to $7 per mile on top of a base hookup fee, and that fee usually covers the first 5 to 10 miles. Pricing varies by company, so treat these numbers as ranges.

Here is how a typical bill is built:

- **Hookup fee:** $75 to $150, often including the first few miles
- **Mileage after the included miles:** roughly $3 to $7 per mile
- **After-hours or holiday surcharge:** commonly an extra $25 to $75
- **Flatbed surcharge:** sometimes $25 to $50 more than a standard hook-and-chain or wheel-lift truck

Ask whether the mileage is counted from the driver's yard or from your location. Some companies bill from the moment the truck leaves its lot, which pads the total.

## What Will a Typical Tow Cost on I-40, I-65, or I-24?

A tow in the Nashville core, say from I-40 near the Charlotte Pike exit to a shop in East Nashville, usually runs $100 to $200 all in. That puts you in a 8 to 12 mile range, which is where most local tows fall.

Highway tows can cost more because the driver is working next to fast traffic. Stretches like I-65 through the Brentwood corridor or I-24 near the Murfreesboro Pike interchange are tight, and a company may add a highway recovery fee. Ask about it up front.

Longer hauls change the math. A tow from Franklin or Hendersonville into Nashville stacks up miles quickly. At $5 a mile, a 25-mile tow can push past $250 once the hookup is added. ## How Do I Keep From Getting Overcharged on a Per-Mile Tow?

Get the total in writing or by text before the driver touches your car. Then confirm four things:

1. The hookup fee and how many miles it includes
2. The per-mile rate after that
3. Whether mileage starts at the company yard or at your car
4. Whether any storage, after-hours, or highway fees apply

## Does Insurance or Roadside Coverage Pay for the Mileage?

Often yes, up to a limit. Many auto policies and AAA memberships cover a set number of towing miles, commonly in the 5 to 100 mile range depending on the plan. Check your card or app before you pay out of pocket, since a covered tow can cost you nothing.

If you have no coverage, you pay the full per-mile rate. ## Common Questions

**Q: What is the average cost to tow a car 10 miles in Nashville?**
A: Usually $100 to $200 for a standard tow, depending on the hookup fee and per-mile rate. A flatbed or after-hours call can add $25 to $75.

**Q: Do Nashville tow companies charge a flat rate or per mile?**
A: Most use a hybrid: a flat hookup fee that covers the first few miles, then a per-mile charge beyond that. Always ask which one applies to your tow.

**Q: Is towing more expensive at night or on weekends in Nashville?**
A: Yes. Many companies add an after-hours or holiday surcharge, commonly $25 to $75. Ask for the surcharge in your quote.

**Q: Can I pick where my car gets towed?**
A: Yes, unless police or a property owner ordered the tow. Choosing your own shop keeps mileage down and avoids storage fees at a tow yard.

**Q: Does AAA cover towing mileage in Nashville?**
A: AAA plans cover a set number of towing miles, and the limit depends on your membership tier. Check your plan details or the app before calling a private tow.
