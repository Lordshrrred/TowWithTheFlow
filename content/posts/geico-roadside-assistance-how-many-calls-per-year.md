> **Quick Answer:** GEICO roadside assistance has no annual limit on the number of service calls you can make. You can use jump starts, flat tire changes, lockout help, and other covered services as many times as needed per year. However, towing has specific dollar limits and distance restrictions that vary by coverage level.

## What To Do

1. **Check your GEICO policy documents** to confirm you have roadside assistance coverage. Not all policies include it automatically.

2. **Call GEICO directly at 1-800-424-3426** when you need roadside help. Save this number in your phone now.

3. **Understand what counts as unlimited**: Jump starts, flat tire changes, lockout assistance, and minor mechanical help have no call limits.

4. **Know your towing limits**: [GEICO covers towing](https://towwiththeflow.com/geico-roadside-assistance-towing-coverage-how-much-pays/) up to $100 per incident for basic coverage, or higher amounts if you purchased enhanced coverage.

5. **Keep receipts** if you pay out of pocket first. GEICO will reimburse covered services up to policy limits.

## How Many Calls You Get

GEICO does not limit the number of roadside assistance calls per year. You can use these services unlimited times:

- Battery jump starts
- Flat tire changes (if you have a spare)
- Lockout assistance
- Emergency fuel delivery (up to 2 gallons)
- Minor mechanical adjustments

Towing works differently. While you can request towing as many times as needed, each incident has dollar limits. Basic coverage typically covers $100 per tow, while enhanced coverage may cover $200 or more per incident.

If your car needs multiple tows in one year, each separate breakdown counts as a new incident with fresh dollar limits. A tow from your breakdown location to a repair shop, then later from the shop to your home, would be two separate incidents. That matters if you drive an older vehicle that sees repeated mechanical failures. Each event resets the clock on your towing dollar limit, so you are not penalized for having a rough month.

One thing to watch: if you run out of gas twice in a single week, each call still qualifies for the 2-gallon fuel delivery benefit. GEICO does not flag repeat callers or reduce service based on call frequency. The unlimited call structure is genuine, not a soft cap with fine print.

## When You Might Pay Extra

You will pay out of pocket when costs exceed GEICO's coverage limits:

- Towing distances beyond covered miles (usually 15-25 miles for basic coverage)
- Towing costs above dollar limits
- Specialized towing for modified vehicles, lowered cars, or all-wheel-drive vehicles that require flatbed transport
- Weekend or holiday premium charges that exceed coverage

To put real numbers on it: a flatbed tow in a metro area commonly runs $150-250. If your basic GEICO coverage caps at $100, you are writing a check for the difference at the scene. That gap grows fast on rural roads where tow trucks charge higher mileage rates, sometimes $5-10 per mile beyond the first 5-10 miles.

Winching a vehicle out of a ditch or off a curb is another gray area. Some policies cover it, some treat it as a separate service with its own dollar cap. Read your declarations page or call GEICO before you need it.

If you frequently need expensive towing services, compare [GEICO's coverage limits with other options](https://towwiththeflow.com/progressive-insurance-towing-coverage-how-many-times/) to see if different insurance roadside assistance might save money.

## Coverage Varies by State

GEICO roadside assistance terms can vary by state. Some states mandate specific coverage levels or service types. Check your policy documents or call GEICO to confirm your exact coverage limits and included services.

Enhanced roadside packages typically cost $15-25 more per year but provide higher towing dollar limits and longer distance coverage. If you drive an older car or live in a rural area, the upgrade often pays for itself with just one long-distance tow. A single 40-mile tow on an enhanced plan that covers $200 instead of $100 could save you $60-80 on that one call alone, which already covers two to three years of the upgrade cost.

If your vehicle is under manufacturer warranty, check whether roadside assistance is already included. Doubling up on coverage is not necessarily bad, but knowing what you have prevents surprises when a provider tells you to call someone else first.

Remember that using GEICO roadside assistance typically does not affect your insurance rates or claims history, since roadside calls are considered a separate service benefit rather than insurance claims.

## Common Questions

**Q: Does calling GEICO roadside assistance multiple times raise my car insurance premium?**
A: No. Roadside assistance calls are logged as a service benefit, not as insurance claims. They do not appear on your claims history and will not trigger a rate increase, even if you call several times in one year.

**Q: What happens if my tow costs more than GEICO's dollar limit?**
A: You pay the difference directly to the tow company at the scene. Ask for an itemized receipt and confirm the total before the truck hooks up your vehicle so there are no surprises when the bill arrives.

**Q: Can I use GEICO roadside assistance on a rental car or someone else's vehicle?**
A: GEICO roadside assistance is tied to your covered vehicle, not to you as a driver. It does not extend to rental cars or vehicles you borrow. For rentals, you would need coverage through the rental company or a credit card benefit.
