## How Much Does a Flatbed Tow Cost in Denver?

A local flatbed tow in Denver usually runs $100 to $200 for the hook-up plus the first few miles. After that, expect roughly $4 to $8 per mile. Prices vary by company, so treat these as ballpark figures and confirm before you agree.

A few examples of how the math plays out:

- **Short tow within Denver:** A breakdown near Colfax and Colorado Boulevard to a shop in Capitol Hill or Cherry Creek is mostly hook-up fee, often under $175.
- **Mid-range tow:** I-25 near Speer to a shop in Aurora or Lakewood adds mileage, so figure a few dollars per mile on top of the base.
- **Long tow:** Denver to Boulder or Colorado Springs gets priced by the mile for the whole trip. See [long distance towing costs in Denver](/towing-cost-in-denver-colorado-long-distance/) for how that adds up.

## Why Does a Flatbed Cost More Than a Hook-and-Chain or Wheel-Lift Tow?

A flatbed costs more because it carries the whole car off the ground, which protects the drivetrain and suspension. That's why it often runs $25 to $75 above a wheel-lift tow.

Flatbeds are the right call for:

- All-wheel-drive and four-wheel-drive vehicles. Subarus, Audis, and most Colorado trucks and SUVs fall here. Dragging two wheels can damage the center differential or transfer case.
- Low-clearance or lowered cars.
- Anything with a transmission failure or locked wheels.
- Cars with a front or rear bumper that's too damaged for a wheel-lift.

If you drive a front-wheel-drive sedan that runs and rolls fine, a wheel-lift can save you money. Extra fees can add $50 to $150 or more to a flatbed tow, and most of them are avoidable if you ask up front. The base rate is rarely the whole story.

- **After-hours or weekend surcharge:** Often a flat bump or a higher hourly rate. Details in [towing cost in Denver at night](/towing-cost-in-denver-colorado-at-night/).

## What Should I Ask Before the Tow Truck Is Dispatched?

Ask for the all-in price before the driver rolls, because that single question prevents most billing disputes. Phone quotes mean nothing if the number changes at pickup.

Ask these in order:

1. What's the total cost to tow from my location to my destination, including mileage and fees?
2. Are you sending a flatbed, and is it on its way now?
3. What's the ETA?
4. Do you take cards, and will I get an itemized receipt?

Get the quote by text if you can. If a driver shows up and the number jumps with no explanation, you can decline and call another company, though you may owe a dispatch fee.

If you're on a highway shoulder, safety comes before shopping around. Get the car as far right as possible, hazards on, everyone out and away from traffic if the shoulder is narrow. ## Can I Get a Cheaper Flatbed Tow in Denver?

Yes, you can often save $30 to $100 by choosing your destination wisely and checking coverage you already have. A little prep before you call pays off.

- **Check your insurance and any membership first.** Many auto policies and credit card benefits include towing. AAA covers towing up to a set mileage depending on the plan.
- **Tow to the closest reputable shop,** not your favorite one across town. Mileage is the biggest variable you control.
- **Avoid the first truck that shows up unsolicited.** Unrequested tow drivers near accident scenes sometimes charge steep rates.
- **Skip the extras you don't need.** If the car runs and rolls, ask whether a wheel-lift works.

## Common Questions

**Q: How much does a flatbed tow cost per mile in Denver?**
A: Most Denver flatbed operators charge roughly $4 to $8 per mile after the base hook-up fee covers the first few miles. Rates vary by company and time of day.

**Q: Is a flatbed tow worth it for an AWD car?**
A: Yes. Towing an AWD vehicle with two wheels on the ground can damage the drivetrain, so a flatbed is the safe choice even though it costs more.

**Q: Do Denver tow companies charge more at night or in snow?**
A: Many do. After-hours, weekend, and storm-demand surcharges are common, so ask whether the quote already includes them.

**Q: Can I refuse a tow if the price changes when the driver arrives?**
A: You can decline and call another company, but you may still owe a dispatch or service fee. Getting the total price by text beforehand protects you.

**Q: Does AAA cover a flatbed tow in Denver?**
A: AAA covers towing up to a mileage limit that depends on your membership level. Check your plan, since going over the limit means you pay the extra miles yourself.
