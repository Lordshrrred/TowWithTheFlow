> **Quick Answer:** A single tow typically runs $75 to $200 for a local haul and can hit $500 or more for long distances. AAA Classic membership costs around $60 to $75 per year. If you need even one tow annually, membership usually pays for itself. But your car insurance or credit card may already cover towing, which changes the math entirely.

## What Does AAA Membership Actually Cost Right Now?

AAA offers three membership tiers, and the pricing is straightforward. Classic (the base level) runs roughly $60 to $75 per year depending on your region. Plus runs $100 to $120, and Premier sits at $130 to $165. These are individual member prices. Adding a second household member usually costs about half the primary rate.

The key difference between tiers is tow distance. Classic covers up to 5 miles per tow (you pay per mile after that). Plus bumps it to 100 miles. Premier covers up to 200 miles. If your car dies 80 miles from home, Classic leaves you with a big bill. Premier covers the whole haul.

## What Does a Tow Actually Cost If You Pay Out of Pocket?

Most tow companies charge a hookup fee plus a per-mile rate. The hookup fee runs $50 to $100, and the per-mile charge typically falls between $2.50 and $7 depending on the company and your location. A 10-mile local tow usually lands between $75 and $150. A 100-mile interstate tow can run $350 to $600 or more. For a real look at what longer hauls cost, see [how much to tow a car 100 miles on the interstate](/how-much-to-tow-car-100-miles-interstate/).

Specialty tows cost more. If your car is in a ditch, if it needs a flatbed because of a seized engine or transmission failure, or if it's after midnight on a weekend, expect the higher end of those ranges.

## Does Your Car Insurance Already Cover Towing?

It might, and a lot of drivers don't know until they're standing on the shoulder. Roadside assistance add-ons from carriers like GEICO, Progressive, and State Farm typically cost $5 to $15 per year added to your existing policy. That's a fraction of an AAA membership and often covers towing, jump starts, and lockouts. Check your declarations page before you pay for anything.

There's a real catch though: using your insurance for towing can sometimes be counted as a claim and affect your rate at renewal depending on your carrier and state. AAA use never touches your insurance record. If you're curious how your current coverage stacks up, [does GEICO cover towing after breakdown](/does-geico-cover-towing-after-breakdown/) and [Progressive insurance covers towing: how much does it actually pay](/progressive-insurance-covers-towing-how-much/) break down the specifics by carrier.

## Common Questions

**Q: Is AAA worth it if I only drive locally and rarely break down?**
A: Probably not. If you drive a newer car, have insurance with roadside add-on, or rarely use the highway, one tow every few years doesn't justify the annual fee. Run the numbers based on your actual situation.

**Q: Can I call AAA the same day I sign up and get a tow?**
A: Yes, AAA activates immediately. But many regions enforce a 3 to 7 day waiting period for the full Classic tow benefit on brand-new memberships to prevent people from signing up just to use it once. Check your local AAA club's terms before assuming same-day coverage.

**Q: What happens if my tow goes over the mileage limit on my AAA plan?**
A: You pay out of pocket for every mile beyond your tier's limit. On Classic that's anything past 5 miles. AAA charges its contracted rate for the overage, which is typically lower than what you'd pay calling a random tow company cold.

**Q: Does AAA cover towing if my car is totaled in an accident?**
A: AAA does cover towing after accidents in most cases, including when the car is not driveable. For the full breakdown of what applies after a crash, [does AAA pay for towing if car is not driveable after crash](/does-aaa-pay-for-towing-if-car-is-not-driveable-after-crash/) covers the specifics.

**Q: Does comprehensive insurance cover towing costs separately from roadside assistance?**
A: Comprehensive covers damage from events like floods, fire, and theft. It does not automatically include towing. Towing coverage is typically a separate roadside assistance add-on.
