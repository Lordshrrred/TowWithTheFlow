## How Much Does a Flatbed Tow Actually Cost in Chicago?

A local flatbed tow in Chicago runs $95 to $175 for the first 5 to 10 miles, with a per-mile rate of $4 to $7 beyond that. The base fee covers dispatch, hooking up the vehicle, and the initial distance window. A run from Wicker Park to a shop in Logan Square might cost $105. A longer haul from the I-55/I-94 interchange at the Circle to a dealer in Schaumburg could push $200 to $250.

Flatbeds cost more than wheel-lift trucks because the entire vehicle rides on the deck, which protects low-clearance cars, all-wheel-drive vehicles, and anything with suspension damage. If your car can't roll freely, a flatbed is not optional.

For a detailed look at what Chicago towing costs in general, [Towing Cost in Chicago Illinois: What You'll Pay and What to Do](/towing-cost-chicago-illinois/) covers the full picture.

## Why Does Chicago Flatbed Pricing Vary So Much?

Location inside the city is the single biggest variable. Pulling a car off Lake Shore Drive near Soldier Field costs more than a residential pickup in Bridgeport because the driver has to navigate traffic, find a legal staging spot, and take more time hooking up safely. The same applies to any breakdown on I-90/94 through downtown.

Other factors that move the price:

- **Time of day.** Night and weekend rates add $25 to $50 to the base. The truck is available around the clock, but the premium is real.
- **Vehicle size.** A full-size pickup or an SUV adds $20 to $40 over a standard sedan rate.
- **AWD or 4WD.** These vehicles require all four wheels on the deck. Some operators charge a flat AWD surcharge of $25.
- **Winching.** If your car is in a ditch, a parking structure, or jammed against a curb, expect a winch fee of $50 to $100 on top of the tow.

If you're dealing with a weekend breakdown, [Towing Cost in Chicago Illinois Weekend Rate: What You'll Pay Right Now](/towing-cost-in-chicago-illinois-weekend-rate/) has the specific premium breakdown.

## Is a Flatbed Required for My Car, or Can I Use a Cheaper Option?

Use a flatbed if your car is all-wheel drive, front-wheel drive with the front axle damaged, has suspension failure, or sits too low to accept wheel-lift equipment without scraping. Wheel-lift tows are cheaper, typically $75 to $120 for a local run, but they lift only two wheels off the ground. Dragging a seized axle or a damaged driveshaft on a wheel-lift truck will create new damage you will pay to fix.

If you are unsure, tell the dispatcher your make, model, drivetrain type, and what is wrong with the car. A reputable operator will tell you which truck

## What Should I Do Right Now While I Wait for the Flatbed?

Get off the road and as far onto the shoulder as possible. On I-90/94 between the Ohio Street exit and the I-55 split, the shoulders are narrow and traffic moves fast. Turn on your hazards the moment the car stops responding. If you can coast forward to an exit, do it. The ramps at Cermak Road and Stony Island Avenue off I-90/94 are wide enough to wait safely.

Once stopped:

1. Set your parking brake and stay in the car if traffic is close.
2. Call the tow company and give them the nearest mile marker or cross street, not just your GPS address.
3. Call your insurance or roadside assistance program if you have one. Some policies, including AAA, cover flatbed tows up to a set mileage limit, which could eliminate the bill entirely.
4. Do not accept a tow from a driver who approaches you without being called. Predatory towing operators patrol Chicago expressways, and you are not obligated to use them.

## Common Questions

**Q: How much does a flatbed tow cost from O'Hare to downtown Chicago?**
A: That run is roughly 18 to 20 miles, so expect $150 to $220. The base fee covers the first portion, and the per-mile rate applies to the rest. Add $25 to $50 if it's after hours.

**Q: Can I negotiate the flatbed rate with a Chicago tow company?**
A: Sometimes. If you call directly rather than going through an accident scene dispatcher, you have more leverage. Asking for the total cost before they hook up is always fair, and some operators will match a competitor quote if you have one handy.

**Q: Do Chicago tow companies charge extra to tow from a parking garage?**
A: Yes. Tight clearances and low ceilings in city garages slow the job down. Most operators add $25 to $75 for a parking structure pickup, depending on how deep in the structure your car is.

**Q: Will my insurance cover a flatbed tow in Chicago?**
A: Only if you have roadside assistance or comprehensive towing coverage. Basic liability does not cover it. Check your policy before you call a truck, because filing a claim later for a $150 tow is usually not worth the potential rate impact.

**Q: What if I don't have insurance or roadside assistance?**
