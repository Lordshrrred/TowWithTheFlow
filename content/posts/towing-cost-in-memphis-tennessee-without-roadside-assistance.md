## How Much Will a Tow Cost Me in Memphis Without Roadside Assistance?

Expect roughly $75 to $175 for a standard local tow in Memphis, with most of that being the hook-up fee and the first few miles. Beyond that, companies usually charge about $4 to $8 per mile.

Here is what moves the price:

- **Distance:** A tow from Midtown to a shop in Cordova costs far more than one from Whitehaven to a shop down the road.
- **Time of day:** After 6 p.m., weekends, and holidays often add $25 to $75.
- **Vehicle type:** Flatbeds cost more than hook-and-chain or wheel-lift trucks. All-wheel-drive cars, lowered cars, and most newer sedans need a flatbed. See the [flatbed breakdown for Memphis](/towing-cost-in-memphis-tennessee-flatbed/) for specifics.
- **Location:** A car stuck off the shoulder, in a ditch, or in a parking structure triggers winch or recovery fees.

These are typical ranges, not a rate card. Memphis companies set their own prices, so the number you hear on the phone is the one that counts. For a wider look at pricing, the [Memphis towing cost guide](/towing-cost-memphis-tennessee/) covers more scenarios.

## What Should I Do Right Now If My Car Dies on a Memphis Highway?

Get the car as far onto the shoulder as you can, turn on your hazards, and get everyone out on the side away from traffic. Memphis highways are fast and crowded, and the shoulder on I-40 near the Sam Cooper Boulevard split or on I-240 around the Poplar Avenue interchange can be narrow.

Then:

1. **Stay out of the car if you can do so safely.** Stand well behind a guardrail if one exists.
2. **Call for help from a safe spot.** On a major highway, call 911 if you feel unsafe or you're blocking a lane. Otherwise start calling tow companies.
3. **Note your exact location.** Use the nearest exit number, mile marker, or cross street. Your phone's map pin works too.

For the full roadside sequence, read [who to call when your car breaks down on a Memphis highway](/car-broke-down-on-memphis-highway-who-to-call/). If the wait drags on, [how long you can safely wait for a tow](/how-long-can-you-wait-for-a-tow-truck-on-highway/) explains when to escalate.

## How Do I Avoid Getting Overcharged on a Tow in Memphis?

Ask for the total price before the truck is dispatched, because the most common overcharge happens after the car is already hooked up. Don't accept "we'll figure it out when we get there."

Ask these questions on the phone:

- What is the total, including hook-up, mileage, and any fees?
- Is it a flatbed, and does my car need one?
- Do you charge extra for after-hours, winching, or wait time?
- How long until you arrive?

Then protect yourself:

- **Do not accept help from an unsolicited truck.** Drivers who "just happen" to show up on I-55 or near the Walnut Grove Road exit may be looking for a vulnerable customer. Only use a

## Should I Tow to a Shop or My Home in Memphis?

Tow to your home if the repair isn't urgent and the shop is far, because you pay by the mile and a long tow to the wrong shop can double your bill. Tow to a shop only if you already trust one nearby.

A few rules of thumb:

- **Shop closed?** Many shops won't accept a car after hours. You may pay storage fees if the tow company has to hold it. Towing home and arranging the shop visit later is often cheaper.
- **Unknown mechanic?** Don't let a tow driver pick the shop for you without asking why. Some drivers earn referral fees.
- **Not sure what's wrong?** A stalling engine or dead battery may be a cheap fix. A seized engine or failed transmission is a different bill entirely. Know the severity before you commit to a destination.

## Is It Cheaper to Pay Out of Pocket or Sign Up for Roadside Assistance?

If you tow more than once every year or two, a roadside plan usually costs less, and you can often buy one right now. Many plans include a set number of tows, covering up to a certain mileage, for an annual fee that's often less than one out-of-pocket tow.

Some plans have a waiting period before coverage starts, so a plan bought on the shoulder may not help today. Check your auto insurance too, since some policies add towing for a small premium. ## Common Questions

**Q: What is the cheapest way to get a car towed in Memphis?**
A: Call two or three local companies and compare total quotes, not just the hook-up fee. Keep the tow short by going home or to a nearby shop. Avoid after-hours if the car is safe where it sits.

**Q: Does it cost more to tow on I-40 or I-240 in Memphis?**
A: The highway itself usually doesn't change the base rate, but a dangerous roadside location can add recovery or wait-time charges. A longer distance to the shop matters more than which interstate you broke down on.

**Q: Can I negotiate a tow price in Memphis?**
A: Sometimes. You have the most leverage before the truck is dispatched. Ask for the lowest total price, mention you're comparing quotes, and confirm the number before agreeing.

**Q: How long does a tow truck take to arrive in Memphis?**
A: Often 30 to 90 minutes, but rush hour on I-40 or bad weather can stretch that. If the wait gets long or you feel unsafe, call another company or 911 if it's an emergency.

**Q: Do I need a flatbed for my car?**
A: Yes if your car is all-wheel-drive, lowered, or has a transmission problem. Flatbeds protect the drivetrain but usually cost more, so tell the dispatcher your car's make and model up front.
