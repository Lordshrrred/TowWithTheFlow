> **Quick Answer:** AAA coverage follows the member, not the vehicle or the passengers. If you are in the car when it breaks down, you get the tow no matter who is driving or riding along. Your passengers ride free with the vehicle. If you are not in the car, your coverage will not apply to a non-member, and they will pay out of pocket or need their own plan.

## Does AAA Cover Towing If I'm in the Car With a Non-Member?

Yes. If you are physically in the vehicle, your AAA membership covers the tow, and your passengers do not need to be members. AAA treats the member as the covered person and the car as the thing being helped.

Passengers typically ride along in the cab of the tow truck or in the vehicle being towed on a flatbed, depending on the driver and the equipment. Ask the dispatcher how many passengers the truck can carry before the driver arrives. A standard flatbed cab fits two or three people, and a wheel-lift truck may fit fewer. If you have four people in the car, tell dispatch up front so they send the right truck or arrange a second ride.

## What If I'm Not in the Car and Someone Else Is Driving It?

If you are not in the vehicle, AAA generally will not cover the tow, because the service is tied to the member being present. Your friend or family member stranded in your car has no member card to invoke.

Sometimes there is a workaround. Depending on your plan and AAA club, the stranded driver may be able to call and have you, the member, request service while giving the tow driver the location. Policies vary by club and plan level, so call the number on your membership card and ask before the tow truck is dispatched. Do not assume it will be approved. I have seen people wait an hour and then get turned back at the curb.

If this is your exact situation, read [Does AAA Cover Towing When Someone Else Is Driving Your Car?](/does-aaa-cover-towing-for-someone-else-driving-my-car/) for the full breakdown.

## What Are My Options If the Person in My Car Isn't a Member and I'm Not There?

Your best options are calling AAA to confirm what your club allows, using your auto insurance roadside add-on, or paying for a private tow. Here is the order I would try them in:

1. **Call AAA with your member number.** Ask whether your club allows you to authorize service for a vehicle you own when you are not present.
2. **Check your auto insurance.** Many policies include optional roadside coverage that follows the vehicle. See [Does Car Insurance Cover Roadside Assistance If I Add It?](/does-car-insurance-cover-roadside-assistance-if-i-add-it/) for how that works.

## Will AAA Cover Towing in My Car If I Lend It to Someone Else Regularly?

Not reliably. Because AAA ties coverage to the member and not the car, lending your vehicle out does not extend your benefits to the borrower. If your teenager, spouse, or roommate drives your car often, they should have their own membership.

AAA offers family and associate plans that add other people on the same account. Each added person is a covered member in their own right and can call for service whether or not they are in your vehicle. If someone else regularly drives your car, adding them is usually cheaper than a single unplanned private tow.

Mileage limits still apply to whoever calls it in. Check [How Far Will AAA Tow My Car for Free?](/how-far-will-aaa-tow-my-car-for-free/) because a Basic plan caps the free distance well below Plus or Premier.

## Common Questions

**Q: Do my passengers need to be AAA members for me to get a tow?**
A: No. As long as you, the member, are in the vehicle, your passengers ride along without needing their own membership. Just confirm how many people the tow truck can carry.

**Q: Can I call AAA for my friend's car if I'm the member and they're stranded?**
A: Generally not. AAA covers the member and the member's vehicle, but your friend's car is not yours and you are not in it. Your friend would need their own membership or a private tow.

**Q: Can I add my spouse or teen driver to my AAA membership?**
A: Yes. AAA offers associate memberships that add other people to your account. Each added member is covered individually, so they can request service even when you are not with them.

**Q: Does AAA cover towing if my passenger is driving and I'm in the front seat?**
A: Yes. You are the member and you are in the vehicle, so the service applies. It does not matter who is behind the wheel.

**Q: What if AAA denies the tow because the member wasn't present?**
A: Ask the dispatcher whether another covered person can authorize it, then try your insurance roadside coverage or a private tow. Get a price quote before the truck hooks up, and see [Can You Negotiate Tow Truck Price?](/can-you-negotiate-tow-truck-price/) if the number looks high.
