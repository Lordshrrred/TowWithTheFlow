Flatbed tows, which are required for most collision-damaged vehicles, run $200 to $400 or more. If police call the tow, you lose price control entirely. ## How Much Does a Tow Cost After an Accident in Atlanta?

A flatbed tow in Atlanta after a collision typically runs $200 to $400 for the first 10 miles. Standard wheel-lift tows are cheaper at $125 to $250, but a damaged car with frame issues, deployed airbags, or locked wheels almost always needs a flatbed. After that first 10 miles, add $4 to $7 per additional mile.

Atlanta's geography makes distance matter fast. If your accident happens on I-20 near Douglasville and the nearest approved body shop is inside the perimeter, you could be looking at 20 to 30 miles. At $5 per mile that adds $100 to $150 on top of the base rate. Know where you want your car delivered before the driver starts loading it.

If your airbag deployed and you're unsure whether the car is safe to move at all, read [Airbag Deployed Car Still Drivable or Need Tow](/airbag-deployed-car-still-drivable-or-need-tow/) before making any decisions.

## Who Controls the Tow After a Crash on I-285 or I-85?

If police respond to the scene, they can call a tow from a rotation list, and you have little say in which company shows up. Rotation tows in Georgia are legal but they are not cheap. You can request a specific company, but on a busy stretch like I-285 near the I-20 interchange or I-85 near the Brookwood split, officers may override that request to clear lanes fast.

Once the tow company hooks your car, you typically owe their rate regardless of fault. Get the tow company's name, truck number, and destination before the vehicle leaves. Call your insurance carrier immediately. Some policies require pre-authorization for towing, and missing that step can cost you reimbursement.

If the other driver was at fault, their liability coverage may cover the tow, but that reimbursement comes later. You still pay the tow company upfront in most cases.

## Does Insurance Cover the Tow After an Atlanta Accident?

Yes, if you have roadside assistance or comprehensive/collision coverage that includes towing. The coverage varies by policy and the amount matters. Many policies cap towing reimbursement at $75 to $100, which does not come close to covering a flatbed tow in Atlanta after a crash.

Call your insurer from the scene if it is safe to do so. They can sometimes dispatch a preferred provider at a negotiated rate. State Farm, Allstate, Geico, and USAA all have roadside programs, but their dollar limits are not always enough. Check your declarations page for the exact towing benefit. If you have been in a prior accident and used roadside coverage recently, confirm you still have remaining benefits before assuming you're covered.

For a deeper look at how coverage plays out when your car cannot be driven, see [Does Insurance Cover Towing If Car Is Not Driveable](/does-insurance-cover-towing-if-car-is-not-driveable/).

## What Should I Do in the First 10 Minutes After an Atlanta Crash?

Move the car out of traffic if it is driveable and safe to do so. On I-75 near the downtown connector or on GA-400 near Buckhead, staying in a lane after a crash is extremely dangerous. Get to the shoulder. Turn on hazard lights. If the car cannot move, stay inside with your seatbelt on and call 911.

Once you are safe, document everything before the tow truck arrives. Photograph the damage, the other vehicle, the license plate, and the scene including lane positions and nearby exit signs. That documentation protects you when you fight a disputed tow bill or file an insurance claim.

Tell the tow driver exactly where you want the car delivered. You have the right to choose your own body shop in Georgia. Do not let the driver default to a shop the tow company prefers unless you have verified that shop independently.

## Common Questions

**Q: Can I refuse a tow truck the police called after my Atlanta accident?**
A: In most cases, no. If police order the tow for traffic safety, you cannot block it. You can request a specific company, but the officer has final authority to clear the roadway.

**Q: How long do I have to pick up my car from an Atlanta impound lot before fees get out of hand?**
A: Storage fees start accruing the day the car arrives, typically $25 to $50 per day. Get there within 24 to 48 hours. After a week, fees can exceed $300 to $400 before you touch the tow bill.

**Q: Will the at-fault driver's insurance pay my tow bill directly?**
A: Usually not upfront. You typically pay out of pocket and submit the receipt to their liability insurer for reimbursement. That process can take days to weeks depending on how quickly fault is determined.

**Q: Is a flatbed always required after a collision in Atlanta?**
A: Not always required by law, but it is almost always the right call. Wheel-lift tows on a crash-damaged car can worsen frame or suspension damage. Any front-wheel-drive car with a damaged front axle, or any car with locked wheels, needs a flatbed.

**Q: What if I have no insurance and need a tow after a crash in Atlanta?**
A: You pay cash or card out of pocket. Rates without any coverage are the same as listed rates, $200 to $400 for a flatbed in the Atlanta metro. There is no discount for being uninsured.
