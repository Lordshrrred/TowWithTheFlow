> **Quick Answer:** Progressive's Roadside Assistance add-on covers towing up to a set dollar limit per incident, typically $75 to $100 for basic plans. If your tow costs more than your limit, you pay the difference out of pocket. The exact cap on your policy depends on which tier you selected and your state. Check your declarations page now, before you need a tow.

## What Is Progressive's Towing Coverage Limit Per Incident?

Progressive caps roadside towing reimbursement at a dollar amount per incident, not per mile or per year. The standard Roadside Assistance add-on generally covers up to $75 to $100 per tow, depending on your state and the coverage tier you purchased. Some states offer a higher-tier option that bumps this limit to $150 or more, but that is not automatic. You only get what you selected when you bought the policy.

This is a reimbursement-based benefit in most cases. You call for a tow, the tow truck driver charges you, and Progressive reimburses up to your limit. If your tow runs $200 and your limit is $75, you are writing a check for $125 yourself.

## Does Progressive Have Different Tiers of Roadside Coverage?

Yes. Progressive offers a base Roadside Assistance option and, in many states, a higher-limit version, sometimes labeled as "Signature" or a premium tier depending on when and where you purchased. The base plan covers typical incidents like towing, flat tire service, battery jump-start, lockout, fuel delivery, and winching. The premium tier raises the per-incident dollar cap and sometimes adds trip interruption reimbursement.

The problem is that many drivers add Roadside Assistance at checkout without reading the limit. A $75 cap sounds fine until your car breaks down 40 miles from the nearest shop. A 40-mile tow at standard rates can run $150 to $250 easily, leaving a real gap. If your vehicle is disabled far from a service center, that gap matters. [See how much a long tow actually costs](/how-much-to-tow-car-100-miles-interstate/) before you assume your policy has you covered.

## What Counts as One Incident Under Progressive?

One incident is one service call for one breakdown event. If your car is dead on the side of the road and you call for a tow, that is one incident. Progressive does not typically split the bill across multiple service categories for the same breakdown. The per-incident limit applies to the total cost of that one dispatch.

Progressive also tracks how many incidents you file per year. Filing excessive claims can affect your renewal, though a single tow claim rarely causes a non-renewal on its own.

## What Happens If My Tow Bill Is More Than My Progressive Limit?

You pay the overage directly to the tow company. The tow company does not negotiate with Progressive for you. They charge you, you pay, and then you submit a reimbursement claim to Progressive for whatever your policy covers.

Keep your receipt. Progressive requires documentation to process a claim: the tow company's invoice showing the service date, your name, and the dollar amount. With

## How Do I Find My Actual Limit Right Now?

Log into your Progressive account online or open the Progressive app. Go to your policy details and look for "Roadside Assistance" under your coverages. The per-incident limit is listed there. You can also call Progressive directly and ask a rep to confirm the dollar cap on your policy. Do this before you are standing on the shoulder at night, not after.

Your declarations page, the document Progressive sends at each renewal, also lists this. If you have a paper copy in your glove box, the Roadside Assistance line will show the coverage amount.

## Common Questions

**Q: Does Progressive roadside assistance cover the full tow cost?**
A: Only up to your policy's per-incident dollar limit, typically $75 to $100 on base plans. Anything above that limit comes out of your pocket.

**Q: Can I use Progressive roadside assistance multiple times per year?**
A: Yes, but there is usually a cap on the number of incidents per year (commonly three to four per vehicle). Check your declarations page for your specific policy's limit.

**Q: Does Progressive pay the tow truck directly or reimburse me?**
A: It depends on how the service is dispatched. If you use Progressive's app or phone line to dispatch a tow, they may pay the driver directly up to your limit. If you arrange your own tow and pay upfront, you submit receipts for reimbursement.

**Q: Will filing a towing claim raise my Progressive premium?**
A: One roadside assistance claim typically does not raise your rate the way an at-fault accident does. Multiple claims in a short window can attract attention at renewal, but a single tow claim is generally low risk.

**Q: Is Progressive's towing limit higher if my car is totaled in an accident?**
A: If the tow follows a covered accident, it may fall under your collision or comprehensive claim rather than your roadside add-on, and the reimbursement rules differ. Ask your claims adjuster specifically which coverage is paying the tow.
