> **Quick Answer:** State Farm roadside assistance covers towing up to 15 miles for basic coverage or unlimited distance with Emergency Road Service Plus. You get 4 service calls per year with basic, unlimited with Plus. Coverage costs $17-25 annually and reimburses you after paying the tow truck company.

## What State Farm Covers

State Farm offers two roadside assistance options with different towing limits:

**Basic Emergency Road Service:**
- Towing up to 15 miles from breakdown location
- 4 service calls per 12-month policy period
- Costs around $17 per year

**Emergency Road Service Plus:**
- Unlimited towing distance
- Unlimited service calls per year
- Costs around $25 per year

Both plans require you to pay the tow truck driver upfront, then submit receipts to State Farm for reimbursement.

## What To Do When You Need Towing

1. **Call State Farm first** at 1-800-SF-CLAIM (1-800-732-5246) to verify your coverage and get claim number
2. **Ask specifically about distance limits** for your plan before authorizing the tow
3. **Get written estimate** from tow truck driver if distance exceeds your coverage
4. **Keep all receipts** and documentation for reimbursement
5. **Submit claim online** through State Farm app or website within 30 days

If you have basic coverage and need towing beyond 15 miles, you'll pay the difference out of pocket. A [tow from highway to dealership](/towing-cost-from-interstate-to-dealership/) often exceeds this limit.

## Distance and Dollar Limits

State Farm doesn't cap dollar amounts for covered towing, but they do limit distance. With basic coverage, anything beyond 15 miles comes out of your wallet. The Plus plan removes distance restrictions entirely.

Your policy year resets annually, not per calendar year. If you use all 4 calls with basic coverage, additional services aren't covered until your policy renews.

Unlike some insurers, [State Farm's annual towing limits](/state-farm-covers-towing-how-much-per-year/) reset completely each policy period rather than rolling over unused services.

## What Happens When You Exceed Limits

When you exceed your plan's limits, State Farm stops paying. With basic coverage, this means:
- Tows beyond 15 miles: you pay the excess distance
- More than 4 calls per year: you pay the entire 5th call and beyond

The tow truck driver won't know your coverage details, so you'll pay the full amount upfront. State Farm only reimburses the covered portion when you file your claim.

## Cost Comparison

State Farm's roadside assistance costs significantly less than paying for emergency towing out of pocket. However, if you frequently drive long distances or need multiple service calls, [comparing with AAA coverage](/aaa-towing-vs-private-tow-truck-cost-comparison/) might save money.

For drivers who rarely need towing, basic coverage handles most situations. Those who commute long distances or drive older vehicles benefit more from the Plus plan's unlimited coverage.

## Stay Safe

- Verify your exact coverage level before your policy period ends
- Keep your policy number and State Farm's claim phone number in your phone
- Document everything at the breakdown scene with photos
- Never authorize towing beyond your coverage without getting cost estimates first
