> **Quick Answer:** Progressive covers towing only if you added Roadside Assistance to your policy. It's an optional add-on, not standard coverage. If you have it, you can call the number on your insurance card or use the Progressive app to request a tow. If you don't have it, towing is an out-of-pocket cost unless the tow follows a covered claim.

## Does Progressive Cover Towing Automatically?

No. Basic liability, collision, and comprehensive coverage don't include breakdown towing by default. Roadside Assistance is a separate add-on that costs a few dollars a month on most policies.

There is one exception. If your car is towed because of a covered accident (collision) or a covered comprehensive loss like theft, hail, or a fallen tree, the reasonable tow from the scene is typically part of that claim, subject to your deductible and policy terms. That is not the same as breakdown towing.

Quick test: a dead battery, flat tire, or failed alternator is a breakdown. A crash is a claim. Progressive treats them differently.

## How Do I Check If I Have Roadside Assistance Right Now?

Pull up your policy in the Progressive app or on progressive.com and look at the coverage list for "Roadside Assistance." If you can't log in, check your ID card or the declarations page in your email. Roadside Assistance is listed as its own line item.

Do this while you're safe. If you're on a highway shoulder, get your hazards on first and read the guidance in [Car Broke Down in the Left Lane of the Highway: What to Do Right Now](/car-broke-down-in-left-lane-of-highway-what-to-do/) before you start scrolling through apps.

Don't assume you have it because a rep mentioned it years ago. Coverage gets dropped at renewal more often than people expect, especially when someone shops for a cheaper rate.

## What Does Progressive Roadside Assistance Actually Pay For?

It covers the common breakdown calls: towing, jump-starts, flat tire changes using your spare, lockout service, and emergency fuel delivery. The details vary by policy and state, so confirm the specifics in your own coverage.

The part that surprises people is the towing limit. Coverage is capped by distance or dollar amount, and anything beyond that comes out of your pocket. The limits are broken down in [Progressive Roadside Assistance Towing Coverage Limit: What You Actually Get](/progressive-roadside-assistance-towing-coverage-limit/), and the general question of tow distance is covered in [How Far Will Insurance Tow My Car After a Breakdown?](/how-far-will-insurance-tow-my-car-after-a-breakdown/).

Practical tip: before the driver hooks up, ask where they plan to take the car and whether that destination is within your coverage. A shop 40 miles away may blow past your limit. A shop 8 miles away probably won't.

## What Do I Do If I Don't Have Roadside Assistance With Progressive?

Call a local tow company directly and get a price before you agree to anything. Without coverage, you pay the full bill. A typical local tow runs roughly $75 to $150 for the hook-up and first few miles, plus per-mile

## Should I Add Roadside Assistance to My Progressive Policy Now?

Yes, if you drive a car with any age or mileage on it. The add-on usually costs a small fraction of a single tow, so one use pays for years of premiums.

You can add it through the app or by calling your agent. Ask when it takes effect. Most carriers won't cover a breakdown that already happened, so you can't buy it from the shoulder and get reimbursed for the tow already underway. ## Common Questions

**Q: Does Progressive cover a tow if my car just won't start?**
A: Only if you have Roadside Assistance on the policy. Without it, a no-start tow is out of pocket, since it's a mechanical breakdown rather than a covered accident.

**Q: Will Progressive reimburse me if I pay for a tow myself?**
A: Often yes, if you have Roadside Assistance, but limits apply. Keep the itemized receipt and contact Progressive about their reimbursement process. Don't count on it without that coverage.

**Q: Does using Progressive roadside assistance raise my rates?**
A: Roadside service calls are generally not treated like at-fault claims. Rate effects can vary by state and insurer, so ask your agent if you want a definite answer for your policy.

**Q: Does Progressive tow after an accident?**
A: If the accident is covered under your collision or comprehensive coverage, the reasonable tow from the scene is generally part of the claim. Your deductible and policy terms still apply.

**Q: Can I use Progressive roadside assistance for someone else's car?**
A: It typically follows the covered vehicle on your policy, not the person. Check your policy terms for borrowed or rented vehicles before you count on it.
