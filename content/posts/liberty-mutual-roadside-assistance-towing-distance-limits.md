> **Quick Answer:** Liberty Mutual's standard roadside assistance covers towing up to 15 miles from your breakdown location. Enhanced coverage may extend this to 100 miles or more, depending on your specific policy. Check your policy documents or call Liberty Mutual directly to confirm your exact towing distance limit.

## What To Do

1. **Check your current policy documents** for your specific towing distance limit. Look for "Emergency Road Service" or "Roadside Assistance" sections. These are often buried in the endorsements or declarations page, not the main policy summary, so flip past the first few pages if you don't see it immediately.

2. **Call Liberty Mutual at 1-800-290-8711** if you can't locate your policy details. Have your policy number ready. The automated system can pull your roadside coverage details fast, or you can ask to speak with a rep who can read you the exact mileage limit on file.

3. **Know your coverage level**:
   - Basic coverage: typically 15 miles
   - Enhanced coverage: usually 100+ miles
   - Some policies offer unlimited towing distance

   The 15-mile basic limit sounds reasonable in a city, but in rural areas or on an interstate far from a major town, 15 miles may not get you anywhere useful. Know your limit before you need it.

4. **If you need towing beyond your limit**:
   - Pay the overage fee (usually $3-5 per additional mile)
   - Ask the tow truck driver for an exact quote before proceeding
   - Consider having your car towed to the nearest qualified repair shop within your limit

   If your preferred dealership or mechanic is 40 miles away but your policy covers 15, you're looking at roughly $75-125 in overage charges at $3-5 per mile. Sometimes it's smarter to take it to a closer shop for diagnosis first, then arrange a second tow if the car needs specialized work.

5. **Request towing to the nearest appropriate facility** if your preferred shop exceeds your distance limit. Liberty Mutual covers towing to the closest repair facility that can handle your specific vehicle type. If you drive a diesel truck or a hybrid, make sure dispatch knows that upfront so they send you somewhere equipped to handle it.

6. **Keep your policy number and Liberty Mutual's roadside number (1-800-290-8711)** saved in your phone for quick access during breakdowns. Screenshot your coverage details page and save it to your photos folder. Cell service is often spotty exactly where breakdowns happen, and having that info offline saves time.

## What It Might Cost

Standard Liberty Mutual roadside assistance typically costs $15-25 per year as an add-on to your auto insurance. If you exceed your towing distance limit, expect to pay $3-7 per additional mile beyond your covered distance.

Enhanced roadside packages with longer towing distances cost $30-50 annually but can save hundreds if you need long-distance towing. A single 80-mile tow without coverage can run $250-400 out of pocket depending on the towing company and region. Paying $25-35 extra per year for the enhanced package is a straightforward call if you do a lot of highway driving or live far from a major metro area.

If you're on the road regularly for work or take frequent long drives, also compare Liberty Mutual's roadside add-on against standalone services like AAA. AAA's Classic plan runs about $60-70 per year but includes 5 service calls and 7-mile towing per call, while their Plus plan covers up to 100 miles per tow. For someone who already carries Liberty Mutual auto insurance, the add-on is usually the more convenient and cost-effective route.

## Common Questions

**Q: Does Liberty Mutual roadside assistance cover towing if I'm far from home?**
A: Yes, coverage applies based on where your breakdown occurs, not where you live. Your towing distance limit stays the same whether you break down two miles from your house or two states away.

**Q: What happens if there's no repair shop within my towing distance limit?**
A: Liberty Mutual will tow you to the nearest facility capable of handling your vehicle type. If nothing qualified exists within your limit, they typically extend coverage to reach the closest appropriate shop, but confirm this with a rep at 1-800-290-8711 before assuming it applies to your specific policy.

**Q: Can I upgrade my towing distance limit mid-policy without waiting for renewal?**
A: In most cases yes. You can call Liberty Mutual and add or upgrade roadside assistance coverage at any time, with the change taking effect immediately or within a day. Do this before a trip if you know you'll be traveling in a remote area.

## Stay Safe

- Pull completely off the roadway before calling for help
- Turn on hazard lights and set up flares or reflective triangles if you have them
- Stay inside your vehicle if you're on a busy highway
- Never accept rides from strangers while waiting for your tow truck
- Keep your doors locked and windows cracked for ventilation while waiting
