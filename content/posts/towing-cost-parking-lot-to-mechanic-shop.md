> **Quick Answer:** Towing from a parking lot to a mechanic shop typically costs $75-150 for the first 5-10 miles, then $3-7 per additional mile. Total cost depends on distance, time of day, truck type needed, and your location. Private lots may add access fees.

Your car broke down in a parking lot and needs to get to your mechanic. Here's exactly what you'll pay and how to handle it.

## What To Do

1. **Get permission from property owner first.** Many private parking lots charge access fees ($25-75) or require specific towing companies. Ask the business or property manager before calling a tow truck.

2. **Measure the distance to your mechanic.** Use your phone's map app to get exact mileage. Most tow companies charge a base rate for the first 5-10 miles, then per-mile fees after that.

3. **Call your insurance company first.** Many policies include roadside assistance that covers towing. [Check if your coverage has distance limits](/state-farm-roadside-assistance-towing-limits-coverage/) before paying out of pocket.

4. **Get quotes from 2-3 local towing companies.** Ask for the total price including base rate, mileage, and any parking lot access fees. Confirm they can reach your car's location safely.

5. **Choose flatbed for AWD, luxury, or low cars.** Standard hook-and-chain costs less but can damage certain vehicles. Flatbed trucks add $25-50 to your bill but protect your car.

6. **Clear a path for the tow truck.** Move other cars if possible and meet the driver at the parking lot entrance to guide them to your vehicle.

## What It Might Cost

**Base towing rate:** $75-125 for first 5-10 miles
**Per-mile fee:** $3-7 for each additional mile
**Parking lot access:** $25-75 (private lots only)
**Flatbed upgrade:** $25-50 extra

**Total examples:**
- 3 miles to shop: $75-100
- 15 miles to shop: $105-175
- 25 miles to shop: $135-245

Night rates (6 PM to 6 AM) add 25-50% to these prices. Weekend and holiday surcharges are common. [After-hours towing costs significantly more](/cheapest-towing-service-near-me-after-hours/) than daytime service.

**Money-saving tip:** If your mechanic is more than 20 miles away, consider towing to a closer shop for diagnosis, then arranging transport to your preferred mechanic if major repairs are needed.

Private parking lots at malls, restaurants, or offices often have contracts with specific towing companies. Using their preferred provider can eliminate access fees but may cost more per mile. Hospital and airport parking lots typically charge the highest access fees.

## Stay Safe

- Stay with your car until the tow truck arrives if it's safe
- Keep your hazard lights on and use road flares if you have them
- Never stand between your car and moving traffic
- Lock your car and remove valuables before it's towed
- Get a receipt with the driver's name and truck number
- Take photos of your car's condition before loading

Your mechanic may offer pickup services for regular customers. Some shops charge flat rates ($50-100) regardless of distance within their service area. Ask about this option before calling a commercial tow truck.

[Towing from highways costs more](/towing-cost-from-highway-to-nearest-exit/) due to safety requirements and traffic complications. Parking lot tows are typically straightforward unless the lot has tight spaces or underground access.
