Your car is dead on the shoulder and you're staring at your GEICO app wondering if you're covered. Here's a straight answer.

> **Quick Answer:** Yes, GEICO covers towing after a breakdown, but only if you have Emergency Roadside Service (ERS) added to your policy. It is not included in standard liability, collision, or comprehensive coverage. ERS is an optional add-on, typically costs $14 per year per vehicle, and covers towing to the nearest qualified shop. If you don't have ERS, you're paying out of pocket.

## Does GEICO Automatically Cover Towing, or Do I Have to Add It?

You have to add it. GEICO's Emergency Roadside Service is a separate endorsement, not something that comes bundled with your base policy. Log into the GEICO mobile app or website right now and check your coverage page under "Policy Details." Look for "Emergency Roadside Service" or "ERS." If it's listed, you have it. If it's not, you don't, and this is not a good moment to find that out.

## What Does GEICO Roadside Assistance Actually Cover for a Breakdown?

GEICO ERS covers the basics you need most: towing to the nearest qualified repair facility, battery jump-starts, flat tire changes (using your spare), fuel delivery for an empty tank, and lockout service. If your car breaks down and needs a tow, they dispatch a local towing contractor. The tow is covered as long as you're using an in-network or GEICO-approved provider. One important detail: "nearest qualified shop" is the standard. If you want your car towed to a specific mechanic across town instead of the closest shop, you may be responsible for the extra mileage cost. Check your policy documents for your state-specific mileage limit.

If you're already locked out of your vehicle on top of the breakdown, the ERS lockout benefit covers that too. [Here's what GEICO roadside assistance covers for lockouts](/does-geico-roadside-assistance-cover-lockout/) in more detail.

## How Do I Actually Call GEICO for a Tow Right Now?

Open the GEICO mobile app and tap "Roadside Assistance" from the home screen. It will use your location to dispatch help. You can also call GEICO's roadside line directly through the app, which routes to their dispatch automatically. Do not search for a random number online. The app is faster, confirms your coverage in real time, and gives you an ETA for the truck.

Before you call, get as specific as possible about your location. Street intersections, highway mile markers, or nearby landmarks all help. If you broke down on a highway, move to the shoulder as far from traffic as possible, turn on your hazard lights, and stay in your vehicle if traffic is passing close. For more on keeping yourself safe while you wait, read [broken down on highway at night: safety tips and what to do](/broken-down-on-highway-at-night-safety-tips-what-to-do/).

## Will Using GEICO Roadside Assistance Raise My Insurance Rates?

No. GEICO ERS claims are not the same as at-fault accident claims. Using your roadside assistance benefit does not count against your driving record, does not trigger a surcharge, and does not affect your premium at renewal. That's consistent across standard roadside assistance endorsements industry-wide. Use the coverage you paid for without worrying about rate impact.

## Common Questions

**Q: How much does GEICO Emergency Roadside Service cost to add?**
A: Around $14 per year per vehicle, though it varies slightly by state. It's one of the cheapest add-ons GEICO offers and is worth adding before you need it.

**Q: Does GEICO tow to any shop I want or just the nearest one?**
A: The standard benefit covers towing to the nearest qualified repair facility. If you want a specific shop farther away, you may owe the difference in mileage. Check your specific policy for the mileage limit in your state.

**Q: Can I get reimbursed if I already paid for a tow and then called GEICO?**
A: Sometimes. GEICO does have a reimbursement process for out-of-pocket roadside expenses if you have ERS and couldn't reach them in time. Submit your receipt through the app or by calling GEICO directly. Reimbursement is not guaranteed and may be capped.

**Q: Does GEICO roadside cover me in a rental car or someone else's car?**
A: GEICO ERS is attached to your vehicle, not to you as a driver. It generally does not follow you into a rental or a borrowed car. If you need coverage in rentals, check if your credit card includes that benefit.

**Q: What if my car needs a tow after an accident, not a breakdown?**
A: If another driver was at fault, their liability coverage may pay for your tow. If you were at fault and you have collision coverage, that may apply.
