> **Quick Answer:** GEICO roadside assistance costs $14-25 per year and covers towing up to 15 miles. Direct tow truck calls cost $75-150 for the same distance. If you need towing more than once every 3-5 years, GEICO's plan saves money.

## What To Do

1. **Check if you already have GEICO roadside assistance** on your policy. Look at your declarations page or call your agent.

2. **Compare immediate costs:**
   - GEICO roadside: Free service calls if you have coverage
   - Direct tow truck: $75-150 for local towing, $3-7 per mile beyond

3. **For emergency situations without coverage:**
   - Call local tow companies for quotes
   - Ask about cash discounts (often 10-15% off)
   - Verify mileage charges upfront

4. **Add GEICO roadside assistance** if you don't have it and your car is older than 5 years or you drive frequently.

One thing most drivers miss: GEICO's roadside assistance is billed per vehicle on your policy, so if you have two cars, you're paying twice. Run the math against a household AAA membership before adding it to every car. Also, each service call you make through GEICO typically does not count against your driving record, but in some states repeated claims can flag your policy for review at renewal. Ask your agent specifically about this if you plan on using it often.

## GEICO Roadside Assistance Coverage

**Basic Plan ($14-19/year):**
- Towing up to 15 miles
- Jump starts, flat tire changes
- Lockout service
- Emergency fuel delivery

**Enhanced Plan ($25-30/year):**
- Towing up to 100 miles
- Same services as basic
- Trip interruption benefits

The 15-mile limit on the basic plan sounds fine until your nearest in-network shop is 18 miles away. Urban drivers usually have no problem, but if you live in a rural area or regularly drive remote stretches of highway, that 3-mile gap means paying out of pocket for the overage. The enhanced plan's 100-mile limit is genuinely useful on road trips where the nearest dealership for your make might be 60 or 70 miles from where you broke down.

Trip interruption benefits on the enhanced plan are worth reading the fine print on. GEICO typically reimburses hotel stays and meals up to $250 per incident when your breakdown happens more than 100 miles from home. You need to save receipts and file within a set window, usually 30 days.

## Direct Tow Truck Costs

**Typical pricing:**
- Base fee: $50-100
- Per mile: $3-7 after first few miles
- After hours: Add 25-50%
- Weekends/holidays: Add $20-40

**Example:** 10-mile tow costs $85-170 depending on company and time.

That after-hours surcharge is the one that catches people off guard. A breakdown at 11 PM on a Saturday is not the same bill as a 2 PM Tuesday call. Some companies also charge a separate hookup fee on top of the base rate, which can add another $30-50 before you've moved an inch. Always ask for a full itemized quote before you agree to anything, and get a name from the dispatcher so you can reference it if the driver shows up with a different number.

If your car needs a flatbed rather than a standard wheel-lift truck, expect to pay 20-30% more. Lowered vehicles, AWD cars, and anything with significant undercarriage damage typically require a flatbed. You usually don't get to choose which style shows up when you call GEICO either, so if you know your car needs a flatbed, mention it immediately when you call.

## When GEICO Makes Sense

- You drive an older vehicle (5+ years)
- You commute long distances daily
- You've needed roadside help before
- Peace of mind is worth $1-2 monthly

A good rule of thumb: one tow every four years on the basic plan costs you roughly $56-76 in premiums. A single direct tow at off-hours weekend rates can hit $150-200 on its own. The math favors coverage pretty quickly once your car is out of bumper-to-bumper warranty.

## When Direct Payment Works Better

- Newer reliable vehicle under warranty
- Rarely drive or short local trips only
- Good emergency fund available
- Live in area with competitive tow pricing

If your car is still under a manufacturer's roadside assistance program, which most new vehicles carry for 3-5 years, you are paying for duplicate coverage if you add GEICO on top. Check your owner's manual or the manufacturer's app before adding anything to your policy.

## Common Questions

**Q: Does using GEICO roadside assistance raise my insurance premium?**
A: Generally no, roadside assistance claims are not accident claims and do not directly affect your rate. However, multiple claims in a short period can draw attention during policy renewal in some states, so it is worth asking your agent how your specific policy handles it.

**Q: What happens if my tow needs to go farther than GEICO's covered mileage?**
A: GEICO pays for the first 15 miles on the basic plan or 100 miles on the enhanced plan, and you pay the per-mile rate beyond that directly to the tow company. The driver will typically collect payment from you before or after the tow, so have a card or cash ready for the overage.

**Q: Can I add GEICO roadside assistance after a breakdown has already happened?**
A: No. Like any insurance coverage, you cannot add it retroactively to cover an incident that has already occurred. You need to have it active on your policy before the breakdown happens for it to apply.
