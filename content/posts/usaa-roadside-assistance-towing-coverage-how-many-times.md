> **Quick Answer:** USAA's Emergency Road Service (ERS) coverage does not publish a hard annual cap on towing uses, but the benefit is tied to each disabling occurrence, not a set number of trips per year. Coverage limits vary by state and policy, typically reimbursing up to $50 to $100 per tow. Your best move right now: call USAA at the number on your insurance card and ask dispatch to confirm your per-incident limit before the truck rolls.

## How Many Times a Year Does USAA Cover Towing?

USAA does not advertise a fixed number of covered tows per year the way some standalone roadside plans (like AAA) do. The coverage applies per qualifying disabling event, meaning each time your car breaks down in a way that makes it undriveable, you can file a claim. There is no widely published cutoff of "three tows and you're done," but filing multiple claims in a short window can trigger a policy review. If you are using roadside assistance more than a couple of times a year, your insurer will notice, and it can factor into your renewal.

## What Does USAA Actually Pay Per Tow?

USAA's ERS coverage typically reimburses between $50 and $100 per towing incident, though the exact limit depends on your state and the specific policy tier you carry. That cap matters a lot in practice. A local tow of 5 to 10 miles in most cities runs $75 to $125. A longer tow, say 30 or more miles, can run $200 to $350 or more. If your car dies on a highway far from the nearest shop, you could easily owe the difference out of pocket. ## Does USAA Roadside Assistance Cover More Than Just Towing?

Yes. USAA's ERS add-on typically covers jump starts, fuel delivery, flat tire changes, lockout service, and winching if your car is stuck in a ditch or off the road. Each of those is a separate covered service per occurrence, not separate from your tow count. The key word is "disabling." If you call for a jump start and the car starts, that's one occurrence. If it dies again two days later, that's a new occurrence. Services like fuel delivery are usually capped at a small quantity, often two gallons, just enough to get you to a station.

## Will Filing Multiple Roadside Claims Affect My USAA Premium?

Frequent roadside claims can affect your renewal, though USAA does not publicly state an exact threshold. Insurance companies treat roadside assistance claims differently from collision claims, and they generally do not raise your rate after one or two ERS calls. But a pattern of repeated use, four or five claims in a policy year, signals to the underwriter that you are a higher-risk customer. ## What Should I Do Right Now If I Need a Tow and Have USAA?

Call the number on your USAA insurance card and ask for Emergency Road Service dispatch. Tell them your location, the nature of the problem, and ask them to confirm your per-incident reimbursement limit before a truck is dispatched. Save your receipt no matter what, because USAA's ERS often works as a reimbursement after the fact rather than direct pay to the tow company. If USAA is sending a contracted provider, ask the estimated arrival time before you commit. If you are broken down in a dangerous spot, like a live lane on the interstate, get away from the car and call 911 first, then call USAA. Being stranded on a highway at night adds real danger to an already stressful situation, so do not stay inside or stand beside the vehicle while you wait. ## Common Questions

**Q: Does USAA limit how many roadside assistance calls I can make per year?**
A: USAA does not publish a hard annual cap on ERS calls. Coverage applies per qualifying disabling incident, not a set number of uses per year. However, repeated claims can trigger a policy review at renewal time.

**Q: How do I get reimbursed for a tow through USAA?**
A: Save your receipt from the tow company, then file a claim through the USAA app, online, or by phone. Reimbursement is typically processed within a few days. Make sure the amount is within your per-incident limit, or you will cover the overage yourself.

**Q: What is the per-tow dollar limit on USAA roadside assistance?**
A: Most USAA ERS policies cap reimbursement at $50 to $100 per towing incident. Your exact limit depends on your state and policy. Call USAA before the truck rolls so you know what you owe if the bill exceeds the cap.

**Q: Can I use USAA roadside assistance if I did not add ERS to my policy?**
A: No. ERS is an optional add-on to your auto policy, not included by default. If you did not elect it when you bought the policy, you are not covered. Check your declarations page or call USAA to confirm whether you have it.

**Q: Does USAA roadside assistance work for a car that broke down away from home?**
A: Yes. ERS coverage applies wherever you are driving in the United States, not just near your home. If you break down on a road trip in an unfamiliar city, call the number on your insurance card and they will dispatch a local provider.
