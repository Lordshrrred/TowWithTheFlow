> **Quick Answer:** In Atlanta, expect to pay $75 to $125 for the hookup fee plus $3 to $7 per mile. A short in-city tow of 5 to 10 miles runs $90 to $195. ## What It Costs to Get Towed in Atlanta

Atlanta towing is priced like most major Southern metros: a flat hookup fee plus a per-mile rate. Here is what you will realistically pay:

| Situation | Estimated Cost |
|---|---|
| Short city tow (under 5 miles) | $75 to $125 |
| Mid-range tow (5 to 15 miles) | $120 to $200 |
| Freeway tow, I-285 or I-75/85 (15 to 25 miles) | $175 to $325 |
| Long haul to suburb or dealership (25+ miles) | $300 to $500+ |
| After-hours or weekend surcharge | Add $50 to $100 |

Flatbed trucks cost more than wheel-lift. If you have all-wheel drive, a low-clearance vehicle, or the car cannot roll, insist on a flatbed. Budget another $25 to $50 on top of standard rates. For a detailed breakdown by distance, see [Towing Cost in Atlanta Georgia Miles Based](/towing-cost-atlanta-georgia-miles-based/).

## Atlanta-Specific Factors That Drive the Price Up

**Traffic on the Connector.** I-75 and I-85 merge through downtown into one of the worst stretches of highway in the country. If you break down during morning or evening rush, the tow driver may charge a wait or delay fee, typically $25 to $50, just to reach you. You can see exactly how that plays out in [Towing Cost in Atlanta Georgia Rush Hour Traffic](/towing-cost-atlanta-georgia-rush-hour-traffic/).

**GDOT Incident Management.** Georgia DOT runs roving service patrols on major interstates. If one of these trucks reaches you first, the push or short move to the nearest exit is free. However, they will not haul you to a shop, so you will still need a private tow from there.

**Impound vs. private call.** If police initiated the tow after an accident or breakdown in a no-stop zone, the tow company working the scene sets the rate. You have no negotiating power and the price will be higher. Get to your car within 24 hours because daily storage fees in Atlanta run $40 to $65 per day.

**Night rates on the highway.** A breakdown after 9 PM on I-20 or GA-400 will almost certainly trigger an after-hours surcharge. [Towing Cost Atlanta Highway Breakdown Night Rates](/towing-cost-atlanta-highway-breakdown-night-rates/) covers exactly what those look like.

## What To Do

1. Get off the road if you can. Exit ramps, parking lots, and side streets all cost less to tow from than active highway shoulders.
2. Turn on hazards immediately. If you are on the Connector or I-285, staying visible is critical.
3. Call your insurance or roadside assistance first. Many policies cover one tow per incident. Even a $50 reimbursement cap helps.
4. Get a price quote before the truck hooks up. Ask for the hookup fee, the per-mile rate, and any after-hours fee. Reputable Atlanta companies will give you this over the phone.
5. Confirm the destination before they move. Changing the drop-off point after the tow starts often triggers an additional charge.
6. Take a photo of your odometer reading and the destination address. This protects you if the mileage on your invoice looks inflated.

## What It Might Cost: Insurance and Roadside Plans

- **AAA membership:** Covers 3 to 100 miles depending on tier. Atlanta members report response times of 45 to 90 minutes on busy days.
- **Progressive roadside:** Typical reimbursement is $50 to $100. Check your policy because Atlanta tows routinely exceed that. See [Progressive Insurance Towing Reimbursement Amount](/progressive-insurance-towing-reimbursement-amount/) for specifics.
- **State Farm roadside:** Usually covers up to a set dollar amount per tow, often $75 to $100. Anything beyond that comes out of pocket.
- **No coverage:** Budget $150 to $350 for a typical Atlanta breakdown scenario if you are paying cash.

## Stay Safe

- Stay in your car on highway shoulders in Atlanta. Distracted driving fatalities on I-85 and I-20 are not rare.
- If you must exit the vehicle, get fully off the pavement and stand behind a barrier wall if one exists.
- Do not argue with police about tow company selection on an active scene. Sort out the cost dispute later.
- Confirm the tow company name and truck number before they leave with your vehicle. You will need it to locate the car if they take it to an impound lot rather than your chosen shop.
