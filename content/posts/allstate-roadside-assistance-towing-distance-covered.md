> **Quick Answer:** Allstate roadside assistance typically covers towing up to 15 miles from your breakdown location to the nearest qualified repair facility. Some premium plans may offer 100+ mile coverage, but standard coverage is limited. Distance varies by your specific policy.

## What To Do

1. **Check your policy documents** - Look for your roadside assistance certificate or log into your Allstate account online to see your exact towing distance limit.

2. **Call Allstate roadside assistance** at 1-800-ALLSTATE (1-800-255-7828) when you need a tow. They'll verify your coverage limits.

3. **Ask about your destination options** - They'll tow to the nearest qualified repair shop within your covered distance, not necessarily where you want to go.

4. **Request distance calculation** - Ask the dispatcher how far your preferred shop is from your location before agreeing to the tow.

5. **Pay the difference if needed** - If you want to go beyond your covered distance, you'll pay the additional mileage cost directly to the tow truck driver.

6. **Consider upgrading coverage** - If 15 miles isn't enough, ask about premium roadside plans that offer 100+ mile coverage.

That 15-mile limit sounds like plenty until you break down in a rural stretch where the nearest qualified shop is 22 miles away, or you need a specialist dealership that happens to be across town. The limit is measured in a straight road distance from your exact breakdown point, not from your home address or where you called from. If you pull off the highway at an exit ramp, the clock starts there.

One detail most drivers miss: Allstate defines "nearest qualified repair facility" on their terms, not yours. That might mean a general shop that handles your make, not the dealership you trust. If you insist on a specific destination, you are on the hook for every mile past your covered distance. That adds up fast if you are 35 miles from your preferred shop and only covered for 15.

If you have a newer vehicle still under manufacturer warranty, it is worth checking whether the automaker provides its own roadside towing benefit before you use your Allstate coverage. Many do, and using the manufacturer program first preserves your Allstate benefit for another situation.

Also worth knowing: roadside assistance through Allstate is a separate add-on from your collision or comprehensive coverage. Filing a roadside claim typically does not affect your fault record or raise your premium, but confirm this with your agent since state rules vary.

## What It Might Cost

Standard Allstate roadside assistance covers the first 15 miles at no charge. Beyond that distance, expect to pay $3-7 per additional mile depending on your location and the towing company rates. A tow that runs 20 miles over your covered limit could cost you $15-35 out of pocket on top of what Allstate handles. In remote areas where tow companies charge premium rates, that per-mile figure can push toward $10.

Premium roadside plans with extended towing coverage typically add $20-40 annually to your insurance premium. If you drive frequently in rural areas or own a vehicle that tends toward expensive repairs at specialty shops far from home, that upgrade pays for itself after a single breakdown.

## Common Questions

**Q: Does the 15-mile towing limit reset if I need a second tow from the repair shop?**
A: Yes, if you need a second tow from a different breakdown location, the distance limit applies fresh to that new call. Each service request is measured from the point of that specific breakdown, not cumulatively.

**Q: Can I choose my own tow truck company instead of the one Allstate dispatches?**
A: Allstate typically dispatches from their network, but if you arrange your own tow, you may be able to submit for reimbursement up to your covered limit. Call the claims line first to confirm the process, since paying out of pocket and seeking reimbursement is slower and not always guaranteed.

**Q: What happens if I break down at night or on a holiday and the nearest shop is closed?**
A: Allstate can tow your vehicle to the shop even if it is closed, leaving it in their lot. Confirm with the dispatcher beforehand that the destination will accept after-hours drop-offs, since not every facility does.

## Stay Safe

- Don't assume unlimited towing distance
- Confirm coverage limits before the tow truck arrives
- Ask for the total estimated cost if going beyond covered distance
- Keep cash or card ready for overage charges
- Get written confirmation of final destination before the tow begins
