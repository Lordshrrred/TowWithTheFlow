> **Quick Answer:** AAA Basic costs $56-68/year but limits you to 4 calls and 3-7 mile tows. Private towing runs $75-200 per call depending on distance and time. AAA wins if you use it 2+ times yearly, but private gives unlimited distance and faster response times.

## What To Do

1. **Calculate your yearly towing needs.** If you call for help twice or more per year, AAA membership pays for itself. A single AAA Plus membership at $91-119 breaks even after just one medium-distance tow that would otherwise cost $125-200 out of pocket.

2. **Check AAA limitations first.** Basic membership covers only 3-5 miles (varies by region). Plus membership covers 10 miles, Premier covers 100 miles. If your tow destination is a dealership across town, Basic often won't get you there. Know your tier before you assume you're covered.

3. **Get private towing quotes immediately.** Call 2-3 local companies for current rates. Prices vary wildly by location and time of day. A rural tow in the midwest can run $50 less than the same job in a metro area. Getting competing quotes takes five minutes and can save you $40-60 on a single call.

4. **Factor in wait times.** AAA often takes 45-90 minutes. Private companies usually arrive in 20-45 minutes, especially if you're blocking traffic. If you're stopped on an interstate shoulder or in a dangerous spot, the faster private response is genuinely worth paying for.

5. **Consider your vehicle type.** AAA has weight limits. Heavy trucks, RVs, or modified vehicles need private towing anyway. AAA Basic typically caps coverage at standard passenger vehicles under 10,000 lbs GVWR. Lifted trucks, trailers, and anything requiring a flatbed due to all-wheel drive or low clearance often fall outside Basic and Plus coverage entirely.

6. **Check whether your auto insurance already covers towing.** Many comprehensive policies include roadside assistance as a rider for $5-15 per year added to your premium. If you're already paying for that, adding AAA on top is redundant for basic towing needs.

## What It Might Cost

**AAA Membership (Annual):**
- Basic: $56-68 (4 calls, 3-7 miles each)
- Plus: $91-119 (4 calls, 10 miles each)
- Premier: $119-144 (4 calls, 100 miles each)

**Private Towing (Per Call):**
- Local tow (under 10 miles): $75-125
- Medium distance (10-50 miles): $125-200
- Long distance: $2-4 per mile beyond base rate
- After-hours surcharge: $25-50 extra
- Highway/dangerous location: $50-75 extra

**Break-even point:** Two AAA calls per year equals private towing costs in most markets.

One scenario where private towing clearly wins: you break down 40 miles from home. A AAA Basic member owes the mileage overage beyond 5 miles at roughly $3-5 per mile, adding $105-175 on top of the membership they already paid. A AAA Premier member is covered, but paid $119-144 upfront for that peace of mind. Run the numbers for your actual situation, not the average.

## Common Questions

**Q: Does AAA towing cover the whole family or just the member?**
A: AAA membership covers the member, not the vehicle. That means if a family member is driving your car and breaks down, they are not covered unless they have their own membership. Household associate memberships are available for an added fee, typically $30-50 per person annually.

**Q: Can private tow companies really negotiate their rates?**
A: Yes, especially for non-emergency situations or if you are a repeat customer. Calling ahead to ask for a flat rate rather than a per-mile rate on longer tows can save $30-60. Rates are rarely negotiable on emergency calls or late-night service when demand is high.

**Q: What happens if AAA reaches its 4-call annual limit before the year ends?**
A: Any additional calls beyond your plan's limit are billed at standard out-of-pocket rates, which vary by region but typically run $75-150 for a local tow. If you hit your limit early in the year, upgrading your membership tier mid-year is an option, though you pay a prorated difference to unlock the higher call count.

## Stay Safe

- Never agree to towing without getting the total price upfront
- Ask about storage fees at the destination
- Private companies can negotiate rates, AAA rates are fixed
- Keep cash or card ready for private towing payment
- AAA membership includes other services (battery, lockout, fuel delivery)
- Some insurance policies include towing coverage, check before paying extra
