> **Quick Answer:** Liberty Mutual roadside assistance covers towing to the nearest qualified repair facility within a reasonable distance, typically 15-20 miles. If you need to go farther, you pay the difference above their covered distance at the standard per-mile rate.

## What To Do

1. **Call Liberty Mutual roadside assistance** at the number on your insurance card or app. Have your policy number ready.

2. **Tell them your exact location** using landmarks, mile markers, or GPS coordinates. Be specific about what's wrong with your car.

3. **Ask for the covered towing distance** for your specific policy. Coverage varies between basic roadside and enhanced packages.

4. **Request your preferred repair shop** if it's within the covered distance. They'll confirm if it qualifies as an appropriate facility.

5. **Get the tow truck driver's contact info** when they arrive. You'll need this if there are any billing issues later.

6. **Pay any overage directly** if your destination exceeds the covered distance. The driver will collect payment for extra miles.

## What Liberty Mutual Typically Covers

Liberty Mutual's standard roadside assistance covers towing to the nearest qualified repair facility, usually within 15-20 miles. Enhanced coverage may extend this to 25-50 miles depending on your policy add-ons.

The "nearest qualified facility" means a licensed auto repair shop, dealership, or service center that can address your specific problem. You can't use the benefit to tow to your home or a friend's garage.

If you have [Liberty Mutual's enhanced towing coverage](/liberty-mutual-towing-coverage-dollar-amount-limit/), you may have higher dollar limits that effectively extend your distance coverage. Each mile beyond the base coverage costs $3-7 depending on your location.

Your policy documents show the exact distance limits. Check your declarations page or log into your Liberty Mutual account online to confirm your specific coverage before you need it.

## When Distance Limits Apply

Distance is measured from your breakdown location to the repair facility, not from your home or starting point. If you're 30 miles from home but only 10 miles from a qualified shop, you're covered.

Liberty Mutual may approve longer distances in rural areas where repair facilities are scarce. They'll consider requests for specialized shops if your car needs specific expertise, like hybrid or luxury vehicle service.

The covered distance resets with each incident. Using your full distance allowance doesn't reduce coverage for future breakdowns, unlike some insurers that have [annual usage limits](/state-farm-roadside-assistance-towing-limits-coverage/).

## What It Costs

If your destination exceeds the covered distance, expect to pay $3-7 per additional mile. Urban areas typically cost more than rural zones due to higher operating costs.

Weekend and after-hours towing doesn't change the distance coverage, but the per-mile overage rate may increase. Holiday surcharges apply to any miles you pay for beyond the covered distance.
