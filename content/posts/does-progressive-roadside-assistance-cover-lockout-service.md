> **Quick Answer:** Yes. Progressive's roadside assistance includes lockout service, but only if you added the coverage to your policy. A locksmith or technician is dispatched to unlock your vehicle at no extra charge, up to a per-event dollar limit that varies by state and policy. If you don't have the add-on, you'll pay out of pocket, typically $50 to $150.

## Does Progressive Cover Lockout Service If I Didn't Add Roadside Assistance?

No. Lockout service is part of Progressive's optional roadside assistance add-on, not standard auto coverage. Liability, collision, and comprehensive don't pay for a locksmith, and neither does [liability-only insurance for towing or related services](/does-liability-only-insurance-cover-towing/).

Check your declarations page or open the Progressive app and look under your policy coverages. If "Roadside Assistance" is listed, you're covered. If it's not, you can still call Progressive, but you'll be paying the service provider directly.

One catch: the add-on cannot be bought after the fact. Adding it mid-lockout doesn't work, because new coverage typically has a waiting period before it applies to claims.

## How Do I Get a Progressive Lockout Dispatched Right Now?

Open the Progressive app and request roadside help, or call the number on your insurance card. The app is faster because it shares your GPS location with the dispatcher so you don't have to describe where you are.

Have these ready:

- Your policy number (the app has it, or check your digital ID card)
- Exact location, including cross streets or a parking lot name
- Vehicle make, model, and color
- A callback number that works if your phone is the thing you're about to lose

Ask for an estimated arrival time before you hang up. Lockout calls are usually quicker than tows, but [real roadside wait times](/roadside-assistance-how-long-do-you-have-to-wait/) swing a lot with weather, time of day, and local demand. If the ETA is long and you're somewhere unsafe, say so. Dispatchers can prioritize based on safety.

## What Does Progressive Roadside Assistance Actually Pay For on a Lockout?

It pays for a service provider to get you back into your vehicle, up to a set dollar amount per occurrence. The exact limit depends on your state and the coverage you selected, so confirm it in your policy documents instead of assuming.

What's typically covered: a technician using non-destructive entry tools like a long-reach tool or air wedge to open the door.

What's typically not covered:

- Cutting or programming a replacement key
- Repairing damage if a door lock or weatherstripping was already failing
- Lockout costs above your per-event limit
- Unlocking a vehicle that isn't the one listed on your policy

If your key fob is gone entirely rather than locked inside, a lockout service gets you in the door, but it won't start the car. Push-button start vehicles need a programmed fob or key. That's a locksmith or dealer job, and it's usually billed separately. For the bigger picture on what these plans include, see [Progressive's towing coverage limit](/progressive-roadside-assistance-towing-coverage-limit/).

## What Does a Lockout Cost If I Have to Pay Myself?

Expect $50 to $150 for a standard daytime lockout from an independent locksmith. Nights, weekends, holidays, and rural calls push it higher. Some shops add a separate trip

Get the total before the tech starts. Ask for a quote that includes the trip fee and any after-hours surcharge, and be wary of anyone who won't give a number over the phone or quotes an unusually low price, then inflates it on arrival. If you're comparing options, [AAA and other paid plans](/how-much-does-roadside-assistance-cost-without-insurance/) can also be worth pricing out, since a membership sometimes costs less than a single bad lockout bill.

Before you pay anyone, check two other sources:

- **Your credit card.** Some cards include lockout or roadside benefits. See [whether your credit card covers roadside assistance](/does-my-credit-card-cover-roadside-assistance-towing/).
- **Your car's manufacturer.** Many newer vehicles include complimentary roadside assistance, often with lockout, for the first few years.

## What Should I Do While I Wait for the Technician?

Stay somewhere safe and visible, and never break a window unless a child or pet is trapped and in danger. If that's the situation, call 911 immediately. Don't wait for a locksmith.

Otherwise, a few things that help:

- Check every door and the trunk. Rear doors and hatches are sometimes unlocked.
- If your phone is inside, borrow one or use a nearby business phone to call.
- Don't try a coat hanger or wedge. On modern cars, you can damage the wiring in the door or trip a side airbag sensor, turning a $75 problem into a repair bill.
- Wait inside a store or restaurant if the weather or location is bad, and give the technician a landmark to meet you at.

When the tech arrives, confirm the work is covered under your Progressive claim before they start, and keep the invoice if you paid anything.

## Common Questions

**Q: Does Progressive charge for using roadside assistance?**
A: If you have the add-on, the service itself is covered up to your limit, but the coverage carries a small premium on your policy. Using it generally isn't treated like an at-fault claim, but check with Progressive about how your state handles it.

**Q: How long does Progressive take to send a locksmith?**
A: Arrival times vary by location, time of day, and demand, and Progressive's dispatcher will give you an estimate when you call. Urban areas are usually faster than rural highways or remote parking lots.

**Q: Can I use Progressive roadside assistance for a lockout in someone else's car?**
A: Coverage generally follows the vehicle listed on your policy, not you personally. A friend's car usually isn't covered. Check your policy language, because some plans extend benefits when you're driving a vehicle you don't own.

**Q: What if I locked my keys in the car with the engine running?**
A: Tell the dispatcher it's running. It doesn't change what's covered, but it signals urgency, especially if it's hot or cold or the car is in a travel lane. If a child or animal is inside, call 911 first.

**Q: Will Progressive reimburse me if I already paid a locksmith?**
A: Possibly, if you have roadside assistance and the service fits the coverage. Call Progressive before paying if you can. If you've already paid, ask about reimbursement and keep your itemized receipt.
