> **Quick Answer:** Roadside assistance without a membership or insurance add-on costs $50 to $300+ out of pocket depending on the service. A jump start runs $50 to $100. A lockout is $75 to $150. A tow typically starts at $100 and climbs fast with distance. You can call a local tow company directly, use an app like Urgently or HONK, or pay a gas station for a referral. No membership required for any of it.

## What To Do

1. **Figure out what you need.** Jump start, lockout, flat tire, or tow. The service type determines who to call and what it costs. Don't call a full tow company if you just need a battery jump.

2. **Search for a local tow company directly.** Google "tow truck near me" and call the first few results. Ask for a price upfront before you commit. Most reputable operators will give you a flat rate or a hook-up fee plus per-mile quote on the spot.

3. **Use a pay-per-use app.** Urgently, HONK, and Blink Roadside connect you to a vetted local provider without a membership. You pay for the single service. Pricing is usually displayed before you confirm, which kills the guesswork.

4. **Check if your credit card covers it.** Visa Signature, some Amex cards, and several other cards include free or reimbursed roadside assistance as a cardholder perk. Check your card benefits before you pay anything out of pocket.

5. **Call your auto insurance company anyway.** Even if you don't have a roadside add-on, your insurer may offer a pay-per-use dispatch option or know of affiliated providers. If you were in an accident, towing may be covered under your collision coverage. See [how insurance handles towing after an accident](/insurance-covers-towing-after-accident-deductible/) before you assume you're on your own.

6. **Get a price in writing or via text.** When you call a tow company, ask them to confirm the total cost before they hook up. Predatory pricing at breakdown scenes happens. A confirmed number protects you.

7. **Do not accept the first price if it seems outrageous.** Call two or three companies. Prices vary by 30 to 50 percent in some markets, especially after hours.

## What It Might Cost

| Service | Typical Pay-Per-Use Cost |
|---|---|
| Jump start | $50 to $100 |
| Lockout service | $75 to $150 |
| Flat tire change (your spare) | $50 to $100 |
| Fuel delivery | $50 to $100 plus fuel cost |
| Local tow (under 10 miles) | $100 to $200 |
| Long-distance tow (50+ miles) | $300 to $700+ |

After-hours calls (nights, weekends, holidays) add $25 to $75 to most of those numbers. If you're on a freeway in a high-cost metro, expect the upper end. For reference, [the cheapest towing services after hours](/cheapest-towing-service-near-me-after-hours/) outlines how to find lower rates when timing works against you.

If you need a tow and want to understand what mileage-based pricing looks like before the truck shows up, reviewing local tow rates for your area helps. Markets like Los Angeles, for example, have published per-mile rates that differ significantly from mid-size cities.

For anyone who drives without any roadside coverage at all, it is worth knowing that adding roadside assistance through your auto insurer typically costs $5 to $15 per month. That math works fast if you break down twice a year. ## Stay Safe

- Pull as far off the road as possible before you stop to make calls.
- Turn on your hazard lights immediately, even in daylight.
- Stay inside your vehicle on highways. Getting out near traffic is more dangerous than waiting in a locked car.
- If it is dark or you are in an unfamiliar area, tell someone your location before you start making calls. [Breaking down in a bad neighborhood at night](/car-broke-down-bad-neighborhood-night-what-to-do/) has its own set of priorities.
- Never hand over your keys until you have a confirmed price and a company name.
- Take a photo of the tow truck's license plate and driver ID before the vehicle leaves your sight.

## Common Questions

**Q: Can I get roadside help right now without signing up for AAA or any membership?**
A: Yes. Apps like Urgently and HONK let you request a single-use service and pay only for that call, no membership or signup fee required. You can also call a local tow company directly and pay on the spot.

**Q: Will my credit card pay for a tow if I have no roadside coverage?**
A: Some credit cards, including Visa Signature and certain Amex cards, include roadside assistance as a built-in perk at no extra cost. Check your card's benefits portal or call the number on the back before you pay out of pocket.

**Q: How do I avoid getting overcharged by a tow truck driver at the scene?**
A: Ask for the total cost before the driver hooks up your vehicle, and request confirmation by text or in writing. If the price seems high, call one or two other companies, since rates in the same area can vary by 30 to 50 percent.
