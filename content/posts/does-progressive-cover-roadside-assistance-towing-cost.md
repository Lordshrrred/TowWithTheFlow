> **Quick Answer:** Progressive covers towing only if you added Roadside Assistance to your policy. It's an optional add-on, not automatic. If you have it, Progressive pays for a tow up to your policy's limit. If you don't, you pay the tow company directly. Check your Progressive app or policy declarations page before you call anyone.

## Does Progressive Cover Roadside Assistance Towing Cost If I Have It?

Yes. If Roadside Assistance is on your policy, Progressive covers your tow up to the limit listed in your coverage, and the limit varies by state and policy. Some policies cap the benefit at a dollar amount, others by distance. Read your declarations page for the exact figure rather than assuming it's unlimited.

The add-on usually covers more than towing: jump-starts, flat tire changes, fuel delivery, and lockouts. Details are in [Progressive's roadside assistance towing coverage limit](/progressive-roadside-assistance-towing-coverage-limit/) and in our breakdown of whether [Progressive covers lockout service](/does-progressive-roadside-assistance-cover-lockout-service/).

## How Do I Find Out Right Now If I Have It?

Open the Progressive app and look at your policy coverages. Roadside Assistance will be listed by name if you have it. No app? Check your digital ID card or declarations page, or call the number on your insurance card.

Don't guess. Plenty of drivers assume collision or comprehensive includes towing. It doesn't. Those pay for repairs after a covered loss, not a breakdown on a highway shoulder. Having full coverage and having roadside assistance are two different things.

If the app shows the coverage, use the roadside request feature. It sends your GPS location to the dispatch network, which is faster than describing where you are by phone.

## What Happens If I Already Called a Tow Truck Myself?

Paying a tow company first and asking for reimbursement can work, but it's the riskier route. Many roadside programs, Progressive's included, prefer to dispatch their own approved provider, and reimbursement of a self-arranged tow isn't guaranteed. Whether you get paid back depends on your policy terms.

If you have to pay on the spot because you're stranded in an unsafe spot at night, do it. Keep the itemized receipt with the company name, mileage, and total. Then call Progressive before you hand over a card to ask whether they'll reimburse it. Our guide on [reimbursement when you've already called a tow](/does-aaa-pay-back-towing-costs-if-i-already-called-a-tow-truck/) covers how this works with AAA, and the logic is similar.

## What Should I Do While I Wait for the Tow?

Get everyone out of the car and away from traffic if you're on a highway shoulder. Stand behind the guardrail, not beside the vehicle. Turn on your hazards. Leave the car in park with the parking brake set and the wheels turned away from the lane.

Take photos of the car's condition and your location before the truck arrives. If the driver will be using a flatbed or dollies, tell them upfront if you drive an all-wheel-drive vehicle, since towing it with two wheels down can damage the drivetrain. Ask for the invoice before you sign anything.

## Common Questions

**Q: Is roadside assistance included automatically with Progressive car insurance?**
A: No. It's an optional add-on that costs extra on your premium. Check your app or declarations page to see whether you added it.

**Q: How much does Progressive roadside assistance cost to add?**
A: It's usually a small per-policy charge, often a few dollars per month, though it varies by state and vehicle. Get the exact figure from your agent or the Progressive app.

**Q: Can I add roadside assistance after I'm already broken down?**
A: Don't count on it. Insurers often impose a waiting period before new coverage applies, so a same-day add may not cover the tow you need right now.

**Q: Does Progressive pay for a tow if my car won't start?**
A: Yes, if you have the roadside add-on. A no-start counts as a breakdown. Without the add-on, you pay out of pocket.

**Q: Will using Progressive roadside assistance raise my rates?**
A: Roadside claims are generally treated differently from at-fault accident claims, but policies vary. Ask your agent if you're worried.
