> **Quick Answer:** Comprehensive insurance does not automatically cover towing. It covers the damage to your car from non-collision events like floods, fire, theft, and falling objects. Towing reimbursement requires a separate roadside assistance or towing endorsement on your policy. Check your declarations page for "Roadside Assistance" or "Towing and Labor" as a listed coverage. If it's not there, it's not covered.

## Does Comprehensive Insurance Pay for a Tow?

No. Comprehensive covers the physical damage to your vehicle, not the cost of moving it. If a tree falls on your car, your comprehensive claim pays for the body damage. The tow from the scene to the shop? That comes out of your pocket unless you have a separate towing endorsement, a roadside assistance add-on, or a membership like AAA.

The confusion happens because towing often follows a comprehensive event. Your car floods, your car burns, your catalytic converter gets stolen and the engine won't turn over. These are all comprehensive claims. But the tow itself is a service call, not physical damage, so it falls under a different coverage category entirely.

If you have a [flooded car that won't start](/car-wont-start-after-rain-flooded-engine-what-to-do/), for example, the water damage claim goes through comprehensive. The tow bill does not.

## What Coverage Actually Pays for Towing?

Roadside assistance or towing and labor coverage pays for towing. Most major insurers sell it as a cheap add-on, typically $5 to $15 per vehicle per year. It's also sometimes bundled into higher policy tiers automatically, so read your declarations page carefully before assuming you don't have it.

Here's how the main options break down:

- **Roadside assistance endorsement**: Covers towing, jump starts, lockouts, and fuel delivery. Usually reimburses up to a set dollar amount or mileage cap per incident.
- **Towing and labor endorsement**: Similar but may be narrower. Covers the tow, but not always additional roadside services.
- **AAA membership**: Independent of your insurer. Classic membership covers towing up to 5 miles; Plus covers up to 100 miles; Premier covers up to 200 miles.
- **Credit card benefits**: Some cards include roadside assistance. Call the number on the back of your card to confirm before you need it.

For a detailed look at how one major insurer handles this, see [how much Progressive actually pays for towing](/progressive-insurance-covers-towing-how-much/).

## How Do I Find Out If My Policy Covers Towing Right Now?

Pull up your declarations page. It's the summary sheet at the front of your policy documents, usually one or two pages long. Look for any line that says "Roadside Assistance," "Towing and Labor," or "Emergency Road Service." If it's listed with a premium next to it, you have it. If it's not there, you don't.

## What Happens If I Tow Without Coverage and Try to File Later?

You'll likely pay out of pocket. A tow that follows a covered comprehensive loss, like a stolen catalytic converter or a fire, does sometimes get folded into the comprehensive claim settlement by some adjusters, but this is not guaranteed and is not standard practice. Don't count on it.

If your car is towed after a non-collision breakdown, like a failed fuel pump or a dead alternator, there is no comprehensive claim at all. That's a mechanical failure, not a covered peril. You're paying for that tow yourself. ## Common Questions

**Q: Does comprehensive insurance cover towing after a car fire?**
A: The fire damage to your car is covered under comprehensive. The tow from the scene is not, unless you have a roadside assistance or towing and labor endorsement on your policy. Some adjusters will include the tow in the total loss settlement, but that's at their discretion, not a guaranteed benefit.

**Q: Does AAA cover towing if I also have comprehensive insurance?**
A: Yes, AAA operates completely independently of your auto insurance. You can use your AAA membership for a tow regardless of what insurance coverage you have. Using AAA does not affect your insurance record or premiums.

**Q: If a flood totals my car, does my insurance pay for the tow to the salvage yard?**
A: If your car is declared a total loss under a comprehensive claim, the insurer typically arranges and pays for the tow to the salvage facility as part of the claims process. You generally won't see a separate tow bill in that scenario. The issue arises when the car isn't totaled and you need a tow to a repair shop first.

**Q: Is towing covered under collision insurance instead of comprehensive?**
A: No. Collision covers damage from hitting another vehicle or object. Like comprehensive, it pays for physical damage to your car, not towing services. Neither collision nor comprehensive is designed to cover the tow. You need a roadside or towing endorsement specifically.

**Q: How much does towing cost if I'm paying out of pocket?**
A: A local tow of 5 to 10 miles typically runs $75 to $125. Longer hauls go up from there, with many companies charging $3 to $5 per mile after the first few miles. A 20-mile flatbed tow in most cities lands between $125 and $200.
