> **Quick Answer:** USAA roadside assistance costs $15-25 per year and covers most towing up to 15-25 miles. Private tow trucks charge $75-150 for hookup plus $2-7 per mile. USAA wins for members who need towing more than once every few years.

## What To Do

1. **Check your USAA policy first.** Log into your account or call to see if you already have roadside coverage. Many comprehensive policies include it automatically. If you have full coverage auto insurance through USAA, there is a real chance roadside assistance is already bundled in at no extra cost. Confirm before you pay out of pocket for a private tow.

2. **Know USAA's limits.** Standard coverage includes 15 miles of towing, jump starts, lockouts, and flat tire changes. Enhanced coverage extends to 25 miles and adds fuel delivery. If your breakdown happens 30 miles from the nearest shop, USAA will tow you to the 25-mile mark and you pay the rest at standard per-mile rates. Know your distance before you call.

3. **Calculate private tow costs.** Local tow trucks typically charge $75-125 hookup fee plus $2-4 per mile in rural areas, $4-7 per mile in cities. A 10-mile tow runs $95-195. After midnight or on holidays, many companies add a 15-25% surcharge. Always ask about the full rate before the truck hooks up, because once it is on the hook you owe the bill.

4. **Factor in your driving habits.** Drive an older car? Take long trips? Park in sketchy areas? You'll likely use roadside assistance multiple times over several years. A vehicle with more than 100,000 miles on it statistically needs roadside help roughly once every two to three years. At that rate, USAA's annual premium pays for itself after a single call.

5. **Consider wait times.** USAA contracts with local providers but you might wait 45-90 minutes. Private companies you call directly often arrive faster, especially in busy areas. During peak hours, bad weather, or major holidays, the network providers USAA dispatches can be stretched thin. If you are in a dangerous spot on a highway shoulder, you may want to call a private company directly and pay out of pocket to get someone there in 20-30 minutes instead.

6. **Read the fine print.** USAA covers most passenger vehicles but excludes motorcycles, RVs over certain weights, and vehicles used for business. Private tow trucks handle anything. If you drive a lifted truck with oversized tires, confirm coverage, because some flatbed-only situations are excluded or flagged as specialty tows that cost more even through a plan.

## What It Might Cost

**USAA Annual Premium:**
- Basic roadside: $15-20 per year
- Enhanced coverage: $20-30 per year

**Private Tow Truck (typical 10-mile tow):**
- Small cities: $95-135
- Major metro areas: $125-195
- Rural areas: $75-115

**Break-even point:** If you need towing twice in five years, USAA saves money. One major breakdown and you're ahead.

Over five years, USAA basic coverage costs $75-100 total. A single 10-mile private tow in a metro area runs $125-195. The math is straightforward. The only scenario where private tow trucks consistently win is if your vehicle is excluded from USAA coverage or you always break down outside the towing radius.

## Common Questions

**Q: Does USAA roadside assistance count as a claim and raise my rates?**
A: No. Roadside assistance calls through USAA do not go through your auto insurance as a claim, so they will not affect your premium. They are handled as a separate service benefit.

**Q: What happens if my car needs to go to a shop farther than 25 miles away?**
A: USAA will cover the first 25 miles under enhanced coverage and you pay standard per-mile rates beyond that. In practice, calling the tow provider directly and negotiating a flat rate for the full distance sometimes costs less than the overage charge, so ask before they start driving.

**Q: Can I use USAA roadside assistance for someone else's car?**
A: Coverage follows the member, not the vehicle in most cases. If you are driving a friend's car and break down, USAA roadside typically will not dispatch for a vehicle not on your policy. Verify with your specific policy details, but do not assume it transfers.

## Stay Safe

- Keep your USAA membership card and policy number in your wallet
- Save USAA's 24/7 roadside number: 800-531-USAA
- Have backup cash for situations USAA won't cover
- Know that private tow trucks accept credit cards but USAA billing goes through your policy
- In dangerous locations, call police first, then your tow service
