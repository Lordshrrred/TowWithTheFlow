> **Quick Answer:** Towing from Seattle mountain passes costs $200-$500+ depending on your exact location, destination, and conditions. Snoqualmie Pass runs $250-$400 to Seattle. Stevens Pass averages $300-$500. Winter weather, night calls, and difficult access add $50-$200 surcharges. Mountain terrain requires specialized equipment.

## What To Do

1. **Get to safety first.** Pull completely off the roadway if possible. Mountain passes have narrow shoulders and fast-moving traffic.

2. **Call 911 if you're blocking traffic or in immediate danger.** Washington State Patrol coordinates mountain pass responses and knows which tow companies can reach you.

3. **Check your insurance coverage.** Most plans cover 5-15 miles of towing. [State Farm roadside assistance towing limits](/state-farm-roadside-assistance-towing-limit-miles/) typically provide enough coverage for shorter mountain tows.

4. **Call a tow company that serves mountain passes.** Not all Seattle tow trucks handle mountain terrain or carry chains. Ask specifically about mountain pass experience.

5. **Confirm the destination before they hook up.** Towing from Snoqualmie Pass to downtown Seattle is 50+ miles. To the nearest exit might be your cheapest option if [your car won't start but has power](/car-wont-start-but-has-power-dashboard-lights-on/).

6. **Get a written estimate.** Mountain towing involves multiple fees. Base rate, mileage, weather surcharges, and hookup fees all add up.

7. **Stay with your vehicle if it's safe.** Tow trucks on mountain passes sometimes take 45-90 minutes to reach you, especially during peak hours or bad weather.

## What It Might Cost

**Snoqualmie Pass to Seattle:** $250-$400
- Base fee: $100-$150
- Mileage (50 miles): $3-$5 per mile
- Mountain surcharge: $50-$100

**Stevens Pass to Seattle:** $300-$500
- Base fee: $100-$150  
- Mileage (65 miles): $3-$5 per mile
- Mountain/difficulty surcharge: $75-$150

**Additional surcharges:**
- Winter conditions: $50-$150
- After hours (nights/weekends): $25-$100
- Difficult access: $50-$200
- Chain installation required: $50-$100

**To nearest exit instead of Seattle:** Often 75% less expensive. Consider [towing to the nearest mechanic](/towing-cost-parking-lot-to-mechanic-shop/) if your car just needs minor repairs.

## Stay Safe

• **Turn on hazard lights immediately**
• **Exit on the uphill side if possible** to avoid downhill traffic
• **Wear bright clothing** if you have it
• **Stay inside your vehicle** unless you're in immediate danger
• **Keep engine running for heat** if the car starts and isn't overheating
• **Pack emergency supplies** for mountain driving: blankets, water, snacks, phone charger
• **Tell someone your location** and estimated arrival time before traveling mountain passes

Mountain passes freeze first and thaw last. [GEICO roadside assistance towing limits](/geico-roadside-assistance-towing-limits-how-many-miles/) and other insurance plans often cover enough mileage to get you off the mountain, but confirm your coverage limits before you need them.
