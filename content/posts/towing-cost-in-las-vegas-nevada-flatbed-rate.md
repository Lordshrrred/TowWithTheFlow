> **Quick Answer:** A flatbed tow in Las Vegas typically runs $95 to $175 for a local haul under 10 miles. The hookup fee starts around $75 to $95, then add $3 to $5 per mile after that. Nighttime calls, Strip congestion, and AWD or low-clearance vehicles push the price higher. Get a written quote before they hook up your car.

## What To Do

1. **Get off the road first.** If you're on I-15, US-95, or the Strip, steer onto the nearest side street, parking lot, or freeway shoulder. Las Vegas traffic is relentless and tow trucks need space to work safely. If you broke down on a freeway, read [what to do when your car breaks down on a freeway](/car-broke-down-on-freeway-what-to-do/) before you do anything else.

2. **Call a private company, not the first number that appears in a panic.** Nevada does not cap tow rates statewide. Las Vegas private companies are competitive, but predatory pricing exists, especially near tourist corridors. Search Google Maps for "flatbed tow Las Vegas" and call at least two companies. Ask for the hookup fee and the per-mile rate before agreeing.

3. **Ask specifically for a flatbed.** Wheel-lift trucks are cheaper, but flatbeds are required for AWD vehicles, cars with front-wheel damage, low-ground-clearance sports cars, and any vehicle that won't roll freely. If you request a flatbed and they send a wheel-lift, you are within your right to decline and wait for the correct truck.

4. **Confirm the destination and get a total estimate.** Give the driver the exact address of your mechanic or repair shop. Distance matters: a tow from Henderson to North Las Vegas can run 20 to 30 miles, which adds $60 to $150 on top of the hookup fee.

5. **Check your insurance or roadside membership before paying cash.** Many policies cover towing. GEICO, State Farm, Progressive, and AAA all have Nevada coverage. If you have a policy with roadside assistance, call that number first. It could cut your out-of-pocket cost to zero or a small copay. Check [whether your car insurance deductible applies to towing costs](/car-insurance-deductible-applies-to-towing-cost/) so you know what to expect.

6. **Document everything before the truck leaves.** Take photos of your car's position, any existing damage, and the tow receipt. Las Vegas has a high volume of vehicle claims and documentation protects you if a dispute comes up later.

## Stay Safe

- Stay inside your vehicle with your seatbelt on if you are on a freeway shoulder. Las Vegas freeway shoulders are narrow and traffic moves fast.
- Turn on hazard lights immediately and set road flares or reflective triangles if you have them, especially at night.
- Do not stand behind your vehicle while waiting. Move to the guardrail side if possible.
- If it is summer, it will be extremely hot. Keep water in your car. Vegas summer heat can exceed 110 degrees, and waiting 30 to 45 minutes outside without water is dangerous.
- If someone approaches your vehicle and offers unsolicited towing, decline politely and call a company you selected yourself. Predatory towing exists in high-tourist areas.
- Confirm the tow truck driver's company name matches the one you called before handing over your keys.

## Common Questions

**Q: Can I negotiate the tow price before they hook up my car in Las Vegas?**
A: Yes, and you should. Ask for the hookup fee and per-mile rate before they touch your vehicle, and get it in writing or confirmed by text. Once the car is on the truck, your negotiating position is essentially gone.

**Q: Will AAA cover a flatbed tow in Las Vegas or just a basic wheel-lift?**
A: AAA will dispatch the appropriate truck for your vehicle's needs, including a flatbed if required. Call the AAA dispatch line directly and describe your vehicle type and situation so they send the right equipment from the start.

**Q: How long does a tow truck typically take to arrive on the Las Vegas Strip or I-15?**
A: Expect 30 to 60 minutes during peak hours due to traffic congestion on the Strip and freeway interchanges. Calling during off-peak hours or from a side street location can shorten that wait noticeably.
