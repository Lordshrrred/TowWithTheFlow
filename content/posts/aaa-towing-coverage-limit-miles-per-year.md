> **Quick Answer:** AAA does not cap total towing miles per year. It caps the number of service calls (typically four per year on most plans) and the distance of each tow: 5 miles on Classic, 100 miles on Plus, and up to 200 miles on Premier, depending on your regional club. Exact numbers vary by club, so check your membership card or app before you call.

## Does AAA Limit Towing Miles Per Year or Just Per Tow?

AAA limits miles per tow, not miles per year. The yearly limit is on the number of service calls, and most clubs set that at four per membership year.

Here is how the distance caps usually break down. These are typical national figures, but each regional club sets its own, so treat them as a starting point:

- **Classic:** about 5 miles of towing per call
- **Plus:** about 100 miles per call
- **Premier:** about 200 miles per call

A call counts as one service event. A jump start, a lockout, and a tow are each a call. If your battery dies in January and you get jumped, that is one of your four.

## What Happens If I Use Up My Four Service Calls?

You can still call AAA after your fourth call, but you pay for the service out of pocket. The dispatcher will quote you a price based on the tow distance, and some clubs charge a per-mile rate above a base hook-up fee.

Don't assume the call limit is identical everywhere. Some clubs and some premium tiers allow more calls. Check the benefits page in your member account or the AAA app, since it shows your remaining calls for the year.

If you are close to the cap and the breakdown is minor, such as a dead battery, consider whether a cheaper fix exists. See [does roadside assistance cover a dead battery jumpstart](/does-roadside-assistance-cover-dead-battery-jumpstart/) for what counts as a service call.

## What If My Tow Is Longer Than My AAA Plan Covers?

You pay the difference, usually per mile past your limit. Say you break down 40 miles from your mechanic on a Classic plan. AAA covers the first 5 miles. You owe the remaining 35, billed at the tow company's mile rate.

The smart move is to ask the dispatcher before the truck is sent: "What will I owe beyond my covered miles?" Get the number up front. A flatbed bill with a surprise per-mile charge is the most common complaint people have after a tow.

You can also pick a closer shop. If the covered distance gets your car to a reputable repair facility, tow there and arrange the move to your preferred shop later. Towing twice can still cost less than one long overage. For a fuller breakdown of distance tiers, read [how far will AAA tow my car for free](/how-far-will-aaa-tow-my-car-for-free/).

## How Do I Check My Exact Limit Right Now?

Open the AAA app or log into your account and look at your membership level and the benefits summary. It lists your tow distance and remaining calls. Your physical card shows the plan tier, not the mileage.

If you have no signal or battery, the number on the back of your card reaches your regional club's dispatch. Tell them your membership level and ask for your tow limit before they send a truck.

Stuck on a shoulder while you sort this out? Stay in the vehicle with seat belts on if you are on a busy highway, or exit away from traffic and stand behind the guardrail if it is safe. Hazard lights on, and don't try to work out billing while standing near moving cars.

## Common Questions

**Q: Does AAA have a yearly cap on total towing miles?**
A: No. The cap is on the number of service calls per year (usually four) and on the miles covered per tow. Each tow gets its own distance limit, and the miles don't accumulate against a yearly total.

**Q: Do jump starts and lockouts count against my four calls?**
A: Yes. Any dispatched service counts as a call, whether it is a tow, jump start, lockout, tire change, or fuel delivery.

**Q: Can I upgrade my AAA plan after I break down?**
A: Some clubs apply a waiting period before upgraded benefits kick in, so upgrading mid-emergency often won't help. Ask your club about timing, and upgrade before you need it.

**Q: What does an overage tow cost per mile after my AAA limit?**
A: It depends on the tow company and region, and rates vary widely. Ask the dispatcher for a quote before the truck leaves. For general pricing context, see [how much does emergency roadside assistance cost without insurance](/how-much-does-emergency-roadside-assistance-cost-without-insurance/).

**Q: Does my AAA membership cover towing if someone else is in my car?**
A: Coverage generally follows the member, not just the vehicle, so the member needs to be present or the situation needs to fit your club's rules. Details are in [does AAA membership cover towing for someone else in my car](/does-aaa-membership-cover-towing-for-someone-else-in-my-car/).
