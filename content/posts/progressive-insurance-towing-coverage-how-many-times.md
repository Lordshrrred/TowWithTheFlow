> **Quick Answer:** Progressive's roadside assistance has no annual limit on towing incidents. You can use it every time you need help, but each tow is limited to 15 miles and costs $30-50 per incident depending on your coverage level. Multiple tows in one day for the same breakdown count as one incident.

## What Progressive Towing Coverage Includes

Progressive offers roadside assistance as an add-on to your auto policy. Here's what you get:

**Basic Roadside Coverage ($30/year):**
- Towing up to 15 miles
- Battery jump-start
- Flat tire change
- Lockout service
- Emergency fuel delivery

**Enhanced Roadside Coverage ($50/year):**
- Towing up to 100 miles
- All basic services
- Winching service

The enhanced tier is worth it if you drive rural routes or regularly travel more than 15 miles from the nearest repair shop. A single out-of-pocket tow beyond your coverage limit can easily run $3-5 per additional mile, so one 50-mile tow without enhanced coverage could cost you $100-175 extra on top of whatever repair you're already facing.

## How Many Times You Can Use It

1. **No annual usage limits** - Use towing as often as needed throughout your policy period
2. **Each breakdown is one incident** - Even if you need multiple attempts to tow the same car on the same day
3. **New breakdowns reset the count** - Each separate mechanical failure starts fresh
4. **Coverage renews annually** - Your usage resets when you renew your policy

In practical terms, this means a driver who has three separate breakdowns in a year, a dead battery in January, a blown tire in May, and a transmission failure in October, files three separate incidents and pays nothing beyond their annual premium for each one. There is no penalty or surcharge triggered by frequent roadside use the way an at-fault accident affects your liability rates.

## What It Might Cost

- Basic coverage: $30/year, covers towing up to 15 miles
- Enhanced coverage: $50/year, covers towing up to 100 miles
- Distance overage: You pay extra for miles beyond your coverage limit
- No deductible for covered services

To put those overage costs in perspective, most tow operators charge a hook-up fee of $50-75 plus a per-mile rate of $3-5. If you have basic coverage and your nearest dealership is 40 miles away, you are responsible for the 25 miles beyond your 15-mile limit, which could add $75-125 out of pocket on a single call.

## Important Limitations

Progressive roadside assistance won't cover:
- Commercial vehicles or motorcycles (separate coverage needed)
- Vehicles used for ride-sharing during active trips
- Intentional damage or racing incidents
- Towing from private property without owner permission

A few additional exclusions drivers miss: coverage does not apply if your vehicle is already at a repair facility when it breaks down again, and Progressive will not reimburse you if you hire your own tow truck without calling them first. Always call Progressive before arranging a tow, or you lose the benefit entirely.

## Common Questions

**Q: Will using roadside assistance multiple times raise my insurance premium?**
A: No. Progressive does not count roadside assistance claims as chargeable incidents, so frequent use will not increase your rate at renewal. It is treated separately from collision or liability claims.

**Q: What happens if the tow truck can't reach me within the covered mileage?**
A: Progressive will still dispatch a provider and cover the first 15 miles (or 100 miles on enhanced). Any distance beyond that limit is billed directly to you by the tow operator at their standard per-mile rate, usually $3-5 per mile.

**Q: Can I add roadside assistance to my policy after a breakdown has already happened?**
A: No. Like any insurance coverage, roadside assistance must be added before the incident occurs. You cannot retroactively add it to cover a tow you already needed.

## How to Request Towing

1. Call Progressive's 24/7 roadside number: 1-800-776-4737
2. Provide your policy number and location
3. Describe your vehicle's problem
4. Wait for the assigned tow truck (typically 30-60 minutes)
5. Show your driver's license to the tow operator
