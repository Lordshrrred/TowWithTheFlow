> **Quick Answer:** AAA does not cap your towing coverage by total miles per year. The limit is per service call, and it depends on your membership tier: Classic covers up to 5 miles per tow, Plus covers up to 100 miles, and Premier covers up to 200 miles. You can call for multiple tows in a year, but the miles-per-call ceiling is fixed by your tier.

## Does AAA Have a Miles-Per-Year Towing Limit?

AAA does not set an annual mileage ceiling on towing. The mileage limit applies to each individual tow, not to a running total across the year. What controls how far they'll haul your car is your membership tier, not how many times you've already used the service.

That said, AAA Classic, which is the entry-level plan most people buy, only covers 5 miles per tow. If your breakdown is on a rural highway 40 miles from the nearest shop, you pay the difference out of pocket. Miles beyond your covered limit are billed at the contracted rate for whatever AAA-dispatched provider shows up, and that rate varies by region.

## How Many Miles Does Each AAA Tier Actually Cover Per Tow?

Here is the breakdown by plan:

- **Classic:** 5 miles per tow
- **Plus:** 100 miles per tow
- **Premier:** 200 miles per tow

Five miles sounds almost useless on a highway, and it often is. It will get you to the nearest exit, maybe. It will not get you to your preferred mechanic across town. AAA Plus is the tier where the coverage starts making practical sense for highway breakdowns. Premier's 200-mile limit covers most cross-state tows you would realistically need.

If you are comparing the cost of upgrading to what you would pay for a long tow out of pocket, the math is usually straightforward. See [how much AAA membership costs compared to paying for a tow](/how-much-does-aaa-membership-cost-compared-to-paying-for-tow/) for a direct cost comparison.

## What Happens If My Tow Exceeds the Miles My Tier Covers?

You pay the overage. AAA dispatches a provider from their network, that provider tows your car, and at the destination they charge you for every mile beyond your covered limit at their posted rate. You do not get to negotiate that rate on the side of the road.

The overage charge runs roughly $3 to $7 per mile in most metro areas, more in rural zones where flatbed availability is thin. On a 60-mile tow with a Classic membership, you are paying for 55 miles out of pocket. That can easily top $200 to $300 before the provider adds any hook-up or drop fees.

If you know you are going to need a long tow, ask the dispatcher upfront what the overage rate is before the truck rolls. You have the right to know that number.

## How Many Times Can You Use AAA Towing in a Year?

AAA limits you to four service calls per membership year, not per vehicle. That four-call limit covers all roadside services combined: tows, jump starts, lockouts, fuel delivery, and tire changes. A tow counts as one call regardless of how many miles it covers.

## What If My Destination Is a Specific Shop and It Is Farther Than My Miles Allow?

You have two options: pay the overage, or have the car towed to the nearest AAA-approved facility within your covered miles and then arrange a second tow later.

The second tow from the shop to your preferred mechanic would count as a separate service call against your four-call annual limit, so that approach costs you a call. If you are near your call limit, paying the overage on a single long tow may be the smarter move.

One practical note: AAA will tow your car to any legal destination you choose, not just AAA-affiliated shops. You direct the tow, you just pay for the miles over your limit.

If your situation involves an insurance claim, your auto policy's roadside coverage may pick up what AAA does not. ## Common Questions

**Q: Does AAA Classic really only cover 5 miles of towing?**
A: Yes. AAA Classic's per-call towing limit is 5 miles. That is the nationwide standard for the base tier. Anything beyond 5 miles is billed to you at the provider's rate.

**Q: Can I upgrade my AAA membership after I break down to get more miles covered?**
A: No. You cannot upgrade mid-incident to apply a higher tier's benefits to an active service call. Upgrades take effect for future calls after the change processes, which typically takes 24 hours.

**Q: Does AAA towing coverage reset every year?**
A: Your four service calls reset when your annual membership renews. The per-call mileage limit is a fixed feature of your tier and does not change based on usage history.

**Q: If my car is totaled in an accident, will AAA tow it under my roadside coverage?**
A: AAA's roadside assistance is designed for mechanical breakdowns. After a crash, coverage depends on whether your AAA membership includes accident-related towing, and many plans do cover it. Your auto insurer's collision or comprehensive coverage may also apply. Check [whether AAA pays for towing if your car is not driveable after a crash](/does-aaa-pay-for-towing-if-car-is-not-driveable-after-crash/) for specifics.

**Q: What is the cheapest way to cover long tows if I only have AAA Classic?**
A: Upgrade to AAA Plus. The annual cost difference between Classic and Plus is typically $30 to $50 depending on your region, and one long tow at overage rates will far exceed that amount. Alternatively, check whether your auto insurance policy includes roadside assistance with a higher per-tow mileage limit.
