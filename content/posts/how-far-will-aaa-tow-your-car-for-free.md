> **Quick Answer:** AAA Classic members get up to 5 miles of free towing per call. AAA Plus bumps that to 100 miles. AAA Premier covers up to 200 miles per call. You get four service calls per membership year regardless of tier. Go beyond your mileage limit and AAA charges you per mile for the overage, billed directly to you on the spot.

## How Many Free Tow Miles Does AAA Actually Give You?

Your free mileage depends entirely on which AAA tier you pay for. Classic: 5 miles. Plus: 100 miles. Premier: 200 miles. Those miles are measured from your breakdown location to the destination you choose, not door to door from AAA's lot.

Five miles sounds like a lot until your car dies on the interstate and the nearest capable transmission shop is 12 miles away. Classic members pay out of pocket for every mile past mile five. The per-mile rate varies by region but typically runs $3 to $7 per mile over the limit. On a 25-mile tow with Classic coverage, that overage adds up fast.

## Does AAA Count Each Tow Against a Yearly Limit?

Yes. Every membership tier gives you four service calls per membership year. A tow counts as one call. A jump start counts as one call. Lockout service counts as one call. If you use all four before your renewal date, additional calls are billed at full price without the membership discount.

The mileage allowance resets per call, not per year. So a Plus member gets 100 free miles on each of their four covered calls, not 100 miles split across the year.

## What Happens When the Tow Goes Beyond My Free Miles?

AAA dispatches the truck and handles the tow. When you arrive at the destination, the driver or AAA bills you for the overage mileage before they unhook your car. You cannot defer that charge. Have a card ready.

If cost is a concern, ask the dispatcher upfront how far the destination is from your location. You can also choose a closer shop within your free mileage and arrange a second tow later, though that second call burns another service call from your annual four. For long-distance breakdowns, a Plus or Premier membership pays for itself on a single tow. See how AAA membership cost compares to just paying for a tow out of pocket at [how much AAA membership costs compared to paying for a tow](/how-much-does-aaa-membership-cost-compared-to-paying-for-tow/).

## Does AAA Cover Towing After an Accident, or Only Breakdowns?

AAA covers towing after accidents on most calls, but the details matter. If your car is drivable after a fender bender, AAA may decline to dispatch. If the car is disabled, they will usually tow it. The destination choice can also get complicated when police or insurance are involved, since sometimes the tow goes to an impound lot first rather than your preferred shop.

## How Long Will It Take AAA to Actually Reach Me?

Wait times vary by region, time of day, and whether there is a major weather event or highway incident pulling trucks away. In dense metro areas during normal conditions, expect 30 to 60 minutes. In rural areas or during bad weather, two hours or more is realistic.

If you are waiting on a highway shoulder, stay out of the car if it is safe to do so, stand behind a guardrail, and keep your hazard lights on. ## Common Questions

**Q: Can I choose where AAA tows my car, or do they pick the shop?**
A: You choose the destination, up to your free mileage limit. AAA will tow to any licensed repair facility, a dealer, your home, or even a parking lot. If the destination is beyond your free miles, you pay the overage for the extra distance.

**Q: Does my AAA mileage reset if I call twice in one day?**
A: Each service call gets its own mileage allowance. Two calls in one day each count toward your annual four-call limit, but each call carries its own full mileage allowance based on your tier.

**Q: Can a AAA member use their coverage for someone else's car?**
A: AAA membership is tied to the member, not the vehicle. As long as the member is present at the breakdown, AAA will generally cover the tow regardless of whose car it is. The member does not have to be the driver or owner.

**Q: What if I need a tow longer than 200 miles, even with Premier?**
A: You pay per-mile overage for anything beyond 200 miles. For very long hauls, compare AAA's overage rate against a private flatbed quote. Sometimes a private carrier on a long interstate run is cheaper than the per-mile overage on a 400-mile tow.

**Q: Is AAA Plus worth the upgrade just for the extra tow miles?**
A: If you regularly drive more than 30 miles from home or travel on interstates, yes. Classic's 5-mile limit is almost useless on a highway breakdown where the nearest competent shop could easily be 15 to 30 miles away. The annual cost difference between Classic and Plus is usually less than one overage bill.
