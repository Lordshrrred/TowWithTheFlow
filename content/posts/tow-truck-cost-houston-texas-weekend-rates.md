## What You'll Actually Pay: Houston Weekend Tow Rates

Houston is a massive, spread-out city. A "short" tow here can easily mean 15 to 20 miles once you account for traffic routing on I-10, I-45, or the Beltway. That distance adds up fast.

Here's a realistic price breakdown for weekend service in the Houston metro:

- **Hook-up fee:** $75 to $125 (this is just to dispatch and attach your vehicle)
- **Per-mile rate:** $3.50 to $5.00 per mile after the first 5
- **Weekend/after-hours surcharge:** $25 to $75 added to the base
- **Flatbed upgrade:** Add $25 to $50 if you have a low-clearance vehicle or AWD

A 10-mile tow on a Saturday night could cost $175 to $225. A longer haul, say from Katy to a shop near Midtown, can push $275 to $350 easily.

Rates in Houston do not have a city-mandated cap for private tows. Non-consent tows (called from accident scenes or by police) are regulated by Harris County, but if you call the truck yourself, pricing is set by the company. Always ask for the total before they hook up.

## What To Do Right Now

1. **Get off the road safely.** Pull onto the shoulder or into a parking lot. Turn on hazard lights. If you're on I-610 or a downtown freeway, call TxDOT's free roadside assistance line: 1-800-525-5555. They may move you off the highway for free.
2. **Call your insurance first.** Roadside assistance through your policy is almost always cheaper than paying out of pocket. Check your app or card before dialing a random tow company. If you have [Progressive roadside assistance](/progressive-roadside-assistance-covers-how-much-towing/), they handle dispatch directly and the call is free.
3. **Get a quote before authorizing.** Say: "What's your hook-up fee, your per-mile rate, and do you charge a weekend surcharge?" Any reputable company answers that in 30 seconds. If they dodge the question, call someone else.
4. **Know your destination.** Have the shop address ready. Tow companies charge to the address you give them. If you don't have a mechanic, ask the driver for a recommendation, but you are not obligated to use one.
5. **Document everything.** Take a photo of your car before they hook it up and get a written receipt that itemizes every charge.

## Does Insurance Cover the Weekend Surcharge?

Sometimes, sometimes not. Most roadside assistance policies cover a flat dollar amount per tow, not a percentage of the bill. If your policy pays $100 and the bill is $195, you pay the difference. The weekend surcharge usually counts toward your total, so it eats into that coverage.

If you have a standalone roadside plan through AAA, your insurer, or a credit card, call them first. Some plans dispatch directly and you pay nothing upfront. Reimbursement-only plans mean you pay the driver and file later, which takes time you may not want to deal with on a Sunday night. [Check whether your insurance deductible applies to towing costs](/car-insurance-deductible-applies-to-towing-cost/) before you assume the whole bill is covered.

## Stay Safe

- Stay in your car if you break down on a Houston freeway at night. The shoulder on I-45 and I-69 is dangerous.
- If you must stand outside, stay behind the guardrail, not between your car and traffic.
- Do not accept a tow from a driver who shows up unsolicited. Predatory towing near accident scenes happens in Houston. Confirm you called them or your roadside service dispatched them.
- Keep your windows up and doors locked while waiting for the truck, especially in unfamiliar areas.
