> **Quick Answer:** Yes, AAA covers towing for a flat tire under all membership levels: Classic, Plus, and Premier. But towing is usually the last resort. AAA will first send a technician to change your flat using your spare. If you have no spare, or the spare is also flat, they'll tow you. How far they'll tow depends on your membership tier.

## Does AAA Actually Come Out for a Flat Tire, or Do They Just Tow?

AAA dispatches a service technician first, not a tow truck, when you call for a flat tire. The tech will mount your spare and get you rolling. Towing only kicks in if you have no usable spare, your rim is destroyed, or the tire damage makes driving unsafe even on a spare.

This matters because the type of service affects your annual usage count. AAA tracks service calls, not just tows. A tire change counts as one of your covered incidents just like a tow would.

## What Membership Level Do I Need for AAA to Tow My Flat Tire?

All three AAA tiers cover flat tire towing, but the tow distance varies significantly. Classic members get up to 5 miles of free towing per call. Plus members get up to 100 miles. Premier members get up to 200 miles on one call per year, then 100 miles on subsequent calls.

If you're stranded with a flat and no spare on a highway far from a tire shop, that distance gap is the difference between a free ride to your mechanic and a bill for whatever mileage goes beyond your limit. A basic Classic membership can leave you paying out of pocket if the nearest shop is 20 miles away. Check [how far AAA will tow your car for free](/how-far-will-aaa-tow-your-car-for-free/) for the exact mileage breakdown by tier before you need it.

## What Happens If My Spare Tire Is Also Flat?

AAA will tow you. If the technician shows up and finds both the flat and the spare are unusable, they switch to tow mode and haul your car to the nearest appropriate shop or to a destination within your mileage allowance.

This is one of the more common flat-tire scenarios people don't plan for. Run-flat tires, compact spare (donut) tires with slow leaks, and foam sealant kits that failed all leave you without a working spare. See [flat tire spare is also flat: what a tow will cost you](/flat-tire-spare-is-also-flat-tow-cost/) for what to expect on the bill if your mileage runs out.

## Common Questions

**Q: Does a flat tire call count against my AAA service limit for the year?**
A: Yes. Every dispatched service call, whether it's a tire change or a tow, counts toward your annual limit. AAA Classic members get 4 calls per year. Plus and Premier members also get 4 calls, but the services themselves are more generous in terms of distance and scope.

**Q: Will AAA tow my car to a specific tire shop I choose, or just the nearest one?**
A: You can request a specific destination as long as it falls within your towing mileage allowance. If your preferred shop is 60 miles away and you have a Classic membership with a 5-mile limit, you'll pay the overage for the remaining 55 miles.

**Q: Does AAA cover towing for a flat tire on a leased or financed car?**
A: Yes. AAA membership covers you as the driver, not the vehicle. It applies to any car you're driving, including leased, financed, borrowed, or rental vehicles, as long as you're in the car when you call.

**Q: How long does AAA take to respond to a flat tire call?**
A: Average response times run 45 minutes to an hour in most areas, but rural locations or high-demand periods like winter storms can push that to two hours or more. See [how long can you wait for a AAA tow truck](/how-long-can-you-wait-for-aaa-tow-truck/) for realistic estimates by situation.

**Q: Is AAA worth it just for flat tire coverage, or should I use my insurance's roadside assistance instead?**
A: It depends on how often you drive and what your insurer actually pays out. Insurance roadside assistance is often capped at very low dollar amounts per incident, sometimes $50 or less, which may not cover a long tow. AAA's mileage-based coverage is more predictable.
