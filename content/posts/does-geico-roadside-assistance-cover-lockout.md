Locked out of your car and wondering if your GEICO policy will cover a locksmith. Here is what you need to know right now.

> **Quick Answer:** Yes, GEICO roadside assistance covers lockouts, but only if you have the Emergency Road Service add-on on your policy. It is not included in a standard liability or collision policy. Coverage typically pays for a locksmith to come to you, and GEICO limits the reimbursement amount per service call. Check your policy declarations page or call the GEICO roadside number before you hire anyone out of pocket.

## Does GEICO Roadside Assistance Actually Cover Lockouts, or Just Towing?

GEICO's Emergency Road Service covers lockouts directly. That means if your keys are locked inside the vehicle, GEICO will dispatch or reimburse a locksmith to open the car. The service is not towing-only. It also covers jump starts, flat tire changes, fuel delivery, and winching if you're stuck. The lockout benefit is part of the same bundled add-on, not a separate purchase.

The catch: you must have added Emergency Road Service to your policy before the lockout happened. If you only carry liability, comprehensive, or collision, you do not have roadside coverage. Pull up the GEICO app or check your policy declarations page right now to confirm.

## How Do I Actually Use GEICO Roadside Assistance for a Lockout?

Call GEICO's roadside assistance line or request service through the GEICO mobile app. The app is the faster route for most people. Open it, tap "Roadside Assistance," select lockout, and drop your pin. GEICO coordinates with a local service provider, and you will get an estimated arrival time. In most metro areas, response runs 30 to 60 minutes. Rural locations can take longer.

If you call a locksmith yourself first and then try to get reimbursed, GEICO may or may not cover it depending on your specific policy terms. Some plans reimburse up to a set dollar limit per service call (commonly $50 to $100, though limits vary by state and policy). Going through GEICO's dispatch avoids that reimbursement ceiling problem entirely.

## What If GEICO's Locksmith Takes Too Long and I Need Help Now?

You have options. If you are in a safe location, waiting is usually the right call. If you are in an unsafe situation, such as a dark parking structure, an unfamiliar area at night, or you have a child or pet in the vehicle, call 911 immediately. Police can request emergency assistance faster than a standard locksmith dispatch.

If the wait time is unacceptable and you hire your own locksmith, keep the receipt and document everything. Submit it to GEICO for reimbursement right after. Expect the reimbursement to be capped at your policy's per-incident limit. If cost is a concern, check out [how much roadside assistance costs without a membership](/how-much-does-roadside-assistance-cost-without-membership/) before you call a random locksmith off Google, because prices vary wildly and some out-of-pocket locksmith calls run $100 to $200 or more.

## How Many Times a Year Can I Use GEICO Roadside Assistance for Lockouts?

GEICO's Emergency Road Service typically limits you to a set number of service calls per policy period, often three to four per year across all roadside services combined, not per service type. Using it for a lockout counts against that total, the same as a tow or a jump start would.

If you use it frequently, GEICO can non-renew the add-on or flag your account. That said, one lockout a year is well within normal use. If you are locking your keys in the car regularly, a magnetic key box stored on the vehicle frame is a simple fix that costs under $10.

## Common Questions

**Q: How do I know if I have GEICO roadside assistance on my policy?**
A: Log into the GEICO app or website and check your policy details. Look for "Emergency Road Service" listed as a coverage. If it is not there, you do not have it and cannot use it for a lockout.

**Q: Can I add GEICO roadside assistance right now if I am locked out?**
A: No. You cannot add coverage during an active emergency and use it immediately. Insurance companies do not allow same-day use of newly added benefits. You would need to pay out of pocket for this lockout and add the coverage afterward.

**Q: Does GEICO cover a second set of keys made after a lockout?**
A: No. Emergency Road Service pays for access to the vehicle, not for duplicate keys. If you need a new key cut or a transponder key programmed, that is out of pocket unless the keys were stolen and you have a comprehensive claim.

**Q: Will using GEICO roadside assistance for a lockout raise my insurance rates?**
A: Generally no. Roadside assistance claims are not reported as at-fault accidents and do not directly affect your premium the way a collision claim does. However, frequent use of the add-on can cause GEICO to drop the roadside benefit at renewal.

**Q: What if GEICO's locksmith can't open my car model without damaging it?**
A: Modern vehicles, especially those with push-button ignitions and no physical key slot, sometimes require dealer-level equipment to unlock remotely. If the locksmith cannot open it, GEICO will typically arrange a tow to a dealership or authorized shop as the next step. Confirm this option with GEICO dispatch before assuming you are stuck.
