> **Quick Answer:** Progressive roadside assistance covers towing up to 15 miles from your breakdown location to the nearest qualified repair facility. Beyond 15 miles, you pay the additional mileage cost directly to the tow truck driver.

## What To Do

1. **Call Progressive roadside assistance** at 1-800-776-4737 or use their mobile app
2. **Confirm your location** with GPS coordinates or nearest cross streets
3. **Ask the dispatcher** to verify your towing coverage and find the nearest shop within 15 miles
4. **If no shop exists within 15 miles**, ask for the closest qualified facility and confirm additional costs
5. **Get written estimate** from tow driver for any mileage beyond the 15-mile limit before authorizing service
6. **Keep all receipts** for potential reimbursement claims

A few things worth knowing before the truck arrives. The 15-mile radius is measured from your exact breakdown point, not from where you called. If you walked half a mile to get a signal, the clock still starts at the car. Also, Progressive dispatches to the nearest qualified repair facility, which means a shop capable of handling your vehicle type. A transmission shop that only does automatics may not qualify if you drive a manual. If you are on a highway, tell the dispatcher which mile marker or exit you are near. That single detail cuts response time significantly, especially at night when landmarks are hard to describe.

If you use the mobile app, it pulls your GPS location automatically, which is faster and more accurate than trying to read road signs in the dark. The app also lets you track the tow truck in real time so you are not standing in the dark wondering if anyone is coming.

## Coverage Details

Progressive's standard roadside assistance includes:
- Towing up to 15 miles from breakdown location
- Service available 24/7/365
- Covers mechanical breakdowns, accidents, flat tires, dead batteries
- Must tow to nearest qualified repair facility, not necessarily your preferred shop

The 15-mile limit applies per incident, not per day. If you break down, get towed, and the repair fails the same afternoon, a second breakdown is a second incident with a fresh 15-mile limit from that new location.

Progressive's roadside assistance also covers jump starts, fuel delivery (you pay for the fuel itself), lockout service, and flat tire changes using your own spare. These are separate from towing, so using one does not burn your towing benefit for that incident.

**Extended coverage options:**
- Some policies offer 100-mile towing for additional premium
- Commercial policies may have different limits
- Check your specific policy documents for exact coverage

The 100-mile upgrade is worth considering if you regularly drive rural stretches where the nearest dealership or specialty shop is far. The added premium is typically modest compared to a single out-of-pocket long tow.

## What It Might Cost

**Beyond the 15-mile limit:**
- Average additional cost: $3-7 per mile
- 25-mile tow total cost: $30-70 for extra 10 miles
- Rural areas typically cost more due to limited tow truck availability

In practice, rural rates often run closer to $6-8 per mile because fewer drivers operate in those areas and deadhead distance (driving back empty) is longer. If you are towing a large truck, SUV, or AWD vehicle that requires a flatbed, expect the per-mile rate to land at the higher end of that range. Flatbeds cost more to operate than wheel-lift trucks and are required for most AWD and low-clearance vehicles.

**Without roadside coverage:**
- Full towing cost: $100-300 depending on distance and vehicle type
- Emergency after-hours service adds 50-100% surcharge

That after-hours surcharge is real. A tow at 2 a.m. on a Sunday that might cost $150 during business hours can easily run $250-300. If you are not sure whether your policy includes roadside assistance, check the declarations page or call Progressive's main line before you need it.

## Common Questions

**Q: Can I ask the tow truck to take me to my own mechanic instead of the nearest shop?**
A: Progressive's coverage only pays for the tow to the nearest qualified facility. If you want to go to a different shop, you can, but you will be responsible for any mileage beyond what the nearest-shop route would have covered, plus all costs if that preferred shop is farther away.

**Q: What counts as a "qualified repair facility" under Progressive's policy?**
A: Generally it means a licensed auto repair shop capable of servicing your vehicle type. Dealerships, franchise repair chains, and most independent shops qualify. A friend's garage or a parts store parking lot does not. If you are unsure, ask the dispatcher to confirm the destination before the truck is dispatched.

**Q: Does using roadside assistance affect my Progressive insurance rates?**
A: Roadside assistance claims are typically not counted as at-fault accidents and do not directly raise your premium the way a collision claim would. However, Progressive can factor in claims frequency over time, so it is worth checking your policy terms if you have used the benefit multiple times in a short period.

## Stay Safe

- Stay in your vehicle if on highway until tow truck arrives
- Turn on hazard lights immediately
- Pull completely off roadway when possible
- Keep emergency kit in vehicle with water, flashlight, phone charger
- Never accept rides from strangers while waiting for tow truck
- If unsafe location, ask dispatcher for police assistance
