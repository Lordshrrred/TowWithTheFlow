> **Quick Answer:** Without AAA, roadside assistance costs $75 to $250+ out of pocket depending on the service. A tow runs $100 to $200 for a short local haul. A jump start or gas delivery from a private operator typically costs $50 to $100. Lockout service runs $50 to $150. Pay-per-use apps like Urgent.ly or Honk can cut these prices by 20 to 40 percent compared to calling a random tow company directly.

## What Does Roadside Assistance Actually Cost If You Don't Have AAA?

The honest answer: it costs more than it should, and it varies a lot based on where you are and who shows up. A basic jump start from a private tow company averages $50 to $80. Gas delivery runs $60 to $100 once you factor in the service fee on top of the fuel. A tire change using your own spare is usually $50 to $75. Winching or extraction from a ditch starts at $100 and climbs fast if the truck has to work for it.

Towing is where costs really jump. A local 5-mile tow runs $100 to $150 in most mid-sized cities. Add mileage charges of $3 to $7 per mile beyond the base distance, and a 20-mile tow can hit $200 to $250 easily. ## Is There a Cheaper Way to Get Roadside Help Without a Membership?

Yes. Your best options in order of cost:

**Your auto insurance.** Check your policy right now. Most comprehensive and full-coverage policies include roadside assistance as an add-on for $5 to $15 per year. If you have it, use it. Progressive and State Farm both offer this. [Progressive's towing coverage](/progressive-insurance-towing-coverage-how-much-will-they-pay/) and [State Farm's towing limits](/state-farm-roadside-assistance-towing-coverage-limit/) vary, so verify what your policy actually pays before you're stuck on the side of the road.

**Pay-per-use apps.** Urgent.ly, Honk, and Agero connect you to vetted service providers with upfront pricing. You see the cost before you confirm. A jump start through these platforms typically runs $50 to $65. A short tow is $90 to $130. No membership required.

**Your credit card.** Visa Signature and certain Mastercard tiers include roadside dispatch. Coverage caps vary, often $50 to $80 per incident, but that covers a jump start or lockout with zero out-of-pocket.

**Calling a tow company directly.** Cheapest only if you compare quotes from two or three local operators. Don't call the first result on Google Maps. Prices can differ by $50 to $80 for the exact same service in the same zip code.

## What If I Just Can't Pay the Tow Truck Driver on the Spot?

Some drivers are surprised to find out that most tow companies require payment before releasing your vehicle. If you can't pay, the driver may not complete the tow or may deliver your car to an impound lot instead of a repair shop. Impound fees stack up fast, often $50 to $75 per day in storage costs. [What happens if you can't pay the tow truck driver](/what-happens-if-i-cant-pay-the-tow-truck-driver/) covers exactly what your options are in that situation.

Credit cards, Venmo, and Cash App are accepted by most independent operators. If you are genuinely out of options, ask the driver directly before the tow starts, not after.

## Common Questions

**Q: Can I get one-time roadside assistance without a monthly subscription?**
A: Yes. Urgent.ly and Honk let you request help and pay per incident with no membership. Most credit cards with roadside dispatch work the same way. You pay for the single event and nothing else.

**Q: How much does a locksmith cost compared to roadside assistance for a lockout?**
A: A local locksmith typically charges $75 to $150 for a car lockout, similar to roadside assistance. The difference is speed and availability. Roadside dispatch apps often pull from the same pool of locksmiths but give you upfront pricing before you commit.

**Q: Does my car warranty cover roadside assistance?**
A: Many new car factory warranties include roadside assistance for the duration of the bumper-to-bumper coverage, typically 3 years or 36,000 miles. Check your warranty booklet or call the manufacturer's customer line. Certified pre-owned vehicles from most major brands also include a roadside assistance period.

**Q: How long does it take for non-AAA roadside help to arrive?**
A: Through a pay-per-use app like Urgent.ly, average response times run 30 to 60 minutes in urban and suburban areas. Rural areas can be 60 to 90 minutes or more. Calling a local tow company directly can be faster if they're nearby, or slower if they're busy.

**Q: Is roadside assistance worth buying as an add-on to my car insurance right now?**
A: If you don't have it already, yes. At $5 to $15 per year, a single use pays for itself five to ten times over. Call your insurer and add it today, not after your next breakdown.
