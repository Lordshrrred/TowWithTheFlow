> **Quick Answer:** Often yes, but not always. Many AAA clubs reimburse towing from a non-AAA company, up to your plan's dollar limit, if you submit an itemized receipt. Reimbursement rules vary by regional club and membership tier. Calling AAA first is always safer, but if you already paid, keep every document and file a claim quickly.

## Will AAA Reimburse Me for a Tow I Already Paid For?

AAA will often reimburse you, but it is not guaranteed. AAA is a federation of regional clubs, and each one sets its own reimbursement policy. Some clubs cap reimbursement at a flat dollar amount, usually tied to your plan's towing mileage limit. Others only reimburse when AAA's own dispatch could not reach you in a reasonable time.

What decides the outcome:

- **Your membership tier.** Basic plans cover shorter tows than Plus or Premier. Your reimbursement can't exceed what AAA would have paid for the same service.
- **Whether you're an active member at the time.** A membership that lapsed or started after the breakdown won't qualify.
- **Whether AAA had a chance to respond.** Clubs are far more generous when you called them and the wait was long than when you skipped them entirely.

Check your plan's mileage limit before you file. [How far AAA will tow your car for free](/how-far-will-aaa-tow-my-car-for-free/) covers the limits by tier.

## What Do I Need to File an AAA Towing Reimbursement Claim?

You need an itemized receipt, proof of membership, and a clear account of what happened. Collect these before you leave the tow truck driver:

1. **An itemized invoice.** It should show the hookup fee, per-mile charge, any storage or after-hours fee, the pickup address, and the drop-off address. A credit card slip alone is rarely enough.
2. **The tow company's name, address, and the date and time of service.**
3. **Your AAA membership number.**
4. **Your own notes.** Where the car broke down, what failed, and why you didn't wait for AAA.

Photograph the invoice before you hand over payment. If the driver writes a total on a scrap of paper, ask for a proper invoice on company letterhead. Without it, expect a denial.

File through your regional AAA club's website, app, or member services line. Most clubs set a filing deadline, so don't sit on it. Submit within a few days, not a few months.

## Should I Cancel the Tow Truck and Call AAA Instead?

If the truck hasn't hooked up yet and you are safe, call AAA first. That is the cleanest path because AAA pays the provider directly and you owe nothing up to your limit. If a driver has already arrived, loaded your car, or you're in a dangerous spot, let the tow happen and claim it back later.

## What If AAA Denies My Reimbursement?

If AAA denies you, ask for the denial reason in writing, then appeal with the missing piece. Denials usually come from one of four things: no itemized receipt, the tow exceeded the plan's mileage limit, the breakdown happened during a gap in membership, or the service type isn't covered.

Accident tows are a common gray area. Coverage differs depending on whether the vehicle was disabled by a mechanical failure or a collision. [AAA's coverage after an at-fault accident](/does-aaa-cover-towing-after-an-at-fault-accident/) lays out where the lines fall.

If your tow ran past your mileage limit, ask AAA to reimburse the covered portion only. You can also check whether another source will cover the rest. ## Common Questions

**Q: Does AAA reimburse towing from any tow company?**
A: In many clubs, yes, as long as you submit an itemized receipt and you're an active member. The reimbursement is capped at what AAA would have paid for the same tow under your plan.

**Q: How long do I have to file an AAA towing reimbursement?**
A: It depends on your regional club, and deadlines vary. Submit within a few days to be safe, and check your club's member services page for its exact window.

**Q: Will AAA pay the full cost if the tow was longer than my plan covers?**
A: No. AAA reimburses only up to your plan's mileage limit or dollar cap. You pay the overage, so confirm your tier's limit before the truck leaves.

**Q: What if I'm not an AAA member yet when my car breaks down?**
A: Joining after a breakdown generally doesn't make that tow reimbursable. Coverage typically starts at enrollment, and some clubs add a short waiting period before service is available.

**Q: Can I get reimbursed if the tow truck came because of a flat tire or dead battery?**
A: Only if the service type is covered under your plan. Towing, flat tire changes, and jump-starts are separate benefits, so file under the right category and keep the invoice itemized.
