> **Quick Answer:** If you paid a tow company out of pocket, USAA will reimburse you through their roadside assistance benefit. Submit your claim online or through the USAA mobile app, attach the original receipt and the tow company's invoice, and USAA will pay up to your policy's covered limit. The process typically resolves within a few business days. Keep every piece of paper from the tow.

## How Do I Actually Submit a USAA Roadside Assistance Reimbursement Claim?

Log into your USAA account at usaa.com or open the USAA mobile app, navigate to "Claims," and select "Roadside Assistance Reimbursement." You fill out the incident details, upload your receipt and invoice, and submit. That is the whole process. USAA does not require you to call in and wait on hold to start a reimbursement claim for roadside events.

If you prefer the phone, you can call USAA's main claims line and request a roadside reimbursement specifically. Have your policy number, the date of the breakdown, the tow company name, and the dollar amount ready before you dial.

## What Documents Do I Need to Get Reimbursed?

You need three things: the original itemized receipt from the tow company, proof of payment (a credit card statement or bank record works), and the date and location of the breakdown. Without an itemized receipt showing what service was performed and what it cost, USAA has grounds to delay or deny the claim.

If the tow company gave you a handwritten slip, photograph it immediately. Those fade. If the company can email you a formal invoice, get that too.

## How Much Will USAA Actually Pay Me Back?

USAA reimburses up to your policy's roadside assistance towing limit, which varies by what you enrolled in. Most standard USAA roadside assistance plans cover towing up to a set dollar amount per disablement, not per mile. For the specific cap on your policy, check your declarations page or the roadside assistance section inside your USAA account. For a detailed breakdown of those limits, see [USAA Roadside Assistance Towing Coverage Limit: What You're Actually Covered For](/usaa-roadside-assistance-towing-coverage-limit/).

Anything above your covered limit comes out of your pocket. A long-distance tow, such as 80 or 100 highway miles, can easily exceed the standard limit, so knowing your cap before you authorize a tow matters.

## Does It Matter Whether I Called USAA First or Arranged My Own Tow?

Yes, it matters for how smoothly the process goes. If you called USAA's roadside assistance line first, they dispatched the tow and paid the provider directly. No reimbursement needed on your end.

## Will Filing This Affect My Insurance Rate?

Using roadside assistance reimbursement is generally not treated the same as filing a collision or comprehensive claim. USAA's roadside assistance benefit is a service benefit, and a single use is unlikely to trigger a premium increase. That said, frequent roadside claims in a short window can flag your account. One tow reimbursement after a breakdown should not move your rate.

This is separate from filing under comprehensive or collision coverage, which does have more direct impact on premiums. ## Common Questions

**Q: How long does USAA take to process a roadside assistance reimbursement?**
A: Most reimbursements process within 3 to 5 business days once you submit a complete claim with all required documents. Missing or unclear receipts are the main reason for delays.

**Q: Can I get reimbursed if I paid cash for the tow?**
A: Yes, but you need a dated, itemized receipt from the tow company and some form of proof that payment was made. A photo of the receipt and a note of the cash amount paid is your minimum documentation.

**Q: What if my tow cost more than USAA's limit?**
A: USAA pays up to your covered limit. You are responsible for the difference. There is no appeal process to recover amounts above your policy cap, so knowing that cap before authorizing a long tow is important.

**Q: Does USAA roadside assistance cover all types of tows, including winching out of a ditch?**
A: USAA roadside assistance covers standard towing to a repair facility. Winching or extraction from a ditch may qualify as a separate covered service, but check your specific policy. Submit the full itemized invoice and let USAA confirm what falls under your benefit.

**Q: What if I already paid and then realized I should have called USAA dispatch first?**
A: Submit the reimbursement claim anyway. USAA's policy allows for reimbursement when you arrange your own tow. You did not forfeit the benefit by not calling dispatch first, though going through dispatch is easier and guarantees direct payment.
