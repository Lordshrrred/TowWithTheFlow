> **Quick Answer:** Progressive covers towing through its Roadside Assistance add-on, which costs roughly $16 per year per vehicle. Coverage pays for towing to the nearest qualified repair facility, with no published per-tow dollar cap in most states. Without this add-on, Progressive pays nothing for a breakdown tow. If you only carry liability, you're paying out of pocket.

## Does Progressive Automatically Cover Towing, or Do I Have to Add It?

You have to add it. Progressive's Roadside Assistance is an optional endorsement, not something included in a standard auto policy. It is not bundled with comprehensive or collision coverage the way some drivers assume. If you never selected it at signup, open the Progressive app or log into your account right now and check under "Policy Details" before you assume you're covered.

The add-on is called "Roadside Assistance" and it covers towing, flat tire changes, jump starts, fuel delivery, lockout service, and winching. All of those services fall under one low annual premium.

## How Much Will Progressive Actually Pay for a Tow?

Progressive's Roadside Assistance pays for towing to the nearest qualified repair facility, and in most states there is no fixed dollar limit on that covered tow. The key phrase is "nearest qualified shop." If you ask the driver to haul your car 80 miles across town to your preferred dealership, Progressive can decline to cover the extra distance and bill you for the overage.

For a standard local tow of 5 to 10 miles, you should expect no out-of-pocket cost beyond your program premium. For longer distances, Progressive may cover the base tow and charge you per mile beyond a reasonable threshold, depending on your state's policy wording. Pull up your declarations page to see if your state version specifies a mileage cap or dollar limit.

If you're also dealing with accident-related towing, the coverage works differently. [Whether insurance or you pays a tow after a crash](/car-accident-towing-fee-who-pays-insurance/) depends on fault, coverage type, and the shop the car goes to.

## How Do I Use Progressive Roadside Assistance When I'm Broken Down?

Call 1-800-776-4737 or use the Progressive mobile app's Roadside Assistance button. The app is faster: it uses your GPS location to dispatch a driver without you having to describe exactly where you are, which matters when you're broken down on an unfamiliar stretch of interstate at night.

After you request service, Progressive dispatches through a third-party network. Response times vary by location. Urban areas typically see 30 to 60 minutes. Rural highways can run longer. Stay in your car with your hazards on if you're on a high-speed road. If you're on an interstate shoulder, get out of the vehicle and behind the guardrail while you wait: vehicles are struck on shoulders regularly, and being outside the car and off the road is safer than sitting in the driver's seat.

Once the tow truck arrives, confirm the destination before the driver hooks up. Tell them which shop you want. If the shop is farther than what Progressive will cover at no cost, get a price on the extra mileage before you agree.

## What If I Don't Have Progressive Roadside Assistance and I Need a Tow Right Now?

You'll pay out of pocket. A local tow runs $75 to $125 for the hook-up fee plus $3 to $7 per mile after that in most metro areas. A 10-mile tow typically lands between $100 and $175.

## Is Progressive Roadside Assistance Worth It Compared to AAA or Other Options?

For most drivers, yes. AAA's basic membership runs $60 to $80 per year and provides more robust coverage, including longer tow distances (up to 7 miles on Classic, unlimited on Premier), but you pay that fee regardless of whether you ever need it. Progressive's add-on costs a fraction of that and ties coverage directly to your vehicle, not a membership card.

The tradeoff: AAA's network is larger and dispatch tends to be faster in rural areas. If you drive frequently in low-density areas or do long road trips, AAA is worth the extra cost. If you drive mostly in metro areas and want basic protection against a busted fuel pump or dead battery, Progressive's add-on does the job.

## Common Questions

**Q: Does Progressive Roadside Assistance have a limit on how many times I can use it per year?**
A: Progressive does not publish a hard annual use cap for Roadside Assistance, but excessive use in a short period can flag your account and potentially affect renewal. One or two uses per year is normal and will not cause issues.

**Q: Will using Progressive towing coverage raise my rates?**
A: Using roadside assistance typically does not count as a claim that affects your collision or comprehensive rates. However, Progressive reserves the right to factor frequent roadside use into renewal pricing. A single tow is very unlikely to move your premium.

**Q: What does Progressive Roadside Assistance cover besides towing?**
A: It covers flat tire changes using your spare, jump starts, lockout service, fuel and fluid delivery (you pay for the fuel itself), and winching if your car is stuck in a ditch or mud within 100 feet of a road.

**Q: Can I add Progressive Roadside Assistance after a breakdown has already happened?**
A: No. You cannot add coverage mid-breakdown and apply it to the same incident. The add-on must be active before you request service. Add it now through the app if you do not have it.

**Q: Does Progressive cover towing for an accident, or only breakdowns?**
A: Roadside Assistance covers breakdowns. Accident-related towing is typically handled through your collision coverage, not Roadside Assistance. If the car is not driveable after a crash, file under collision and contact claims directly.
