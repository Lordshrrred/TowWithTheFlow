## How Much Does Towing Cost Per Mile in Seattle?

Most Seattle tow companies charge a flat hook-up fee that covers the first few miles, then bill roughly $4 to $7 per mile after that. Rates vary by company, vehicle type, and time of day, so treat these as ballpark figures, not a price list.

A typical bill has three parts:

- **Hook-up fee:** the base charge for sending a truck and loading your car
- **Mileage:** billed per mile after the included distance, sometimes counted from the driver's yard, not from you
- **Add-ons:** after-hours fees, flatbed surcharges, winching if you're off the pavement, storage if the car sits on a lot

That last point about mileage matters. Some operators measure loaded miles only. Others measure from their dispatch point to you, then to your destination. Ask which one before you agree.

## What Will My Total Be for a Typical Seattle Tow?

A tow within the city usually lands between $100 and $250 all in, and longer runs climb from there. Here's a rough way to think about it:

| Scenario | Rough Total |
|---|---|
| Ballard to a Fremont shop (short city tow) | $100 to $200 |
| Downtown to a Renton or Tukwila shop | $150 to $275 |
| Seattle to Everett or Tacoma | $250 to $450+ |
| Night, holiday, or weekend call | Add a premium on top |

These are estimates. Your actual quote depends on the company and your vehicle. For a deeper breakdown of base fees, see [how much a tow truck costs in Seattle](/how-much-does-a-tow-truck-cost-in-seattle-washington/).

## Why Is Towing Pricier on I-5, I-90, and SR-520?

Congestion, tolls, and restricted access make highway tows in Seattle cost more and take longer. A truck sitting in stop-and-go traffic on I-5 near the Mercer Street exits burns time, and some operators price that in.

A few local factors:

- **Rush hour:** I-5 through downtown and the I-90 approach from Mercer Island crawl at peak times, and a truck may take a long while to reach you
- **Bridges:** the SR-520 floating bridge has tolls, and you should ask whether a tolled route would be added to your bill. See [towing cost with Seattle bridge tolls](/towing-cost-seattle-washington-bridge-tolls/)
- **Hills and tight streets:** Capitol Hill, Queen Anne, and West Seattle have steep grades and narrow streets that can complicate loading

## Should I Ask for a Flatbed, and Does It Cost More?

Yes, ask for a flatbed if your car is all-wheel drive, low to the ground, or has transmission trouble. Flatbeds often cost a bit more than wheel-lift trucks, but they prevent drivetrain damage.

Subarus, Audis, and most AWD crossovers common around Seattle should not be dragged on two wheels. Towing an AWD car with wheels down can damage the center differential or transfer case. If the driver shows up with a wheel-lift and your car is AWD, say so before they hook up. Compare options in [flatbed vs wheel lift towing costs in Seattle](/towing-cost-seattle-washington-flatbed-vs-wheel-lift/).

## How Do I Avoid Getting Overcharged on a Seattle Tow?

Get the full price in writing or by text before the truck touches your car. That one step prevents most disputes.

Ask these five things on the phone:

1. What is the hook-up fee, and how many miles does it include?
2. What is the per-mile rate after that?
3. Are miles counted from your yard or from my location?
4. Are there after-hours, flatbed, or winch fees?
5. What is the total to my destination?

## Common Questions

**Q: Is towing in Seattle charged by the mile?**
A: Usually yes, after a base hook-up fee that covers the first few miles. Past that, most companies bill a per-mile rate, so a longer tow costs noticeably more.

**Q: How much does a 10-mile tow cost in Seattle?**
A: Roughly $120 to $250 for a 10-mile daytime tow, depending on the company and vehicle. Nights, weekends, and flatbed loads can add to that.

**Q: Does AAA cover towing costs in Seattle?**
A: AAA covers towing up to a mileage limit set by your membership level, and you pay only for miles beyond it. Check your plan's limit before you call so you know what's covered.

**Q: Can I choose my own tow company on I-5?**
A: Often yes, but if you're stopped on a busy freeway shoulder, state patrol may send a rotation company instead. You can usually request a different one if you have a roadside plan or a preferred shop.

**Q: Do tow trucks charge extra for after-hours calls in Seattle?**
A: Many do. Nights, weekends, and holidays often carry a premium, so ask for the all-in total before agreeing.
