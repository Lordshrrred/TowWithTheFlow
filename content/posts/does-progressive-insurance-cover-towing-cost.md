> **Quick Answer:** Progressive covers towing only in two situations: you added Roadside Assistance to your policy, or the tow follows a covered accident under your collision or comprehensive coverage. A basic liability-only policy pays nothing toward a tow. Check your declarations page or the Progressive app to see which coverage you have before you call anyone.

## Does Progressive Cover Towing If My Car Just Broke Down?

Only if you purchased the Roadside Assistance add-on. A standard Progressive policy, even with full coverage, does not automatically pay for a breakdown tow.

Roadside Assistance is an optional extra, usually a few dollars a month. It typically covers towing, jump-starts, flat tire changes, lockout help, and fuel delivery, with a set tow distance or dollar limit. The exact limit depends on your state and policy, so read your own documents. For the specifics, see [Progressive Roadside Assistance Towing Coverage Limit](/progressive-roadside-assistance-towing-coverage-limit/).

If you didn't add it, you're paying out of pocket. Your options are a membership service like AAA or a direct call to a local tow company.

## Does Progressive Cover Towing After an Accident?

Yes, if you carry collision coverage (for crashes) or comprehensive coverage (for things like hail, theft, or hitting a deer). Towing from the scene is normally part of the claim, though your deductible still applies to the overall repair.

If the other driver caused the crash, their liability insurance should ultimately pay for your tow, but you may have to front the cost or let your own insurer pay first and recover it later. See [Who Pays for Towing After a Car Accident That Wasn't Your Fault](/who-pays-for-towing-after-a-car-accident-not-my-fault/).

If you caused the accident and carry only liability, your policy pays for the other person's damage, not your tow. Collision coverage is what handles your own car. More detail in [Does My Car Insurance Cover Towing If I Cause the Accident](/does-my-car-insurance-cover-towing-if-i-cause-the-accident/).

## How Do I Check If I Have Progressive Roadside Coverage Right Now?

Open the Progressive app or log into your online account and look at your policy coverages. Roadside Assistance will be listed by name if you have it. If you don't have signal or the app won't load, check your declarations page, the summary document emailed when you bought or renewed.

You can also call the number on your insurance card. Have your policy number ready.

If you're stopped on a highway, don't spend twenty minutes hunting through paperwork on the shoulder. Get safe first. If you're in live traffic, exit the vehicle on the passenger side, stand well away from the road, and call 911 if you feel unsafe. Decide on coverage after you're out of danger.

## Common Questions

**Q: Does Progressive pay the tow company directly?**
A: With Roadside Assistance, you call Progressive's roadside line or use the app and they dispatch the tow, so you usually pay nothing at the curb up to your limit. If you hire your own tow company, you typically pay first and submit the receipt for reimbursement.

**Q: Does Progressive cover towing to the shop of my choice?**
A: Roadside Assistance usually covers towing to the nearest qualified shop or a set distance, not any shop you pick. If you want a farther shop, you pay the extra mileage. Check your policy's distance limit.

**Q: Will using Progressive roadside assistance raise my rates?**
A: Roadside Assistance claims are generally treated differently from at-fault collision claims, and many insurers don't surcharge for them. Progressive can tell you how it applies to your specific policy, so ask before you file if it worries you.

**Q: Is towing covered if my car was stolen and recovered?**
A: Often yes, if you carry comprehensive coverage, since theft falls under it. Towing and storage from the recovery location are normally handled through the claim.

**Q: Does Progressive cover a tow for a flat tire or dead battery?**
A: Only with Roadside Assistance, and those usually get a service call first: a tire change or jump-start. A tow comes into play if the car still can't be driven. Compare this to [Does Roadside Assistance Cover a Flat Tire or Just Towing](/does-roadside-assistance-cover-flat-tire-or-just-towing/).
