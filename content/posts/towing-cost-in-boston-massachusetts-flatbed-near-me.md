## How Much Does a Flatbed Tow Cost in Boston?

Plan on $125 to $250 for a local flatbed tow in Boston, and more for longer hauls or bad conditions. Prices vary by company, so treat this as a range, not a rate card.

Most shops charge a hookup fee, commonly $75 to $150, plus a per-mile rate after the first few miles. Flatbeds cost more than wheel-lift trucks because the equipment is pricier to run.

Things that push the bill up:
- Rush hour on I-93, the Mass Pike (I-90), or Storrow Drive
- Nights, weekends, and holidays
- Snow, ice, or a car in a ditch that needs winching
- Tight access, like a narrow Back Bay side street or an underground garage
- Distance beyond the first few miles

If you're comparing numbers across the area, our [Boston towing cost breakdown](/towing-cost-boston-massachusetts/) goes deeper on hookup fees and mileage.

## Do I Actually Need a Flatbed or Will a Regular Tow Truck Do?

You need a flatbed if your car is all-wheel drive, electric, hybrid, low to the ground, or has transmission, drivetrain, or major suspension damage. A wheel-lift truck drags two wheels on pavement, and that can wreck certain drivetrains.

Specific cases where flatbed is the right call:
- **AWD vehicles** like Subarus, which are everywhere in Massachusetts. Towing with two wheels down can damage the center differential.
- **Teslas and other EVs.** Dragging wheels can cause motor and regen damage. Tell the dispatcher it's an EV when you call.
- **Slammed or low-clearance cars** that can scrape on a wheel-lift.
- **Cars that won't roll or steer**, such as a seized engine or a failed transmission.

A basic front-wheel-drive car with a dead battery can often go on a cheaper wheel-lift. Ask for the flatbed price and the wheel-lift price, then decide.

## How Do I Avoid Getting Overcharged on a Boston Tow?

Ask for a total price, in writing or by text, before the truck leaves the yard. Then confirm what's included.

Ask these before you say yes:
1. What is the total with hookup, mileage, and taxes?
2. Are there extra fees for winching, after-hours, or storage?
3. Where exactly is my car going, and what are their storage rates?

Be wary of any driver who shows up unrequested. Scam tow operators work breakdown hot spots, and a stranger who stops and offers a tow on the shoulder of I-93 near the Zakim Bridge approach may quote a number that grows once your car is hooked up. Call the company yourself. If you have roadside coverage, use it first. Our guide to [GEICO roadside assistance limits](/geico-roadside-assistance-how-many-times-per-year/) shows how many tows a policy typically covers.

Take photos of your car before loading. It takes ten seconds and settles damage disputes.

## What If I Have No Insurance or Roadside Coverage?

Then you pay out of pocket, so shop the call. Phone two or three companies, give the same details (vehicle, location, destination, drivetrain), and compare total quotes.

Ask about payment up front. Many Boston tow operators take cards, but some want cash. Get the answer before the driver arrives so you aren't stuck at the curb.

Pick the destination carefully. Towing to your regular mechanic costs per mile, so a shop ten miles away costs more than one two miles away. ## Common Questions

**Q: Is a flatbed tow more expensive than a regular tow in Boston?**
A: Yes, usually by $25 to $75 or more, because flatbed trucks cost more to operate. For AWD cars and EVs the extra cost is worth it to avoid drivetrain damage.

**Q: How long does it take for a flatbed to arrive in Boston?**
A: Often 30 to 60 minutes in normal conditions, but longer in rush hour, snow, or heavy demand. Ask for an honest ETA when you call and a number to reach the driver.

**Q: Can I ride in the flatbed with my car being towed?**
A: Often yes, since most flatbeds have a bench seat for one or two passengers. Ask the dispatcher when you book, because some companies limit riders for insurance reasons.

**Q: What does it cost to tow a car in Boston at night?**
A: Expect an after-hours surcharge on top of the normal price, often tens of dollars extra. Confirm the full price before the truck is dispatched.
