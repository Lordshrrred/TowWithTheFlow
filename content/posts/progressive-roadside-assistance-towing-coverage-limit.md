> **Quick Answer:** Progressive's towing limit depends on your policy, not a single national number. Coverage is typically capped by dollar amount or by distance, and the cap is printed in your policy documents and app. If the tow runs past your limit, you pay the difference. Before the truck loads your car, ask the driver for the total cost and confirm it against your Progressive limit.

## What Is the Towing Coverage Limit on Progressive Roadside Assistance?

There is no single Progressive towing limit that applies to everyone. Your limit is set by the roadside coverage you bought, and it is usually written as either a maximum dollar amount per tow or a maximum distance.

Progressive sells roadside assistance as an add-on to an auto policy. Terms vary by state and by the plan selected, so quoting a number from a forum or a friend's policy is a mistake. Your declarations page and the Progressive app show your actual terms. Look for the roadside or "towing and labor" line.

Two things to confirm while you're stuck:

- **The cap type.** Is it dollars per disablement, or miles?
- **The use limit.** Some plans cap how many service calls you get per policy period.

If you can't find it in the app, call the number on your insurance card. Don't guess.

## What Happens If the Tow Costs More Than My Limit?

If the tow exceeds your limit, you pay the overage, usually to the tow company directly at pickup or drop-off. Progressive covers up to the cap and not a dollar past it.

This is where drivers get surprised. A flatbed hauling a car 40 miles on a weekend can blow through a small cap fast. Our breakdowns of [how much emergency towing costs on weekends](/how-much-does-emergency-towing-cost-on-weekends/) and [how far insurance will tow your car after a breakdown](/how-far-will-insurance-tow-my-car-after-a-breakdown/) show how quickly mileage and timing add up.

Do this before the driver hooks up:

1. Ask for the total price and the per-mile rate in plain terms.
2. Compare it to your cap.
3. If it's over, decide whether to pay the difference or have the car taken to the nearest safe shop instead of your preferred one.

The nearest-shop option often keeps the whole tow inside your limit. You can move the car again later, on your schedule.

## Does Progressive Pay the Tow Company Directly or Do I Reimburse Myself?

It depends on how the tow is dispatched. If you request service through Progressive, the tow company is generally paid by Progressive up to your limit, and you cover any excess. If you call a tow company on your own, you typically pay first and submit for reimbursement.

Reimbursement is where claims go sideways. Keep every piece of paper:

- The itemized receipt, showing pickup point, drop-off point, mileage, and any extra fees
- The time and location of the breakdown
- Photos of the car and its position if you can take them safely

A receipt that says only "towing $240" is harder to claim than one that itemizes hookup, mileage, and storage. Ask the driver to write it out.

Reimbursement can also hinge on whether Progressive approved the provider. If the stress of the moment allows, start the request through the app so the tow is

## What Does Progressive Roadside Assistance Cover Besides Towing?

Roadside plans usually bundle several services beyond the tow itself, though exact inclusions depend on your plan. Common items are jump-starts, flat tire changes, lockout help, and fuel delivery, often as separate service calls with their own limits.

Don't assume a flat tire is covered the same way a tow is. Read the details in [does roadside assistance cover a flat tire or just towing](/does-roadside-assistance-cover-flat-tire-or-just-towing/). If you're unsure whether your policy even includes roadside, see [does car insurance cover roadside assistance if I add it](/does-car-insurance-cover-roadside-assistance-if-i-add-it/).

One practical detail: a tow that follows an accident can fall under your collision coverage rather than roadside assistance. That's a different limit with different rules, so tell the claims rep which situation you're in.

## What Should I Do Right Now While I'm Waiting?

Get yourself safe first, then deal with the paperwork. Pull as far onto the shoulder as you can, turn on your hazards, and stay out of the car if you're beside moving traffic, standing well off the roadway behind a barrier when possible.

Then:

1. Open the Progressive app or call the number on your card and request service so the dispatch is documented.
2. Share your exact location. Use a mile marker, exit number, or cross street.
3. Ask for the estimated arrival time and the tow cost against your cap.
4. If the wait drags, read [how long you can wait for a tow truck on the highway](/how-long-can-you-wait-for-a-tow-truck-on-highway/).

Write down the dispatch confirmation number. If a driver shows up who wasn't sent through your request, verify the company name before you let anyone touch the car.

## Common Questions

**Q: Is there a standard dollar limit for Progressive roadside towing?**
A: No. The limit depends on the plan you bought and your state. Check your declarations page or the Progressive app for the exact dollar or mileage cap on your policy.

**Q: Can I use Progressive roadside assistance more than once?**
A: Often there is a cap on service calls per policy period, but it varies by plan. Your policy documents state how many calls you get.

**Q: Will Progressive tow my car to any shop I choose?**
A: Only up to your limit. If your preferred shop is farther than the cap allows, you pay the extra miles, or you can tow to a closer shop to stay covered.

**Q: What if I don't have roadside coverage added to my Progressive policy?**
A: Then towing isn't covered under roadside, and you pay out of pocket unless a collision claim applies. Get the total price from the driver before the hookup.

**Q: Should I pay the tow driver and get reimbursed or let Progressive handle it?**
A: Dispatching through Progressive usually means they pay the company directly up to your limit. If you hire a truck yourself, keep an itemized receipt and expect to file for reimbursement.
