> **Quick Answer:** Freeway towing in Los Angeles costs $150-400+ for standard distances. Base rates start at $100-150, plus $4-8 per mile. Add $50-100 for nights/weekends, $25-75 for freeway hazards, and up to $150 for rush hour surcharges. Heavy traffic zones like I-405 and I-10 cost more.

## What To Do

1. **Call 911 first if unsafe** - California Highway Patrol will dispatch a tow truck and secure the scene on busy freeways like I-5, I-405, or I-10.

2. **Get off the freeway if possible** - Even rolling to the nearest exit can save $100-200 compared to [towing cost from highway to nearest exit](/towing-cost-from-highway-to-nearest-exit/) scenarios.

3. **Ask for total cost upfront** - LA tow operators must provide written estimates. Demand breakdown of base fee, mileage, and surcharges before they hook up.

4. **Check your insurance first** - Many policies cover freeway breakdowns. [State Farm roadside assistance towing limits](/state-farm-roadside-assistance-towing-limits-coverage/) can save you hundreds.

5. **Choose your destination wisely** - Towing to the nearest exit costs $150-250. Going to your preferred shop across town can hit $300-500.

6. **Document everything** - Take photos of your car's position, the tow truck, and get the driver's license number. LA has strict towing regulations.

## What It Might Cost

**Base towing rates:**
- Standard tow: $100-150 base + $4-8/mile
- Flatbed (recommended): $125-175 base + $5-10/mile
- Heavy-duty vehicle: $200-300 base + $8-15/mile

**Los Angeles freeway surcharges:**
- Rush hour (6-10am, 3-7pm): $50-150 extra
- Night/weekend: $50-100 extra  
- Hazardous position: $25-75 extra
- Police-required tow: $75-125 extra

**Distance examples:**
- Freeway to nearest exit: $150-250
- 10 miles to shop: $200-350
- Cross-town (25+ miles): $350-500+

**High-cost freeway zones:**
- I-405 through West LA: Premium rates
- I-10 downtown connector: Heavy surcharges
- I-5 through Hollywood: Rush hour penalties

## Stay Safe

- Pull as far right as possible, ideally past the white line
- Turn on hazard lights immediately  
- Exit through the passenger door if traffic allows
- Stand away from your vehicle and traffic flow
- Never accept rides from passing motorists
- Keep your phone charged for emergency calls
- Stay visible with reflective clothing if you have it
