> **Quick Answer:** Yes. AAA covers towing after an accident, not just breakdowns. A tow is a tow to them, whether the car died or got hit. What changes your cost is your plan tier: Classic, Plus, or Premier. Each has a different free tow distance, and anything past that limit is billed to you. Your membership must also be active on the day of the crash.

## Does AAA Cover Towing After a Car Accident?

Yes. AAA treats a drivable-or-not collision tow the same as a breakdown tow, as long as the car can't safely be driven. Roadside assistance is about vehicle disablement, not what disabled it.

Call the number on your membership card or use the AAA app. Tell the dispatcher it was a collision so they know what they're towing to. Two things to know:

- If police are on scene, they may have already called a rotation tow company. You can decline that tow and ask for AAA instead, unless the car is blocking traffic and officers need it cleared immediately.
- Never let a stranger's truck hook up just because it showed up first. Tow companies chasing accident scenes is a real thing.

## How Far Will AAA Tow My Car After a Wreck?

It depends on your tier. Classic generally covers about 5 miles. Plus extends that to around 100 miles. Premier goes to around 200 miles. These figures vary by regional AAA club, so check your own membership terms.

Past the free distance, you pay a per-mile rate to the driver. Classic members towing to a body shop 15 miles away usually owe the overage. If your preferred shop is far, tell dispatch up front so the quote isn't a surprise. For more on how mileage caps work across providers, see [how far insurance will tow your car after a breakdown](/how-far-will-insurance-tow-my-car-after-a-breakdown/).

## Does AAA Pay Anything Beyond the Tow Itself?

No. AAA covers the tow, not the damage, storage, or repairs. Roadside assistance is a service benefit, not collision coverage.

What AAA does not pay for after a wreck:
- Body shop or mechanical repairs
- Storage fees if your car sits at a tow yard
- Rental cars or rideshare home
- Cleanup fees for fluid spills or debris, which some tow yards charge

Storage is the one that bites. If your car lands at an impound-style lot, daily fees start fast and some lots charge a gate fee to retrieve belongings. Get the car moved to your shop or home within a day or two.

Yes, that is exactly when you need the tow. Deployed airbags, a bent frame, leaking fluid, or a car that won't steer straight are all reasons not to drive it, even if it starts.

## Common Questions

**Q: Does AAA cover towing if the accident was my fault?**
A: Yes. AAA doesn't ask who caused the crash. If your membership is active and the car is disabled, you're eligible for the tow up to your plan's mileage limit.

**Q: Do I have to call AAA myself or can the police do it?**
A: You should call yourself. Police generally call their own rotation tow companies, not AAA. Tell the officer you have AAA and want your own tow unless the car must be cleared immediately.

**Q: Can I join AAA after the accident and use it right away?**
A: No. New memberships typically have a waiting period before roadside service kicks in, and AAA won't cover a tow for an incident that already happened. Sign up before you need it.

**Q: What if my car is towed to a tow yard instead of my shop?**
A: Call your insurer and AAA as soon as you can to arrange a transfer to your shop. Storage fees accrue daily, so move it fast and keep every receipt for your claim.

**Q: How many tows does AAA cover per year?**
A: Most plans allow up to four service calls per year, though the exact number depends on your club and tier. A single accident tow counts as one call.
