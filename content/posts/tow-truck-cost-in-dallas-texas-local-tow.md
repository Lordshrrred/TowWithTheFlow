## How Much Does a Local Tow Cost in Dallas?

Most local tows in Dallas land between $75 and $175, depending on the truck type and how far you're going. The base fee, called a hook-up fee, usually covers the first few miles. After that, expect a per-mile charge on top.

Here's how it typically breaks down:

- **Hook-up fee:** $75 to $125 for a standard tow truck
- **Flatbed surcharge:** often $25 to $75 more
- **Per-mile charge past the included distance:** roughly $4 to $7
- **After-hours or holiday fee:** commonly $25 to $100 extra

These are typical ranges, not guaranteed quotes. Prices vary by company. For a deeper breakdown, see [how much a tow truck costs in Dallas](/how-much-does-a-tow-truck-cost-in-dallas-texas/).

## What Do I Do Right Now If My Car Dies in Dallas?

Get the car out of traffic lanes if it still rolls, then call for a tow from a safe spot. Dallas freeways are unforgiving: I-35E, US-75 (Central Expressway), the Dallas North Tollway, and the High Five interchange at I-635 and US-75 all carry fast, heavy traffic with narrow shoulders in places.

Do this in order:

1. Turn on your hazard lights.
2. Coast to the right shoulder or the nearest exit if you can. Avoid stopping in the left-side shoulder on I-30 or I-35E.
3. If you're stuck in a live lane, stay buckled in and call 911 first, tow second.
4. Once you're safe, call your insurer or roadside plan before a random tow company. You may already be covered.
5. Get a total price quote and an arrival estimate before agreeing to anything.

If you're on a highway, read [who to call when your car breaks down on a Dallas highway](/car-broke-down-on-dallas-highway-who-to-call-fast/). Highway tows often cost more than the same car moved off a surface street.

## What Makes One Dallas Tow Cost More Than Another?

Distance, truck type, time of day, and pickup location are what move the price. A car dead in a parking lot in Deep Ellum is a simple pull. A car stuck in a lane on I-635 near the LBJ and Preston Road area is a more dangerous, more expensive job.

- **Flatbed vs. hook and chain:** All-wheel-drive vehicles, low sports cars, and most newer automatics should go on a flatbed. Dragging them can damage the drivetrain. See [hook and chain vs. flatbed costs in Dallas](/towing-cost-in-dallas-texas-hook-and-chain-vs-flatbed/).

## How Do I Avoid Getting Overcharged on a Dallas Tow?

Get the full price in writing or by text before the truck hooks up, and never accept an unsolicited tow. Predatory towing exists, especially in parking lots and on freeway shoulders where drivers show up before you've called anyone.

Practical steps:

- Ask: "What is the total price to tow from here to [exact address]?" Not just the hook-up fee.
- Ask whether mileage, fuel, or after-hours fees are added on top.
- Confirm the truck type matches your car (flatbed for AWD or low clearance).
- Never let a driver who showed up uninvited tow you. If a truck pulls up on I-30 and you didn't call, say no.
- Take photos of your car before it's loaded, and get an itemized receipt afterward.
- Ask which shop or lot the car is going to. Storage fees at an impound-style lot add up daily.

If you're uninsured, you have fewer discounts but the same right to a clear quote. See [Dallas tow costs with no insurance](/tow-truck-cost-dallas-texas-no-insurance/).

## Common Questions

**Q: How much does a basic tow cost in Dallas?**
A: A basic local tow in Dallas usually runs $75 to $175, covering the hook-up and the first several miles. Extra miles, flatbeds, and after-hours service raise the total.

**Q: Is a flatbed tow more expensive in Dallas?**
A: Yes, usually by about $25 to $75 over a standard tow truck. It's worth it for AWD, 4WD, low-clearance, or electric vehicles, which can be damaged by being dragged.

**Q: Does my car insurance cover towing in Texas?**
A: Only if you added roadside assistance or towing coverage to your policy. Check your app or policy documents before paying out of pocket, because many policies include a set mileage or dollar limit.

**Q: Can a tow truck charge extra after quoting me a price?**
A: Legitimate companies stick to the quote, but surprise fees happen when you didn't get the total up front. Always ask for the all-in price and keep a text or recorded confirmation.

**Q: How long does a tow truck take to arrive in Dallas?**
A: Often 20 to 60 minutes, longer during rush hour, storms, or on a busy weekend night. Ask for an honest arrival estimate and call someone else if it stretches past what you were told.
