> **Quick Answer:** Progressive's towing reimbursement varies by policy type. Standard roadside assistance typically covers $100 per incident, while some premium policies cover up to $200. You pay upfront and submit receipts for reimbursement. Coverage includes up to 15 miles of towing per incident.

## What Progressive Reimburses for Towing

Progressive's towing reimbursement depends on your specific roadside assistance coverage level:

**Standard Roadside Assistance:** $100 maximum per towing incident
**Enhanced Coverage:** Up to $200 per incident (available with select policies)
**Distance Limit:** Up to 15 miles included in the reimbursement amount

The reimbursement covers the base towing fee and mileage within the limit. Any costs beyond 15 miles come out of your pocket.

## What To Do When You Need Towing

1. **Call Progressive first** at 1-800-776-4737 to arrange towing through their network
2. **If using Progressive's service fails,** find your own tow truck and pay upfront
3. **Get a detailed receipt** showing the breakdown location, destination, mileage, and total cost
4. **Take photos** of your disabled vehicle and the tow truck if possible
5. **Submit your claim** through Progressive's mobile app, website, or by calling customer service
6. **Include all documentation:** receipts, photos, and your claim number

Progressive prefers you use their contracted tow services, but they will reimburse you for private towing when their service is unavailable or inadequate.

## Getting Your Money Back Fast

Submit your reimbursement claim within 30 days of the towing incident. Progressive typically processes reimbursements within 5-7 business days once they receive complete documentation.

Required documentation includes:
- Original towing receipt with company details
- Proof of payment
- Your policy number
- Date and location of breakdown

Keep receipts for any towing costs that exceed Progressive's limits. You might be able to claim these on your taxes or through other insurance coverage.

## What Counts Toward Your Limit

Progressive counts each breakdown incident separately. If your [car transmission is slipping and needs immediate towing](/car-transmission-slipping-need-tow-truck-now/), that uses one incident regardless of whether you need multiple attempts to reach a repair shop.

The reimbursement covers:
- Towing from breakdown location to nearest qualified repair facility
- Winching your vehicle if stuck
- Basic roadside labor (up to 30 minutes)

It does not cover storage fees, impound costs, or towing for convenience rather than necessity.

## When Standard Coverage Isn't Enough

If you frequently drive long distances or in remote areas, Progressive's standard $100 limit might not cover your actual towing costs. [Highway breakdowns often cost $150-300](/towing-cost-from-highway-to-nearest-exit/) depending on location and distance to facilities.

Consider upgrading to enhanced roadside coverage or supplementing with AAA membership for higher limits and additional services.

## What It Might Cost

Progressive's roadside assistance typically adds $15-25 per six-month policy period. The enhanced coverage with higher reimbursement limits costs an additional $10-15 on top of the standard rate.

Compare this to average towing costs that range from $85-200 for short-distance tows, making the coverage valuable even if you only use it once during your policy period.

Remember that [Progressive covers a specific number of incidents per year](/progressive-insurance-towing-coverage-how-many-times/), so plan accordingly if you experience multiple breakdowns.
