## What You'll Actually Pay for a Tow in Nashville

Nashville towing prices follow a two-part structure: a base hookup fee plus a per-mile charge. Here is what local operators typically charge in 2026.

**Hookup fee:** $75 to $125

**Per mile:** $3 to $7

**After-hours surcharge:** Add $25 to $75 on top of the base rate for calls after 9 p.m., weekends, or holidays.

**Flatbed:** Usually $15 to $30 more than a standard wheel-lift truck. If you drive an AWD or 4WD vehicle, a flatbed is not optional. Running the wrong tow equipment on those drivetrains causes real damage.

A 7-mile tow from a surface street to a shop in Midtown or East Nashville: roughly $100 to $170. A longer haul, say from Antioch or Hermitage back into downtown, can hit $175 to $250 depending on the company and time of day.

If you broke down on I-40, I-65, or I-24, the response may come from a Tennessee Department of Transportation HELP truck first. HELP trucks provide free short-distance pushes to the nearest exit. That will not get your car to a shop, but it gets you off the shoulder and buys you time to call a private tow.

## What To Do

1. **Get off the road.** If you are on a Nashville interstate, get to the shoulder as far right as possible. Turn on your hazards immediately.
2. **Call 511 or *THP** on your cell. Tennessee Highway Patrol can dispatch a HELP truck for free on major interstates. Do this before calling a private company.
3. **Get at least two quotes.** Call two local tow companies and give them your exact location and destination. Ask for the base fee and per-mile rate upfront. Any company that refuses to quote over the phone is a red flag.
4. **Check your insurance or membership first.** State Farm, AAA, USAA, Progressive, and most other carriers include towing coverage. A five-minute call to your insurer can save you $150. If you have State Farm, check their distance limits before assuming full coverage. [State Farm roadside assistance towing distance limits](/state-farm-roadside-assistance-towing-distance-limits/) explains what they will and will not cover.
5. **Confirm payment method before the truck arrives.** Most Nashville tow operators take credit cards, but some budget operators are cash-only. Sort that out before you are standing on the side of Murfreesboro Pike. See [tow truck near me accepting credit card](/tow-truck-near-me-accepting-credit-card/) if you are not sure what to expect.
6. **Photograph your vehicle before it is hooked up.** Walk around and take pictures on your phone. If there is damage after the tow, you have documentation.
7. **Get a written receipt.** Itemized: base fee, miles charged, any surcharges. You need this for insurance reimbursement.

## Stay Safe

- Do not stand behind your vehicle or between your car and traffic while waiting. Get to the guardrail side or over the barrier if possible.
- Keep your seatbelt on if you stay inside the car on a highway shoulder. A moving vehicle striking a stopped car is a real risk.
- At night, use road flares or a reflective triangle if you have them. Your hazard lights alone are not enough on a busy interstate.
- Nashville summer heat is serious. If your car is not running and the AC is off, do not sit in a closed vehicle. Get out and stand in shade away from traffic.
- If you broke down with kids in the car, [car broke down with kids in car](/car-broke-down-with-kids-in-car-safety-steps/) covers the specific steps to take.

## Common Questions

**Q: Does AAA cover towing in Nashville, and how far will they tow my car for free?**
A: Yes, AAA covers towing in Nashville. Basic membership typically includes free towing up to 5 miles, while Plus and Premier tiers extend that to 100 and 200 miles respectively. If your destination is farther than your tier allows, you pay the per-mile rate only on the overage.

**Q: How much extra will I pay if my car breaks down on a Nashville interstate at night?**
A: After-hours calls on interstates like I-40 or I-65 typically add $25 to $75 on top of the standard hookup fee. That means a tow that would cost $130 during the day could run $155 to $205 after 9 p.m. or on a weekend.

**Q: Do Nashville tow truck drivers take credit cards, or do I need cash?**
A: Most established Nashville tow operators accept credit cards, but some smaller or budget companies are cash only. Confirm the payment method before the truck is dispatched so you are not caught off guard on the side of the road.
