> **Quick Answer:** Towing during Los Angeles rush hour costs $150-$300 for the base tow, plus $50-$100 in traffic delay fees. Morning rush (7-9 AM) and evening rush (4-7 PM) add 30-45 minutes to response times. Freeway breakdowns cost more than surface streets due to CHP requirements and safety equipment.

## What To Do

1. **Call 911 if you're on a freeway** - CHP must clear the scene before tow trucks can work safely in traffic lanes.

2. **Get to the shoulder immediately** - Even if your car is barely running, move as far right as possible. Traffic delays cost you money.

3. **Call your insurance roadside assistance first** - [Progressive insurance towing reimbursement](/progressive-insurance-towing-reimbursement-amount/) and other major carriers often cover $100-$150, which helps with rush hour surcharges.

4. **Request ETA and traffic fee upfront** - Ask the dispatcher: "What's your rush hour traffic charge?" Don't get surprised by a $75 delay fee.

5. **Specify your exact location** - "405 North at Wilshire exit" gets you faster service than "somewhere on the 405." Precision matters in heavy traffic.

6. **Stay in your vehicle only if safe** - On surface streets, stay inside. On freeways, exit passenger side and get behind the barrier if possible.

## What It Might Cost

**Base towing rates in LA:**
- Local tow (5-10 miles): $125-$200
- [Freeway breakdown](/towing-cost-los-angeles-freeway-breakdown/): $150-$250
- Long distance (20+ miles): $4-$6 per mile after first 5 miles

**Rush hour surcharges:**
- Traffic delay fee: $50-$100
- Extended response time (over 45 minutes): Additional $25-$50
- Freeway safety equipment: $25-$40 extra

**Peak traffic times with highest costs:**
- Monday-Friday 7:00-9:30 AM
- Monday-Friday 4:00-7:30 PM
- Friday evenings until 8:30 PM

Expect total costs of $200-$400 for rush hour towing. [Towing cost from highway to nearest exit](/towing-cost-from-highway-to-nearest-exit/) is often your cheapest option if your car can be safely moved off the freeway first.

**Payment:** Most LA tow companies require cash or card payment on scene. Credit cards add 3-4% processing fees.

## Stay Safe

- Never exit your vehicle into traffic lanes
- Turn on hazard lights immediately
- Call someone to let them know your location
- Keep phone charged for GPS tracking updates to tow truck
- Stay hydrated - LA traffic delays can stretch towing waits to 90+ minutes
- If on surface streets, stay inside your locked vehicle
- On freeways, exit passenger side only if there's a safe barrier area
