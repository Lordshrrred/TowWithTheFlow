> **Quick Answer:** Allstate covers towing after a breakdown only if your policy includes Roadside Assistance. Standard liability, collision, and comprehensive coverage don't pay for a mechanical breakdown tow. Check your Allstate app or declarations page for the roadside add-on. If it's there, call the number on your card or request help in the app. If it's not, you're paying out of pocket.

## Does Allstate Cover Towing After a Breakdown?

Only if you bought the roadside add-on. Allstate sells its roadside coverage as an optional endorsement, so a base auto policy won't pay for a tow when your car simply dies.

Breakdown towing is a maintenance-type event, not a loss event. A dead alternator, a failed fuel pump, or a seized engine isn't something collision or comprehensive pays for. The tow is covered only when the roadside endorsement is on your policy.

Check in this order:
1. Open the Allstate app and look at your policy coverages.
2. Look at your declarations page for a line item reading roadside assistance or towing and labor.
3. If you can't tell, call the number on your insurance card and ask whether roadside is active on your vehicle.

## What If I'm Stranded Right Now and Don't Know If I Have Coverage?

Get safe first, then verify coverage while you wait. Move the car off the roadway if it still rolls, turn on your hazards, and get everyone out of the vehicle on the side away from traffic if you're on a highway shoulder.

Then use the app or your card to confirm roadside is active. Request service through Allstate directly rather than calling a tow company yourself. This matters: if you call an independent tow truck first and pay, getting reimbursed is slow and often partial. The same rule applies to other carriers, as covered in [Does AAA Pay Back Towing Costs If You Already Called a Tow Truck?](/does-aaa-pay-back-towing-costs-if-i-already-called-a-tow-truck/).

If you're somewhere unsafe, like a tight shoulder or a blind curve, call 911 first. Don't stand behind the car waiting on a dispatcher.

## How Far Will Allstate Tow My Car?

Distance limits depend on the roadside plan on your policy, so confirm the exact mileage in your own coverage documents. Most insurer roadside programs cap towing by miles or by dollar amount per event, and anything past the cap is billed to you.

A practical move: tell the dispatcher your preferred repair shop before the truck loads up. If the shop is past your limit, you can ask what the overage will cost, or have the car taken to the nearest qualified shop and moved later. For how these caps work across carriers, see [How Far Will Insurance Tow My Car After a Breakdown?](/how-far-will-insurance-tow-my-car-after-breakdown/).

## What Do I Pay If Allstate Won't Cover the Tow?

Without roadside coverage, you pay the tow company directly, and prices vary by market, distance, and time of day. Nights, weekends, and highway recoveries cost more than a daytime local tow. Flatbed service, which you need for all-wheel-drive and many modern vehicles, usually costs more than a hook-and-chain tow.

Before you agree to anything, ask the driver for the total price including hookup, per-mile rate, and any after-hours fee. Get it in writing or by text. ## Common Questions

**Q: Does Allstate cover towing if my car breaks down but I only have liability insurance?**
A: No. Liability pays for damage you cause to others, not your own car's tow. Without a roadside endorsement, a breakdown tow is your cost.

**Q: Does Allstate cover towing after an accident?**
A: Often yes, but through your collision or comprehensive coverage as part of the claim, not through roadside. The tow can be covered when the car is undrivable from a covered loss. Confirm with your claims adjuster.

**Q: Can I add Allstate roadside assistance after my car already broke down?**
A: Don't count on it. Coverage generally applies to events after the endorsement takes effect, and adding it mid-emergency to cover that same event is something insurers specifically guard against. Add it after the fact for future protection.

**Q: Will using Allstate roadside raise my premium?**
A: Roadside calls are typically handled as a service benefit, not an at-fault claim, but policies and state rules differ. Ask your agent directly if you're concerned.

**Q: Is Allstate roadside better than AAA?**
A: It depends on mileage limits, price, and whether you want coverage that follows the driver or the car. See [AAA vs Allstate Roadside Assistance: Which Is Better?](/aaa-vs-allstate-roadside-assistance-which-is-better/) for the comparison.
