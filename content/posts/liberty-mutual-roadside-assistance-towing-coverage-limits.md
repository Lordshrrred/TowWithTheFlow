> **Quick Answer:** Liberty Mutual's roadside assistance add-on typically covers towing to the nearest qualified repair facility, but the coverage limit varies by policy, usually $50 to $100 per occurrence. It does not automatically cover long-distance tows or your preferred shop across town. Always call Liberty Mutual's dispatch first, because using a tow truck you called yourself may not be reimbursed.

## What Does Liberty Mutual Actually Cover for Towing?

Liberty Mutual's standard roadside assistance covers a tow to the nearest qualified repair facility, not necessarily the shop you want. The benefit is typically capped at a dollar amount per tow, commonly in the $50 to $100 range, though some policy tiers go higher. If the tow costs more than your cap, you pay the difference at the scene.

The key word is "nearest." If your car breaks down on the interstate and the closest shop is two miles away but your usual mechanic is 30 miles away, Liberty Mutual will pay for the shorter tow. Anything beyond that comes out of your pocket.

What's included under most Liberty Mutual roadside plans:

- Towing to the nearest repair facility
- Battery jump-start
- Flat tire change (if you have a usable spare)
- Lockout service
- Fuel delivery (you pay for the fuel itself)
- Winching if you're stuck in a ditch or snow

What's not included: towing to a dealership of your choice that happens to be far away, recovery from a flood or serious accident (that usually falls under comprehensive or collision), or repeat calls for the same breakdown if the vehicle wasn't repaired.

## How Much Will Liberty Mutual Pay Per Tow?

The actual dollar limit depends on which tier of roadside assistance you added to your policy. Entry-level coverage often caps at $50 to $75 per incident. Mid-tier plans push that to $100 or more. A local tow in most U.S. cities runs $75 to $125 for the first five miles, so a low-cap plan can leave you short fast.

If you're facing a longer tow, say after a fuel pump failure on the highway, the bill climbs quickly. A 20-mile tow can run $100 to $175 depending on the provider and region. Check your declarations page right now, before you need a tow, so you know exactly what your cap is.

## Does Liberty Mutual Cover Long-Distance Towing?

No, not as a default. Standard Liberty Mutual roadside assistance is designed for a tow to the nearest facility, not a 100-mile haul to a specialist or a dealer in another city. Long-distance towing is expensive. Hauling a car 100 miles on the interstate typically runs $300 to $600 or more depending on vehicle size and route. Liberty Mutual's roadside benefit will not cover that gap.

## What Happens If I Call My Own Tow Truck Instead of Going Through Liberty Mutual?

You risk not getting reimbursed. Liberty Mutual, like most insurers, requires you to use their dispatch network to guarantee coverage. If you call a tow company directly and pay out of pocket, you can submit a reimbursement claim, but Liberty Mutual may deny it or only pay up to your coverage cap with no guarantee of approval.

Always call Liberty Mutual first. Their dispatch number is on your insurance card and in the Liberty Mutual mobile app. They coordinate the tow through their network, which keeps you from paying and waiting on reimbursement. If you're in an area where their network is thin and wait times are long, ask the dispatcher what your out-of-network reimbursement options are before you call someone else.

## Common Questions

**Q: Does Liberty Mutual roadside assistance cover towing after an accident?**
A: Usually not through the roadside assistance add-on. Post-accident towing is typically handled under your collision or comprehensive coverage, which has a separate deductible. Call Liberty Mutual and tell them it was an accident so they route the claim correctly.

**Q: Is there a limit on how many times I can use Liberty Mutual roadside assistance per year?**
A: Most Liberty Mutual roadside plans limit you to 3 to 4 service calls per policy period. After that, you either pay out of pocket or the coverage is suspended until renewal. Check your specific policy for the exact number.

**Q: Can I add roadside assistance to my Liberty Mutual policy mid-term?**
A: Yes, you can add it anytime, but Liberty Mutual typically imposes a short waiting period (often 24 to 48 hours) before it becomes active. Do not assume adding it today covers a breakdown tonight.

**Q: Will Liberty Mutual tow my car to a shop that's not the nearest one?**
A: They will dispatch a tow to the nearest qualified facility by default. If you want a specific shop that's farther away, you can request it, but you'll likely pay the mileage difference above what the nearest-facility tow would have cost.

**Q: Does Liberty Mutual roadside assistance cover motorcycles or trailers?**
A: Standard roadside assistance on an auto policy covers the insured vehicle listed on that policy. Motorcycles and trailers typically need to be separately listed and covered. Check your declarations page or call Liberty Mutual to confirm which vehicles are included.
