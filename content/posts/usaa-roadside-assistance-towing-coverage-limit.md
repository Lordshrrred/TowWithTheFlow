USAA roadside assistance is built into their Emergency Road Service add-on, and the towing benefit has a real dollar cap that can catch you off guard on a long haul or an expensive recovery. Here is exactly what that limit means and what to do if your tow costs more than it covers.

> **Quick Answer:** USAA's Emergency Road Service typically pays up to $100 per towing event, though limits can vary by policy and state. That covers a local tow in many cases, but a long-distance haul or a specialty recovery will likely exceed it. You pay the difference out of pocket unless you have a separate comprehensive coverage towing endorsement that kicks in.

## What Is the Actual Towing Coverage Limit on a USAA Policy?

USAA's Emergency Road Service add-on generally covers towing up to $100 per disablement. That is the baseline figure listed in their standard policy language, though your specific state or policy version may show a slightly different number. The $100 limit is per incident, not per year, so each separate breakdown gets its own cap applied.

If you added roadside assistance through USAA's partnership with Agero (the network that actually dispatches the trucks), the benefit is coordinated through that network, and the covered amount is charged against your policy limit before you see a bill for the overage.

For comparison, a local tow of five to ten miles in a mid-size city typically runs $75 to $125, so the limit may just barely cover a short haul. A 30-mile tow to a dealership or specialty shop can run $150 to $250 or more, leaving you responsible for the gap.

To confirm your exact limit, log into the USAA mobile app, pull up your auto policy, and look under "Emergency Road Service" in the coverages section. The dollar amount is listed there, not buried in a PDF.

## Does USAA Comprehensive Coverage Pay for Towing on Top of That?

Only if you added a separate towing and labor endorsement to your comprehensive coverage, and not all states or USAA policy types offer it. Standard comprehensive coverage pays for damage to the vehicle from events like fire, theft, or flooding. It does not automatically extend to the cost of getting the car to a shop.

If you have both Emergency Road Service and a towing endorsement, one may apply first and the other picks up the remainder, but this depends on how your specific policy is written. Call USAA claims before the tow starts if you are facing a large bill, not after. They can confirm which coverage applies and whether pre-authorization is needed.

For a broader look at how comprehensive coverage interacts with towing costs, see [Does Comprehensive Insurance Cover Towing Cost](/does-comprehensive-insurance-cover-towing-cost/).

## What Happens When the Tow Bill Exceeds the Limit?

You pay the overage directly to the tow company at the time of service or upon delivery of the vehicle. The tow driver is not going to wait for an insurance check. Get a written receipt showing the total charge, the mileage, and the destination. You will need that if you plan to dispute anything or submit the overage to a flex spending account or reimbursement program.

## How Does This Compare to USAA's Per-Year Usage Limit?

The per-year limit is separate from the per-incident dollar cap. USAA caps Emergency Road Service at a set number of service calls per policy period, typically four per year. Each call counts as one use regardless of what service was provided: tow, jump start, lockout, or fuel delivery.

Once you have used your annual allotment, USAA will not dispatch another truck under the policy. You would need to call a tow company directly and pay out of pocket. ## Should You Supplement USAA's Roadside Coverage with Something Else?

If you drive older vehicles, frequently travel rural highways, or have a specialty car that needs flatbed transport, USAA's standard $100 towing limit is not enough on its own. AAA membership is worth comparing directly. A mid-tier AAA Plus membership covers towing up to 100 miles per incident, which removes almost all out-of-pocket exposure for most breakdowns. Whether that is worth the annual fee depends on how many miles you drive and where.

## Common Questions

**Q: Does USAA roadside assistance cover towing to any repair shop I choose?**
A: Yes, within the covered distance and dollar limit. You direct the tow driver to the shop of your choice. USAA does not require you to use a specific repair network.

**Q: Will USAA raise my rates if I use roadside assistance?**
A: Generally no. Emergency Road Service claims are not counted as at-fault accidents and typically do not trigger a rate increase, but your state's rules can differ. Confirm with your USAA agent if you are concerned.

**Q: How do I file a USAA roadside assistance claim after a tow?**
A: Call 1-800-531-8722 or use the USAA mobile app. If you already paid out of pocket for a tow, submit the receipt for reimbursement up to your policy's covered amount.

**Q: Does USAA cover towing for a motorcycle under the same roadside policy?**
A: Not automatically. USAA offers separate motorcycle insurance policies, and roadside assistance for motorcycles requires its own add-on. Check your motorcycle policy specifically.

**Q: What if USAA can't get a tow truck to me quickly enough and I need to hire my own?**
A: Pay the driver, get a detailed receipt, and submit for reimbursement through USAA. Reimbursement is capped at your policy's towing limit, so you will absorb anything above that amount.
