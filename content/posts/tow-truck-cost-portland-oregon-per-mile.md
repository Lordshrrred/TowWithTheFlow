## What Does a Tow Truck Actually Cost Per Mile in Portland?

Portland tow rates land between $3 and $7 per mile, with most local companies charging toward the middle of that range, around $4 to $5 per mile. That per-mile rate never tells the full story because there is always a hook-up or dispatch fee on top of it, typically $75 to $125 regardless of distance. So even a 2-mile tow from a busted rig on the Morrison Bridge to a nearby shop is going to cost you $85 to $140 minimum.

Portland's terrain and traffic push costs up compared to flatter metros. Tows that cross the West Hills via Highway 26 or involve a winch recovery off a sloped shoulder on I-5 near Terwilliger can trigger a separate recovery fee of $50 to $150.

## Does My Insurance or Roadside Plan Actually Cover the Per-Mile Rate?

It depends entirely on your plan's cap. Many basic roadside plans cover only 5 to 10 miles of towing, leaving you to pay per mile beyond that. If your car dies on US-26 near the Sylvan interchange and the nearest qualified shop for your vehicle is in Beaverton or Tigard, you might be 12 to 18 miles out and absorbing 7 or more uncovered miles.

Call your insurer or AAA before the truck arrives and get the coverage limit confirmed. If you have no insurance at all, pay by card if possible and ask for an itemized receipt showing the hook-up fee, per-mile charge, and any extras separately. ## Common Questions

**Q: What is the average tow truck cost per mile in Portland Oregon?**
A: Most Portland tow operators charge $4 to $5 per mile as a middle-of-the-road rate, with a $75 to $125 hook-up fee on top. Rates vary by company and time of day.

**Q: Is there a minimum charge even for a short tow in Portland?**
A: Yes. The hook-up fee applies regardless of distance, so even a 1-mile tow typically costs $85 to $130 minimum after the base charge is added.

**Q: Will the tow truck driver tell me the per-mile rate before starting?**
A: They should, and you can ask for a written or verbal quote before you agree to the tow. If a driver refuses to give you a rate estimate upfront, that's a red flag worth noting.

**Q: Does AAA cover the full per-mile rate in Portland?**
A: AAA Classic covers towing up to 5 miles, Plus covers up to 100 miles, and Premier covers up to 200 miles. Miles beyond your tier's limit are billed at the company's standard per-mile rate, which you pay out of pocket.

**Q: Can I negotiate the per-mile rate on the spot in Portland?**
A: Sometimes. If you call multiple companies before agreeing, you have more leverage. Once the truck is hooked up, your negotiating position drops sharply. Get quotes from at least two operators if you have time and are in a safe location.
