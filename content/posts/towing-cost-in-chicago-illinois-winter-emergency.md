> **Quick Answer:** Chicago winter emergency towing costs $150-400+ for local tows. Base rates run $100-200, but winter weather adds $50-150 surcharges. Ice storms, blizzards, and subzero temperatures drive prices higher due to dangerous conditions and equipment needs. Call multiple companies for quotes during storms.

## What To Do

1. **Get to safety first.** Turn on hazard lights, move away from traffic if possible, and stay warm in your vehicle.

2. **Call your insurance roadside assistance.** Many policies cover winter emergencies. [Check if State Farm roadside assistance covers your situation](/state-farm-roadside-assistance-towing-limits-coverage/) before paying out of pocket.

3. **Request quotes from 3-4 local tow companies.** Prices vary widely during winter storms. Ask specifically about weather surcharges and wait times.

4. **Confirm the total price upfront.** Include base towing rate, mileage fees, winter weather surcharge, and any equipment charges for ice or snow conditions.

5. **Specify your exact location clearly.** Winter conditions make some areas harder to reach. Mention if you're on a highway, side street, or parking lot covered in ice.

6. **Prepare for longer wait times.** Winter emergencies can mean 2-4 hour delays during storms when demand spikes.

## What It Might Cost

**Standard Winter Rates:**
- Base tow: $100-200
- Winter weather surcharge: $50-150
- Per mile beyond included distance: $3-7
- Ice/snow equipment use: $25-75

**Peak Storm Pricing:**
- Major snowstorms: $250-400+
- Ice storm conditions: $300-500+
- Subzero temperatures: Additional $50-100

Distance affects your total significantly. [Chicago downtown towing](/towing-cost-chicago-illinois-downtown/) typically costs more than suburban areas due to traffic and access challenges. A 10-mile tow during a winter storm can easily reach $350-450.

**What Drives Winter Costs Higher:**
- Dangerous driving conditions requiring specialized equipment
- Limited tow truck availability during storms
- Increased insurance and liability costs
- Longer response times due to weather

## Stay Safe

- Keep blankets, water, and phone chargers in your car during winter months
- Never leave your vehicle running unattended while waiting for a tow
- Stay inside your car if you're on a busy road or highway
- Clear snow from your exhaust pipe if engine is running to prevent carbon monoxide buildup
- Have cash or card ready as some winter emergency services require immediate payment

Winter emergency towing in Chicago requires patience and preparation. [Winter storm surcharges](/towing-cost-chicago-winter-storm-surcharge/) are standard practice when roads become hazardous. The key is calling early before conditions worsen and tow trucks become completely unavailable.
