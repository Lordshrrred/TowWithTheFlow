> **Quick Answer:** A tow truck in Seattle costs $75 to $150 for a short local tow of 5 to 10 miles. Longer hauls run $150 to $300 or more, billed at $4 to $7 per mile after the hook-up fee. Flatbed trucks cost more than wheel-lift rigs. Traffic, bridge tolls, and your breakdown location all push the price up.

## What Does a Tow Truck Actually Cost in Seattle Right Now?

A standard local tow in Seattle costs $75 to $150 for the first 5 to 10 miles. After that, expect to pay $4 to $7 per mile depending on truck type and the company you call.

Here is a quick breakdown of what you are actually paying for:

- **Hook-up fee:** $50 to $100, charged just to show up and connect to your vehicle
- **Per-mile rate:** $4 to $7 per mile after the base distance
- **Flatbed tow (5 miles):** $100 to $175
- **Wheel-lift tow (5 miles):** $75 to $130
- **After-hours or weekend dispatch:** Add $25 to $75 on top of standard rates

If you are broken down on I-5 near the Ship Canal Bridge or on SR-99 heading into the Battery Street Tunnel approach, trucks dispatched from SoDo or Capitol Hill have a short run to you. Getting stuck on I-90 near Mercer Island or on SR-520 over Lake Washington adds distance and, potentially, bridge costs. If your tow crosses a tolled bridge, some companies pass that charge directly to you. For a detailed look at how bridge tolls affect your final bill, see [Towing Cost Seattle Washington Bridge Tolls](/towing-cost-seattle-washington-bridge-tolls/).

## Does It Cost More If I Need a Flatbed vs. a Wheel-Lift Truck?

Yes. Flatbeds cost more to operate and more to dispatch. In Seattle, a flatbed tow typically runs $25 to $50 higher than a wheel-lift for the same distance.

Low-clearance vehicles like a Tesla Model 3, all-wheel-drive SUVs, and cars with front-end damage usually require a flatbed. ## Will My Insurance or Roadside Plan Cover This?

It depends on your policy and what caused the breakdown. Most standard auto policies do not include towing unless you added roadside assistance as a rider. If you have comprehensive coverage with roadside assistance, towing is typically covered up to a set dollar limit per incident, often $50 to $100, which may not fully cover a longer Seattle tow.

AAA members typically get towing covered up to 5 or 7 miles depending on membership tier, with per-mile charges beyond that. GEICO and State Farm offer similar programs but with varying reimbursement caps. Check your card or app before you call a truck, because the process for getting reimbursed differs from being dispatched directly through your insurer.

If you have no coverage and are paying cash, get at least two phone quotes before you commit. Rates vary significantly between companies in the Seattle metro.

## Common Questions

**Q: How much does a tow truck cost for 10 miles in Seattle?**
A: Expect $100 to $175 for a 10-mile tow in Seattle. That includes a hook-up fee of $50 to $100 and a per-mile rate of $4 to $7 for the distance beyond the base drop.

**Q: Do tow truck prices in Seattle change at night or on weekends?**
A: Yes. After-hours and weekend calls typically add $25 to $75 to your base rate. Some companies charge a flat after-hours fee, others adjust their per-mile rate. Ask before they dispatch.

**Q: How long does a tow truck take to arrive in Seattle?**
A: In most Seattle neighborhoods, expect 30 to 60 minutes. If you are stuck on SR-520 or I-90 during peak hours, or in a lower-traffic area like Magnolia or West Seattle after midnight, it can run longer.

**Q: Can I negotiate the tow truck price in Seattle?**
A: Sometimes. If you call multiple companies and mention a lower competing quote, some dispatchers will match or reduce it. This works better for non-emergency, non-rush situations. If you are already on the side of I-5 in the dark, your negotiating position is limited.

**Q: What happens if I can't pay the tow truck driver in Seattle?**
A: Most companies require payment on delivery, either cash or card. If you cannot pay, the company may tow your vehicle to their lot and hold it until the balance is settled, with daily storage fees accruing. Do not let it sit more than a day or two.
