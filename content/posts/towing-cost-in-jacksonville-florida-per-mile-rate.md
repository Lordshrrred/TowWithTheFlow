> **Quick Answer:** Jacksonville towing is typically a base hookup fee of about $75 to $125 plus roughly $3 to $7 per mile after the first few miles. A 10-mile tow often lands between $100 and $200. Nights, weekends, flatbeds, and highway recoveries cost more. Get the total in writing before the truck hooks up.

## What Is the Per Mile Rate for Towing in Jacksonville?

Expect about $3 to $7 per mile in Jacksonville, charged on top of a flat hookup fee. These are typical market ranges, not fixed prices. Rates vary by company, time of day, and vehicle type.

Many companies include the first 5 to 10 miles in the base fee, so the per-mile charge only applies beyond that. Always ask "does the hookup fee include any miles?" That one question can change your bill by $20 to $40.

Jacksonville is spread out. It's one of the largest cities in the country by land area, so a tow from Arlington to Orange Park or from the Southside to the Beaches can easily run 12 to 20 miles. That's where per-mile rates add up.

## How Much Will My Total Jacksonville Tow Bill Be?

For most local tows, plan on $100 to $250 all-in, depending on distance and conditions. Here's how a typical bill breaks down:

## What Makes a Jacksonville Tow Cost More Than Quoted?

Add-ons are where bills inflate. The most common ones are flatbed fees, after-hours fees, and recovery charges. Ask about each before you agree.

- **Flatbed required.** All-wheel-drive vehicles, low-clearance cars, and anything with transmission trouble should go on a flatbed. A wheel-lift tow on the wrong car can damage the drivetrain.
- **Highway timing.** A disabled car on I-95 or I-295 during rush hour often means a faster, pricier priority response.
- **Recovery work.** If your car is in a ditch off I-10 or stuck in soft ground, winching is billed separately.
- **Storage fees.** If your car goes to the tow company's lot instead of a shop, daily storage starts quickly. Ask the lot's hours and fees up front.

Get the total price quoted by phone, then confirm it again when the driver arrives. If the number changes, you can refuse service before the hookup.

## How Do I Avoid Getting Overcharged on a Jacksonville Tow?

Get the quote in writing (text counts), confirm the destination, and never accept a tow from an unsolicited truck that shows up at the scene. Predatory towing happens, especially after accidents and on busy corridors.

A few specific protections:

- Ask for the company name, truck number, and total price before hookup.
- Don't sign a blank authorization form.
- Take photos of your car's condition before it's loaded.
- Ask where your car is going and confirm the shop is open. Dropping at a closed shop means storage fees.
- Pay by card when possible so you can dispute a bad charge.

Florida limits what towers can charge for some non-consensual tows, but you generally have more protection when you call the company yourself and agree to the price first.

## Common Questions

**Q: Is towing cheaper per mile for longer distances in Jacksonville?**
A: Often yes. Some companies lower the per-mile rate past 20 or 30 miles, but the base fee still applies. Ask whether they offer a flat long-distance price.

**Q: How much does a flatbed tow cost in Jacksonville?**
A: Usually $25 to $75 more than a standard wheel-lift tow, either as a surcharge or a higher base fee. All-wheel-drive and many newer vehicles need one.

**Q: Does my car insurance cover towing in Florida?**
A: Only if you added roadside assistance or towing coverage. Check your policy or app. Most plans cap the distance, so you may owe the difference on a longer tow.

**Q: Can I have my car towed to my own mechanic instead of the tow yard?**
A: Yes, if you call the tow company yourself and agree to the destination. Confirm the shop is open to receive it, or you'll pay storage fees at a lot overnight.

**Q: Why is a tow on I-95 more expensive than a street tow?**
A: Highway tows can involve traffic control, faster response demands, and tougher loading conditions. Expect higher pricing than a car in a parking lot.
