> **Quick Answer:** State Farm's roadside assistance typically covers $100-200 per towing incident with no annual dollar limit, but limits the number of service calls per year. Standard coverage includes 4-6 service calls annually, while enhanced plans may offer unlimited services.

## What Your State Farm Towing Coverage Includes

State Farm offers roadside assistance through two main options:

1. **Basic Roadside Coverage**: Usually 4 service calls per year, $100-150 per tow
2. **Enhanced/Premium Coverage**: 6+ service calls per year or unlimited, $150-200+ per tow

The annual limit isn't a dollar amount. It's based on service incidents. That means a $180 tow and a $95 tow both count as one service call each. You don't lose extra calls for using more money on a single incident.

Each service call typically covers one dispatched unit, one trip. If the tow truck arrives and can jump-start you instead of towing, that still counts as one call. Use it wisely.

## What To Do When You Need Towing

1. Call State Farm's roadside number: 1-877-SF-CLAIM (1-877-732-5246)
2. Provide your policy number and location
3. Specify you need towing service
4. Ask for confirmation of your remaining service calls for the year
5. Get the tow truck company's name and estimated arrival time
6. Keep all receipts if you pay upfront

If you're on a busy highway or in an unsafe location, get off the road completely before you make the call. Response times vary by region, but urban areas typically see trucks in 30-60 minutes. Rural areas can run 90 minutes or longer. Ask the dispatcher for a realistic ETA so you're not standing roadside longer than necessary.

If you use the State Farm mobile app to request service, you can track the truck's location in real time, which is useful when you're in an unfamiliar area or after dark.

## Coverage Details That Matter

**Distance limits**: Most policies cover towing to the nearest qualified repair facility, typically 15-25 miles included. "Nearest qualified" is the key phrase. State Farm dispatchers define this, not you.

**Excess distance**: You pay per-mile charges beyond the included distance, usually $3-5 per mile. If your preferred shop is 40 miles away and your policy covers 15, expect to pay out of pocket for those extra 25 miles. At $4 per mile, that's $100 added to your bill.

**Service call resets**: Your annual service calls reset on your policy anniversary date, not January 1st. If your policy renewed in April, you get a fresh count every April. Keep track of this, especially if you've already used two or three calls mid-year.

**Winching and lockout**: Many roadside plans under State Farm also cover winching if you're stuck in a ditch or snow, plus lockout service and fuel delivery. These all draw from the same service call pool, so a lockout in January and two tows later that year could leave you short by fall.

## What It Might Cost

- **Monthly premium**: $15-35 added to your auto policy
- **Out-of-pocket towing**: $75-200+ without coverage
- **Excess mileage**: $3-5 per mile beyond included distance

For context, a standard flatbed tow in most metro areas runs $125-175 for the first 10 miles, then climbs fast. Coverage that costs you $180 a year in premium can pay for itself in one incident.

## When Coverage Doesn't Apply

State Farm roadside assistance excludes:
- Towing due to accidents (covered under collision/comprehensive)
- Commercial vehicles over certain weight limits
- Off-road recovery in some policies
- Towing to your preferred shop if farther than nearest facility

If your breakdown is accident-related, that claim goes through your collision or comprehensive coverage, not roadside assistance. Those are separate buckets entirely, and mixing them up can slow down your claim.

Off-road recovery is a common gray area. If you drove off a paved surface intentionally and got stuck, many standard policies won't cover the winch-out. Check your declarations page or call your agent directly before you need it.

## Common Questions

**Q: Does using roadside assistance raise my State Farm insurance rates?**
A: Generally no. Roadside assistance claims are not considered at-fault incidents and typically do not affect your premium. However, excessive use in a short period could prompt a review, so confirm with your agent if you're concerned.

**Q: What happens if I run out of service calls mid-year?**
A: You can still call State Farm, but you'll pay the full towing cost out of pocket. You can also pay a private towing company directly. Keep a backup like a credit card with a decent limit accessible for this scenario.

**Q: Can I choose which repair shop the tow truck takes my car to?**
A: You can request a specific shop, but State Farm's coverage only guarantees payment to the nearest qualified facility within your mileage limit. If your preferred shop is farther away, you cover the extra mileage costs at the per-mile rate in your policy.

## Stay Safe

- Keep your State Farm app updated with current location services
- Store the roadside assistance number in your phone
- Know your policy number and have it accessible
- Ask about remaining service calls before using coverage
- Verify tow destination with the driver matches your choice
