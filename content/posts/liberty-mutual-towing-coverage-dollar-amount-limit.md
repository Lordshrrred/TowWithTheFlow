> **Quick Answer:** Liberty Mutual's standard towing coverage typically covers $100-150 per incident. Enhanced roadside assistance plans can provide $200-500 coverage. Your exact limit is listed on your policy declarations page under "Emergency Road Service" or "Roadside Assistance."

## What To Do

1. **Check your declarations page immediately** - Look for "Emergency Road Service," "Roadside Assistance," or "Towing Coverage" sections
2. **Call Liberty Mutual directly** at 1-800-290-8711 to verify your current coverage limits
3. **Ask the tow truck driver for an estimate** before they hook up your vehicle
4. **Get a written receipt** showing the total towing cost and distance
5. **Submit your claim online** through Liberty Mutual's app or website with your receipt
6. **Pay any amount over your limit** out of pocket at the time of service

Once the tow truck hooks up and moves, the meter is running. Knowing your limit before that happens is the difference between a minor inconvenience and a surprise $200 bill. If you are stuck on a highway after hours, rates climb fast, so step two on that list is worth doing from the side of the road before you wave down help.

## Coverage Details by Policy Type

**Basic Auto Policy:**
- Usually $100-150 per towing incident
- Covers towing to nearest qualified repair facility
- May include one free tow per policy year

"Nearest qualified facility" is the phrase that trips people up most often. If the closest shop is a dealership you distrust, or a shop without the right equipment for your vehicle, you may still be directed there under basic coverage. Upgrading to enhanced roadside is worth it if you drive a specialty, diesel, or high-trim vehicle.

**Enhanced Roadside Assistance:**
- Typically $200-500 per incident
- Higher annual limits (often 3-4 incidents)
- May cover towing to facility of your choice

The ability to choose your shop is a real benefit. Towing a vehicle to a second shop because the first one lacked the diagnostic equipment adds another $75-150 in towing costs that you absorb out of pocket.

**Platinum Coverage:**
- Up to $500+ per incident
- Unlimited distance to preferred repair shop
- Additional perks like rental car coordination

Platinum-level coverage makes the most sense for drivers who regularly travel long distances or live in rural areas where the nearest repair facility could be 40-60 miles out. A 50-mile tow at typical commercial rates runs $150-250 on its own, which already pushes past a basic policy limit.

## What It Might Cost

Standard towing runs $75-125 for local distances under 10 miles. Beyond that, most tow companies charge $2-4 per mile. A 30-mile tow can easily land at $175-225. Long-distance tows, anything over 50 miles, can hit $300-400 or more, especially if a flatbed is required for an all-wheel-drive or low-clearance vehicle.

If your Liberty Mutual limit is $150 and your tow costs $275, you pay $125 out of pocket. That gap grows quickly on highways or in remote areas where the nearest provider charges a premium. Flatbed towing, which is required for many modern vehicles with low ground clearance or all-wheel drive, adds $50-100 over standard wheel-lift rates.

After-hours calls, typically anything between 8 PM and 7 AM, and weekend or holiday service often carry surcharges of $25-75 on top of base rates. Factor that in before assuming your coverage will stretch far enough.

## Common Questions

**Q: Does Liberty Mutual towing coverage reset every year?**
A: Yes, per-incident and annual limits reset at your policy renewal date. If your policy renewed in March and you have a 3-incident annual limit, you get 3 covered tows until the following March regardless of when you use them.

**Q: Will filing a towing claim raise my Liberty Mutual rates?**
A: A single roadside assistance or towing claim typically does not raise your premium the way an at-fault accident would. However, multiple claims in a short period can flag your policy for review, so check your specific policy terms if you use coverage frequently.

**Q: What if the tow costs more than my coverage limit?**
A: You pay the difference directly to the tow company at the time of service. Liberty Mutual reimburses up to your stated limit, and the driver will collect the remainder from you before releasing your vehicle. Always confirm the full estimated cost before they hook up.

## Important Notes

- Distance matters: Coverage may specify "nearest qualified facility"
- Some policies have annual limits, not just per-incident limits
- Luxury or specialty vehicles may exceed standard coverage quickly
- Weekend and holiday towing often costs more
