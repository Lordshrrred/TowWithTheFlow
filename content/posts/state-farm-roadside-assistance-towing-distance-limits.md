> **Quick Answer:** State Farm roadside assistance does not publish a hard mileage cap for towing. Coverage is tied to a dollar limit, not a distance limit. Most policies cover towing to the nearest qualified repair facility. The benefit amount varies by policy, typically $50 to $150 per occurrence. If your tow costs more than your covered amount, you pay the difference out of pocket.

## What To Do

1. **Pull your declarations page before you call.** The roadside assistance benefit amount is listed there. You need that number right now, not after the tow truck arrives. Check your State Farm app or call your agent directly.

2. **Understand "nearest qualified repair facility."** State Farm's standard language covers a tow to the nearest shop that can handle your specific repair, not necessarily the shop you prefer. If you want a tow across town to your trusted mechanic, you may be responsible for the extra miles.

3. **Call State Farm roadside directly at 1-800-732-5246.** Do not call a tow company first and plan to get reimbursed. If you go out of network, the reimbursement process is slower and you risk getting less than the full benefit. Calling through State Farm connects you to their dispatch network, which keeps the claim clean.

4. **Ask the dispatcher two questions before they send the truck.** First: is this tow fully covered under my benefit amount? Second: if the destination I want is farther than the nearest shop, what is my out-of-pocket per mile? Get a name and a confirmation number.

5. **If the tow cost exceeds your benefit, know what you owe.** State Farm pays up to your limit. Anything above that is billed directly to you by the tow operator at their rate, usually $3 to $7 per mile beyond what insurance covers. Have a payment method ready. A [tow truck near you that accepts credit card](/tow-truck-near-me-accepting-credit-card/) is worth confirming before the driver shows up.

6. **Document everything.** Take a photo of the final invoice. Keep the receipt. If you paid out of pocket because dispatch told you to call your own truck, submit for reimbursement through your State Farm account within the claim window, which is typically 60 to 90 days.

7. **If your breakdown is on a freeway or busy road, prioritize safety first.** Get your hazards on, stay behind the guardrail, and do not stand between your car and traffic. Sorting out insurance details from a safe distance is far better than handling it roadside. If you need a reference on staying safe while waiting, see [car died on highway shoulder: is it safe to wait for a tow](/car-died-on-highway-shoulder-safe-to-wait-for-tow/).

## What It Might Cost

If your tow falls within State Farm's covered benefit, your out-of-pocket is zero. Where people get surprised is on longer hauls.

- Local tow (under 10 miles): usually covered in full
- Mid-range tow (10 to 30 miles): may exceed your benefit by $50 to $150
- Long-distance tow (50+ miles): expect to pay $150 to $400+ above your covered amount depending on your market

State Farm's Emergency Road Service add-on is separate from a standard policy. If you added it, your limit is spelled out per occurrence. If you are relying on a comprehensive or collision claim to cover towing after an accident, that situation works differently. Check [does insurance cover towing after an accident deductible](/insurance-covers-towing-after-accident-deductible/) for how that math works.

For comparison, [Progressive's towing reimbursement](/progressive-insurance-towing-reimbursement-amount/) and [Farmers Insurance roadside towing distance](/farmers-insurance-roadside-assistance-towing-distance/) both operate on similar dollar-cap models. State Farm is not unusual here. The industry standard is a per-occurrence dollar limit, not miles.

## Stay Safe

- Turn on hazard lights the moment the car stops moving.
- Exit away from traffic, toward the shoulder or a barrier.
- Do not stand directly behind or in front of your vehicle.
- If you are on a highway at night, put as much distance between yourself and the road as possible while staying visible.
- Keep your phone charged. A dead phone during a breakdown makes everything harder.

## Common Questions

**Q: Does State Farm roadside assistance cover the full cost of a long tow?**
A: Only up to your per-occurrence benefit limit, which is typically $50 to $150. If your tow runs longer than that limit covers, the tow operator bills you directly for the difference at their per-mile rate.

**Q: What if I want to be towed to my regular mechanic instead of the nearest shop?**
A: State Farm's standard coverage applies to the nearest qualified repair facility. If your preferred shop is farther away, you may owe the additional mileage cost out of pocket, so confirm the destination and any extra charges with the dispatcher before the truck is sent.

**Q: Can I call my own tow truck and get reimbursed by State Farm later?**
A: You can, but it creates more friction. Reimbursement takes longer, and you risk receiving less than your full benefit if the tow company is outside State Farm's network. Calling State Farm's roadside line at 1-800-732-5246 first keeps the claim straightforward.
