## What Is the Per-Mile Towing Rate in Detroit Right Now?

Detroit tow companies typically charge $3 to $5 per mile, on top of a hook-up or dispatch fee that usually runs $75 to $100. That hook-up fee is non-negotiable and gets charged the moment the driver arrives, whether you end up using the tow or not.

A quick breakdown based on common Detroit scenarios:

| Distance | Estimated Total |
|---|---|
| 1-5 miles (local, in-city) | $90 to $130 |
| 6-10 miles (e.g. downtown to Midtown) | $115 to $150 |
| 11-20 miles (e.g. I-94 to Dearborn or Ferndale) | $145 to $195 |
| 21-30 miles (e.g. downtown to Sterling Heights) | $185 to $250 |

Flatbed towing costs 20 to 30 percent more. If your car is all-wheel drive, lowered, or has a seized wheel, flatbed is required and the driver will tell you upfront. If you're unsure whether your car qualifies for a flatbed, [this breakdown explains exactly when you need one](/how-do-i-know-if-my-car-needs-a-flatbed-tow/).

## What Pushes the Bill Higher on Detroit Highways and Surface Streets?

Several things make your final number jump past the base estimate.

**Location on a highway:** Breaking down on I-75 near the Fisher Freeway interchange or on I-96 near the Jefferies puts you in a high-traffic, higher-risk zone. Some companies add a $15 to $30 highway or incident surcharge for retrieval from live freeway lanes. MDOT's Freeway Courtesy Patrol covers stretches of I-75, I-94, I-96, and M-10, and that service is free if they reach you first, but coverage is not guaranteed and wait times vary.

**After-hours calls:** A breakdown at midnight on the Lodge Freeway versus 2 p.m. on a Tuesday can mean a 20 to 40 percent price difference. Evening, overnight, and weekend calls routinely carry a surcharge.

**Vehicle type:** A Ram 2500 or a Ford Expedition costs more to tow than a Civic. Heavy-duty tow rates start around $125 for the hook-up alone.

**Winching or recovery:** If your car is in a ditch, off the shoulder on I-696, or stuck in a flooded underpass near the Conner Avenue area, that is a recovery, not a standard tow. Recovery adds $75 to $200 or more depending on how complicated the extraction is.

## Does Insurance or Roadside Assistance Cover the Per-Mile Cost?

It depends entirely on your policy. If you have roadside assistance through your insurer, most plans cover a fixed dollar amount per tow, typically $50 to $100, and you pay anything over that limit. A 15-mile tow in Detroit could leave you with a $50 to $80 out-of-pocket balance even with coverage active.

## How Do I Avoid Getting Overcharged on a Detroit Tow?

Ask for the full quote before you authorize anything. Any reputable operator will give you the hook-up fee, the per-mile rate, and the estimated total in writing (or text) before they attach to your car. If they refuse, call someone else.

Three specific things to nail down:

1. **Confirm the destination address and the mileage.** Some drivers take a longer route. Get the quoted mileage confirmed before they load the car.
2. **Ask directly if there is a highway or after-hours surcharge.** Do not assume it is included in the quoted rate.
3. **Know where you want the car dropped.** If you say "anywhere nearby" or pick a shop on the fly, you lose negotiating position on distance. Decide on your mechanic before you call.

If you do end up with a bill that looks inflated once you see it itemized, [this guide walks through how to dispute a towing charge after a roadside emergency](/how-to-dispute-a-towing-charge-after-roadside-emergency/).

## Common Questions

**Q: What is the average total towing bill in Detroit for a short local tow?**
A: For a tow of 5 miles or less within the city, expect $90 to $130 all in. That covers the hook-up fee and the per-mile rate at typical Detroit market prices.

**Q: Does MDOT's Freeway Courtesy Patrol charge for towing on Detroit freeways?**
A: No. The Freeway Courtesy Patrol that operates on sections of I-75, I-94, and I-96 in metro Detroit is free, but they do not tow your car. They push you to a safe spot, change tires, add fuel, and help you call a tow truck. The tow itself still costs you.

**Q: Can a tow company hold my car until I pay?**
A: Yes. Michigan law allows tow operators to hold a vehicle until the bill is paid. They can also charge storage fees that accumulate daily, often $40 to $75 per day, so do not leave a car sitting at a tow yard in Detroit.

**Q: Is a tow cheaper if I call a company directly instead of going through my insurance?**
A: Sometimes. If your insurance plan's per-tow reimbursement cap is low, calling a company directly and negotiating may cost you the same or less, and avoids a potential claim on your record. Get quotes from two operators if you have time.

**Q: How much does a tow from Detroit to a suburb like Troy or Ann Arbor cost per mile?**
A: Detroit to Troy is roughly 20 miles. At $4 per mile plus a $90 hook-up fee, that comes to about $170. Detroit to Ann Arbor is around 40 miles, putting you at $250 or more before any surcharges. For longer hauls, ask specifically about long-distance flat rates, some companies offer them.
