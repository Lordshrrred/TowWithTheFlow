> **Quick Answer:** Sometimes. Progressive covers towing after an accident if you carry collision coverage (which pays for the tow as part of the claim) or the optional Roadside Assistance add-on. Liability-only policies don't pay for your tow. If another driver caused the crash, their insurer may owe you the tow cost. Call the tow first if you're unsafe, then sort out payment.

## Does Progressive Pay for My Tow After a Crash?

Progressive pays for a post-accident tow only if your policy includes collision coverage or the Roadside Assistance add-on. A liability-only policy covers damage you cause to others, not your own car or its tow.

Here's how each piece works:

- **Collision coverage:** Towing from the accident scene to a shop or yard is generally treated as part of the claim, subject to your deductible and the policy terms. You file the claim, and the tow is part of the loss.
- **Roadside Assistance add-on:** This is a separate, low-cost option. It's aimed at breakdowns, but many drivers have it and use it after a drivable-but-risky fender bender. Check your policy for the towing limit, because it often caps by mileage or dollar amount. Our breakdown of the [Progressive roadside assistance towing coverage limit](/progressive-roadside-assistance-towing-coverage-limit/) covers what the cap actually looks like.
- **Liability only:** No tow coverage for your vehicle. You'd pay out of pocket or recover it from the at-fault driver.

Pull up your declarations page in the Progressive app. It lists exactly which coverages you carry, which settles this in about 30 seconds.

## What Do I Do Right Now If I Was Just in an Accident and Need a Tow?

Get everyone safe first, then call 911 if anyone is hurt or the car is blocking traffic. Payment questions can wait until you're out of danger.

1. Turn on hazards. If the car moves and you're on a highway, get to the shoulder. If it doesn't, stay buckled inside with hazards on unless there's fire or smoke. Standing on a live interstate is how people get hit.
2. Photograph everything: all four sides of each vehicle, the damage, the road, plates, and the other driver's insurance card.
3. If police respond, get the report number. Progressive and any other insurer will ask for it.
4. Call Progressive's claims line from the app or your ID card, or ask the officer on scene which tow company is being dispatched.
5. Ask the tow driver where the car is going and get that in writing or text before they hook up.

One real tip: if a police-called tow company shows up, you generally still have a say in where your car goes. Tell them your preferred shop or ask for a storage address and daily rate before you agree. Storage fees at an impound lot start accruing fast, and they can dwarf the tow itself.

## Who Pays for the Tow If the Other Driver Caused the Accident?

If the other driver was at fault, their liability insurance should ultimately pay for your tow, but you may need to front the money or use your own coverage first.

Their insurer owes you reasonable towing and storage as part of your property damage claim. In practice, adjusters move slowly, and the tow company wants payment when your car is released. Many drivers pay first, keep the receipt, and get reimbursed

## How Much Does a Post-Accident Tow Cost Out of Pocket?

Expect roughly $75 to $200 for a local tow, and more for flatbed service, heavy damage, or a long haul. Accident recovery costs more than a plain breakdown tow because of the extra labor.

Several things push the price up:

- **Winching or recovery:** A car in a ditch or against a guardrail costs extra.
- **Flatbed requirement:** Cars with all-wheel drive or serious suspension damage usually need one. See [towing a car with all wheel drive](/towing-a-car-with-all-wheel-drive-damage-risks/) for why.
- **After-hours or weekend rates:** Nights and weekends run higher.
- **Storage:** Daily fees at the yard add up quickly.

If a bill looks inflated, you have options. Read [how to dispute a towing charge if overcharged](/how-to-dispute-a-towing-charge-if-overcharged/) before you pay.

## Should I Call Progressive Before or After the Tow Truck Arrives?

Call Progressive as soon as you're safe, ideally before the car is hooked up. That lets them tell you what's covered and where the car can go.

If you can't reach them in time, don't leave a damaged car in a dangerous spot to wait on hold. Get it moved, keep every receipt, and report the claim afterward. Progressive can usually still reimburse a covered tow after the fact if you document it.

## Common Questions

**Q: Does Progressive cover towing if I only have liability insurance?**
A: No. Liability pays for damage you cause to others, not your own car's tow. You'd pay out of pocket or recover the cost from the at-fault driver's insurer.

**Q: Will using Progressive for a tow raise my rates?**
A: A roadside tow alone usually doesn't. An at-fault accident claim can, regardless of the tow. Not-at-fault claims are generally treated more favorably, but your state and history matter.

**Q: How far will Progressive tow my car after an accident?**
A: It depends on your coverage and the claim. Roadside Assistance often has a mileage or dollar limit. Collision claims typically cover the tow to a reasonable repair facility. Confirm the limit with Progressive before agreeing to a long haul. See [how far will insurance tow my car for free](/how-far-will-insurance-tow-my-car-for-free/).

**Q: Can I choose the body shop my car gets towed to?**
A: Generally yes. You can request a specific shop, though insurers may suggest preferred ones. Don't let a tow company talk you into their own lot if it means storage fees you can avoid.

**Q: What if I don't have Roadside Assistance and the accident wasn't my fault?**
A: Pay the tow, keep the receipt, and submit it to the at-fault driver's insurer as part of your property damage claim. You can also file under your collision coverage if you have it.
