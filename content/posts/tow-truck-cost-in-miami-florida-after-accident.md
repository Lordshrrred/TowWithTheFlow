> **Quick Answer:** A tow truck in Miami after an accident costs $100 to $200 for a short local haul, and $250 to $400 or more if you're on I-95 near the Golden Glades interchange, the Palmetto Expressway (SR-826), or anywhere that requires a flatbed and a longer run. If police dispatch the tow, expect the higher end. Your insurance or AAA can cut that bill significantly.

## How Much Does a Tow Truck Actually Cost in Miami After an Accident?

Most Miami-Dade tow companies charge a hookup fee of $75 to $125, then $3 to $5 per mile after the first few. A 10-mile flatbed tow from a crash scene on the Dolphin Expressway (SR-836) to a body shop in Doral runs roughly $125 to $175 out of pocket. If your car is heavily damaged, stuck in a median, or needs a winch recovery, add $50 to $150 on top of that.

Flatbed trucks are standard for accident tows because they protect cars with airbag deployment or frame damage. If a company offers a wheel-lift for a wrecked car, push back. That's the wrong equipment.

## Who Dispatches the Tow, and Does It Change the Price?

Yes, it changes the price significantly. If Miami-Dade Police or FHP dispatch the tow, they pull from a county rotation list. Those companies are rate-regulated, but you still pay the going rate, and you have no say in which company shows up. If you call your own tow company before police arrive, you can shop the rate.

On high-speed corridors like I-95 through Overtown or SR-112 into Miami International Airport, FHP often dispatches quickly and the tow truck arrives before you've had a chance to make a call. In that case, get the tow company's name and invoice immediately, and keep it for your insurance claim.

## What Will My Insurance Actually Cover?

If you carry roadside assistance or comprehensive coverage, your insurer will typically cover the tow to the nearest qualified repair facility. State Farm, GEICO, and AAA all cover accident tows, but each has a dollar limit or mileage cap. [Check what GEICO's towing distance limit actually covers](/geico-roadside-assistance-towing-distance-limit/) before you assume the full bill is gone.

The at-fault driver's liability insurance does not pay for your tow on the spot. You'll need to file a claim and wait for reimbursement. Pay the tow driver, get a receipt, and document everything. If there's a dispute about fault on something like a multi-car pileup near the I-95/I-195 split, the reimbursement process takes longer. [Knowing whether insurance covers a non-driveable tow](/does-insurance-cover-towing-if-car-is-not-driveable/) helps you set the right expectations up front.

## What Do I Do Right Now If I Just Had an Accident in Miami?

Move to safety first. On I-95 or SR-826, stay behind the barrier or guardrail if possible. Florida has a Move It law: if the cars are drivable and no one is injured, move them out of the travel lane. If the car is not drivable, turn on hazard lights and stay away from live traffic lanes.

Call 911 if there are injuries. Then call your insurance company's roadside line or AAA before police dispatch a tow. That call gives you control over which company takes your car. If you're near the Golden Glades interchange in North Miami, the Palmetto near Hialeah, or the I-95/836 junction downtown, traffic is dense and tow trucks arrive fast. Have your insurance card ready and know your destination shop before the truck hooks up. Once it's on the hook, changing the drop location usually costs extra.

## Common Questions

**Q: How much is a flatbed tow in Miami after a crash?**
A: A flatbed tow in Miami runs $125 to $250 for most accident scenarios within Miami-Dade County. Longer hauls, winch recovery, or after-hours service push that to $300 to $400.

**Q: Can I refuse the tow truck that police dispatch after my accident in Miami?**
A: Yes. You can call your own towing company instead of accepting the police-dispatched rotation truck. Do it quickly, before the rotation truck hooks up your car.

**Q: Does AAA cover towing after an accident in Miami, or only breakdowns?**
A: AAA covers towing after accidents on most membership tiers, not just breakdowns. The tow needs to go to a qualified repair facility and must fall within your plan's mileage limit. [Here is exactly what AAA covers in accident situations](/does-aaa-cover-towing-after-accident-or-only-breakdowns/).

**Q: How long can I leave my car at a Miami tow yard after an accident?**
A: Storage fees start accumulating fast, typically $40 to $75 per day. Get your car moved to your chosen repair shop or a friend's property within 24 hours to avoid a large storage bill on top of the tow.

**Q: What if the other driver was at fault? Do I still pay for the tow upfront?**
A: Yes, you pay the tow driver upfront. Save every receipt. You then submit the tow bill to the at-fault driver's liability insurance as part of your property damage claim. Reimbursement is not instant.
