> **Quick Answer:** GEICO covers towing and roadside help only if your policy includes the optional Emergency Road Service add-on. It's not automatic. With it, you get towing, flat tire changes, jump-starts, lockout help, and fuel delivery. Without it, you pay out of pocket. Check your policy in the GEICO app or call the number on your insurance card before you spend anything.

## Does GEICO Automatically Cover Towing?

No. GEICO does not include towing in a standard auto policy. It's an add-on called Emergency Road Service, and you either bought it or you didn't.

The one exception: if you were in a covered accident, your collision coverage can pay for the tow from the scene, since towing a wrecked car is part of the claim. That's a different thing from a breakdown. Dead battery, blown tire, or an engine that quit on you is not an accident, so collision won't touch it. More on that distinction in [does my car insurance cover towing if I cause the accident](/does-my-car-insurance-cover-towing-if-i-cause-the-accident/).

## How Do I Find Out If I Have Emergency Road Service Right Now?

Open the GEICO Mobile app or log in at geico.com and look at your policy coverages. Emergency Road Service is listed by name if you have it. No app handy? Check your declarations page, the summary document GEICO emailed or mailed when you bought or renewed. Or call the customer service number printed on your insurance card and ask.

Do this before you call a tow truck. If you're covered, GEICO dispatches the tow through its own network, and calling a random company first can mean you pay and wait for reimbursement, or get denied outright.

## What Does GEICO Emergency Road Service Actually Cover?

It covers the common breakdown fixes: towing, tire changes, jump-starts, lockouts, and fuel delivery. Here's the typical lineup:

- **Towing:** to a repair shop, within the limits of your policy
- **Flat tire:** they swap on your spare (you need a usable spare)
- **Jump-start:** for a dead battery
- **Lockout service:** they get you back in the car
- **Fuel delivery:** they bring gas, but you pay for the fuel itself
- **Winching:** if you're stuck in mud, sand, or a ditch near the road

Limits matter. Towing distance and dollar caps vary by state and policy, so confirm yours before assuming a long haul is covered. Our breakdown of [how far insurance will tow your car after a breakdown](/how-far-will-insurance-tow-my-car-after-a-breakdown/) explains why a 40-mile tow can turn into a bill.

Parts and repairs are never included. They tow you to the shop. You pay for the fix.

## How Much Does the GEICO Roadside Add-On Cost?

It's usually cheap, often just a few dollars per vehicle per policy period, but the price depends on your state, vehicle, and policy. I won't quote you a number I can't verify. Your quote or declarations page shows the exact line item.

## What If I Don't Have It and I'm Stuck Right Now?

Get safe first, then decide how to pay. If you're on a highway, get the car as far onto the shoulder as possible, turn on your hazards, and get everyone out and away from traffic if it's unsafe to stay inside. Call 911 if you're in a live lane or feel unsafe.

Then work through these in order:

1. **Call GEICO anyway.** Agents can sometimes add Emergency Road Service by phone. Be aware many insurers impose a waiting period before new roadside coverage applies, so ask directly instead of assuming it kicks in instantly.
2. **Check your other coverage.** Some credit cards, new-car warranties, and manufacturer programs include free roadside help. Look at the card you used to buy the car or the warranty paperwork.
3. **Ask for the full price before the hook-up.** Get the total, including hook-up fee, mileage, and any after-hours surcharge, in writing or by text before the driver touches your car.
4. **Know your tow type.** All-wheel-drive and many low-clearance cars need a flatbed. Our [flatbed tow checklist](/how-to-know-if-my-car-needs-a-flatbed-tow/) takes about a minute.

## Common Questions

**Q: Does GEICO cover towing if I don't have roadside assistance?**
A: Generally no. A breakdown tow requires the Emergency Road Service add-on. The exception is a covered accident, where collision coverage may pay for towing the car from the scene as part of the claim.

**Q: How do I call GEICO for a tow?**
A: Use the GEICO Mobile app to request service, or call the number on your insurance card. Have your location, policy number, and vehicle details ready. Don't use a random number from a web search.

**Q: Will GEICO reimburse me if I paid for a tow myself?**
A: Only if you have Emergency Road Service, and reimbursement rules and caps apply. Keep your itemized receipt and contact GEICO claims as soon as you can. Without the add-on, expect no reimbursement.

**Q: Does GEICO roadside assistance cover a flat tire?**
A: Yes, with Emergency Road Service, they'll install your spare. If you don't have a usable spare, they can typically tow you instead. Read more in [does roadside assistance cover a flat tire or just towing](/does-roadside-assistance-cover-flat-tire-or-just-towing/).

**Q: Can I add GEICO roadside assistance after my car breaks down?**
A: You can ask, but coverage added mid-breakdown is often not honored for that event, and waiting periods may apply. Treat it as protection for the next breakdown, not this one.
