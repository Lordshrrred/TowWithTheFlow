> **Quick Answer:** Progressive's roadside assistance add-on covers towing to the nearest qualified repair facility, with no hard mileage cap written into the base benefit. In practice, "nearest facility" language is the real limit: if you want your car towed further than that, you pay the overage. Costs beyond what Progressive approves are billed directly to you by the tow company.

## How Far Will Progressive Actually Tow My Car?

Progressive's roadside assistance does not publish a fixed mile number the way some competitors do. The coverage is written as towing to the nearest qualified repair facility. That phrase does real work: if you break down on an interstate and there's a repair shop five miles away, that's likely all they'll authorize. Wanting your car towed 40 miles to your regular mechanic instead is a separate conversation, and often a separate bill.

The "nearest" standard is common across insurance-backed roadside plans. For a direct comparison of how other insurers handle mileage caps, the breakdown at [GEICO Roadside Assistance Towing Reimbursement Limit](/geico-roadside-assistance-towing-reimbursement-limit/) shows how this plays out across programs.

## What Happens If I Need a Tow Beyond the Covered Distance?

You pay the difference. Progressive will cover what the plan allows, and the tow company bills you for the rest. There's no automatic override for preference or convenience. The dispatch operator may negotiate a maximum dollar amount rather than a mile count depending on how your specific policy is written, so call the number on your insurance card before the truck hooks up. Once it's moving, the meter is running.

If your policy has a dollar cap per incident instead of a mileage cap, that's actually the binding limit. Check your declarations page. A local tow running $150 to $200 for the first several miles can eat through a low dollar cap fast. For more detail on how per-incident dollar limits work on Progressive's policies specifically, see [Progressive Insurance Towing Coverage Limit Per Incident](/progressive-insurance-towing-coverage-limit-per-incident/).

## Can I Choose Which Shop My Car Gets Towed To?

You can ask, but the coverage only obligates Progressive to pay for the nearest qualified shop. If you direct the tow to a shop farther away, you're likely paying the mileage beyond what the nearest shop would have required. "Qualified" typically means a licensed repair facility, not just any parking lot. If your preferred mechanic is two towns over, price out the extra miles before you commit. A tow running $5 to $7 per mile past the covered destination adds up fast on a 20 or 30 mile overage.

## What Do I Do Right Now If Progressive's Coverage Isn't Enough?

Call the roadside assistance number on your card first and get the authorization in writing via text or email before the tow starts. Ask the dispatcher exactly how many miles or how many dollars the claim will cover. If the covered amount won't get your car where it needs to go, you have three options: accept the nearest facility and arrange a second tow later, pay the full overage yourself and submit for partial reimbursement, or call a private tow company directly and pay out of pocket if it's actually cheaper.

Second tows on the same day are possible but have their own cost structure. See [How Much Does a Second Tow Cost Same Day](/how-much-does-a-second-tow-cost-same-day/) for what to expect if you need to move the car again after the first tow drops it.

## Common Questions

**Q: Does Progressive roadside assistance have a mileage limit per tow?**
A: Not a fixed mileage number in most policies. Coverage is tied to towing to the nearest qualified repair facility. The real-world limit is how far that nearest shop is from your breakdown location.

**Q: What if the nearest shop can't fix my car?**
A: Progressive's obligation is to get your car there, not to guarantee repairs. If that shop can't handle the job, you'll need to arrange and likely pay for a second tow to a facility that can.

**Q: Does Progressive roadside assistance cover towing after an accident?**
A: Roadside assistance add-ons typically cover mechanical breakdowns, not accident scenes. Accident towing usually falls under your collision coverage. Check your policy declarations page to confirm which coverage applies.

**Q: How do I get reimbursed if I paid for a tow out of pocket under Progressive?**
A: Call Progressive's claims line and ask about reimbursement for roadside expenses. You'll need the tow receipt showing the date, distance, and total cost. Reimbursement is not guaranteed beyond what your policy authorizes, but submit the claim anyway.

**Q: Can I use Progressive roadside assistance more than once in a year?**
A: Progressive's roadside add-on does not publicly advertise a per-year use cap the way some insurers do. However, frequent claims can affect your renewal terms. Call your agent if you expect to need it repeatedly.
