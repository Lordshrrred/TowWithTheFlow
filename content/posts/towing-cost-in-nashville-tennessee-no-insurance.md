Without insurance or a roadside plan, you're paying the full rate directly to the tow company. Nashville has no shortage of tow operators, but rates vary by zone, time of day, and truck type. Here's what to expect before you make that call.

> **Quick Answer:** Expect to pay $75 to $125 for a short local tow in Nashville, typically covering the first 5 to 7 miles. After that, most companies charge $3 to $5 per mile. A flatbed costs more than a wheel-lift. Night and weekend calls add $25 to $50 on top of the base rate. Have a card ready, because cash is not always accepted.

## How Much Does a Tow Actually Cost Out of Pocket in Nashville?

A standard local tow in Nashville runs $75 to $125 for the hook-up fee plus the first few miles. That covers most breakdowns within the same part of the city, say from Midtown to the Gulch, or from East Nashville across the Cumberland River into downtown.

Longer hauls get more expensive fast. If you're stranded on I-65 near the Brentwood exit and need to get to a shop in Antioch, you're looking at 10 to 15 miles. At $4 per mile after the base charge, that adds $40 to $60 on top of your hook-up fee. Total out of pocket: $115 to $185.

Flatbed trucks, which are required for all-wheel-drive vehicles and most modern low-clearance cars, typically run $20 to $40 more than a wheel-lift tow for the same distance. See [towing cost in Nashville Tennessee per mile rate](/towing-cost-nashville-tennessee-per-mile-rate/) for a full mile-by-mile breakdown.

## What If I Break Down on I-24 or I-40 Inside Nashville?

Get off the travel lane immediately, turn on your hazard lights, and call for a tow before you do anything else. On I-24 near the Spaghetti Junction interchange or on I-40 through the Nations neighborhood, stopping in a live lane is extremely dangerous. Get as far onto the shoulder as possible.

TDOT's HELP trucks patrol Nashville interstates during peak hours and can push your car to a safer spot for free, but they do not tow you to a shop. You still need to call a private tow company for that. Expect a 30 to 60 minute wait for a private truck during rush hour on I-24 or I-65.

Highway breakdowns may also trigger a faster response because some stretches of Nashville interstate have incident management contracts. The tow operator who responds may charge a set rate negotiated with TDOT, which can be lower than a cold-call private rate. Ask before they hook up your vehicle.

## Does It Cost More at Night or on Weekends in Nashville?

Yes, almost always. Most Nashville tow companies charge an after-hours surcharge for calls between roughly 9 p.m. and 7 a.m., typically $25 to $50 extra. Weekend rates, especially Saturday night, run at the higher end because demand is high and crews are limited.

If you break down late on Broadway near Lower Broad or after a Predators game near Bridgestone Arena, you are competing with a surge in calls. Wait times stretch, and some operators prioritize higher-paying commercial accounts. Plan for a longer wait and a higher bill.

The total for a late-night flatbed tow from downtown Nashville to a shop in Green Hills, roughly 7 miles, can run $160 to $210 after dark.

## Common Questions

**Q: How much does a tow truck cost in Nashville if I have no roadside assistance?**
A: Plan on $75 to $125 for a short local tow, plus $3 to $5 per mile beyond the first 5 miles. Night and weekend calls add $25 to $50 to that base.

**Q: Can I negotiate towing prices in Nashville?**
A: With private companies, yes. Ask for a cash discount or call a second company for a competing quote. You will not negotiate with a police-dispatched tow, those rates are sometimes set by contract.

**Q: How long will it take for a tow truck to reach me on I-65 in Nashville?**
A: During off-peak hours, 20 to 40 minutes is typical. During rush hour on I-65 near the I-440 split, expect 45 to 75 minutes. TDOT HELP trucks may reach you sooner but cannot tow you to a shop.

**Q: Do Nashville tow companies take credit cards?**
A: Most do, but not all. Confirm payment methods when you call to dispatch. Some smaller operators are cash only, especially after hours.

**Q: What happens if my car gets towed and I can't pay the bill?**
A: The tow company can hold your vehicle until the bill is paid. Storage fees accumulate daily, sometimes $35 to $60 per day. Do not leave the car sitting. Negotiate a payment arrangement directly with the operator before walking away.
