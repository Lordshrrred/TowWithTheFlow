> **Quick Answer:** A local tow in Memphis runs $75 to $125 for the first few miles, with per-mile rates of $3 to $5 after that. Flatbed service adds $20 to $50 on top of the base rate. Expect after-hours, holiday, and highway calls to push the total higher. If you're on I-40 near the I-240 split or stuck in Midtown, budget $100 to $175 for most short hauls.

## What Does a Local Tow Actually Cost in Memphis Right Now?

A standard wheel-lift tow within Memphis city limits runs $75 to $125 for the hookup and first few miles, then $3 to $5 per mile beyond that. Flatbed service, which you need for AWD vehicles, low-clearance cars, and accident-damaged vehicles, starts closer to $95 to $145 and carries the same per-mile rate. Those numbers hold for daytime calls on a weekday. Call after 9 p.m., on a Sunday, or on a holiday and most Memphis operators add a $25 to $50 surcharge automatically.

A short crosstown haul, say from Poplar Avenue near East Memphis to a shop in Midtown, usually lands between $110 and $160 total. A longer drag from the I-55 bridge area down to Germantown or Collierville can hit $200 or more depending on the company.

## What Are the Extra Fees That Push the Final Bill Up?

Memphis tow companies commonly tack on additional charges that don't appear in the quoted base rate. The hookup fee, sometimes called an initial service charge, is legitimate and expected. Watch for these others:

- **Fuel surcharge:** $10 to $25, often buried in the invoice
- **Winching or recovery fee:** $50 to $150 if your car is off the road surface, in a ditch, or up against a barrier on I-240
- **Storage fee:** $40 to $75 per day if your car sits on the lot overnight before you claim it
- **After-hours dispatch:** $25 to $50 added to the base rate after 9 p.m.

Ask for the total estimate before the driver hooks up your vehicle. A reputable company will give you a number. If they refuse, that is a signal to call a different operator. You do have the right to [negotiate tow truck price after a breakdown](/can-you-negotiate-tow-truck-price-after-breakdown/) before the tow begins, not after.

## What Do I Do If I Break Down on I-40 or I-240 in Memphis?

Get off the travel lane immediately. If you can't move the car, put it in park, turn on your hazard lights, and get out on the passenger side away from traffic. I-40 through Memphis carries heavy commercial truck traffic, especially near the I-240 interchange and the Lamar Avenue exit. Do not stand behind your vehicle.

Call 911 first if you're in an active lane or if there's any safety risk. For a simple breakdown on the shoulder, call a tow directly or your roadside assistance plan. Tennessee's TDOT HELP trucks patrol major interstates including I-40, I-240, and I-55 during peak hours and can push your car to a safe location at no charge, but they don't tow to a shop. If your car died suddenly and won't restart, a tow is the right call. ## Does Insurance or AAA Cover Towing in Memphis?

Most comprehensive auto insurance policies include roadside assistance as an add-on, and it typically covers $50 to $100 of towing cost per incident. That covers a short local Memphis tow most of the time,

AAA Basic covers towing up to 5 miles, which in Memphis traffic geography might not even get you from the I-40/Covington Pike interchange to the nearest dealership. AAA Plus bumps coverage to 100 miles, which handles nearly any in-metro or mid-state tow. ## How Do I Avoid Getting Overcharged by a Memphis Tow Company?

Call your insurance company or a known dispatch service first rather than accepting help from a truck that pulled up uninvited. Predatory tow operators do target breakdown scenes in Memphis, particularly on I-40 near Sycamore View Road and on surface streets in areas with heavy commuter traffic. An unsolicited truck showing up at your accident scene is worth being skeptical about. ## Common Questions

**Q: How much does a tow truck cost in Memphis for a short local trip?**
A: Expect $75 to $125 for the base hookup and first few miles, then $3 to $5 per mile after that. A typical Midtown to East Memphis haul runs $100 to $160 depending on the company and time of day.

**Q: Is there a cheaper towing option in Memphis if I have no insurance?**
A: Without insurance or a roadside plan, you're paying full out-of-pocket rates. Call two or three local operators and get a quote before committing. Some Memphis companies offer slightly lower rates for cash payment, but don't expect a major discount.

**Q: Do TDOT HELP trucks on Memphis interstates tow your car for free?**
A: TDOT HELP trucks will push your car to a safe shoulder location and can call a tow for you, but they do not haul your vehicle to a shop. Their service is free, but you still need a paid tow company to get your car off the highway.

**Q: What's the after-hours towing rate in Memphis?**
A: Most Memphis operators add $25 to $50 for calls after 9 p.m., on weekends, or on holidays. A tow that costs $110 during business hours can easily run $150 to $175 at midnight.

**Q: How long does a Memphis tow truck take to arrive?**
A: Typical wait times run 30 to 60 minutes for most Memphis locations. During heavy traffic on I-40 or I-240 or during bad weather, expect 60 to 90 minutes. Giving your exact cross street or exit number speeds up dispatch.
