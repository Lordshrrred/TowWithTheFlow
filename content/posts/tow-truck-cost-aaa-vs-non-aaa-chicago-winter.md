> **Quick Answer:** AAA members in Chicago pay $0-200 for winter tows (depending on plan and distance). Non-AAA customers face $150-400+ with winter surge pricing. AAA Basic covers 5 miles free, Plus covers 100 miles. Non-AAA rates jump 25-50% during snowstorms.

## What To Do

1. **Check your AAA membership status** on the app or call 1-800-AAA-HELP
2. **If you have AAA**, call immediately for fastest service during winter storms
3. **If no AAA**, call multiple local tow companies for quotes before choosing
4. **Ask about winter surcharges** upfront, many companies add 25-50% during snow events
5. **Get total cost estimate** including hookup, mileage, and any weather premiums
6. **For non-AAA emergency signup**, you can join over the phone but service starts 24-48 hours later

A few things worth knowing before you make that call: AAA wait times during a major Chicago snowstorm can stretch 2-4 hours, sometimes longer on the expressways. If you are on the Dan Ryan or I-290 and traffic is gridlocked, dispatchers may route trucks from farther out, adding more time. Calling early in the storm, before conditions peak, usually gets you faster service. If you have AAA Plus or Premier, that 100-200 mile coverage is genuinely useful if you need a tow from the suburbs all the way back into the city or to a specific shop across town. Basic members who go beyond 5 miles often do not realize they owe mileage charges until the truck arrives, so confirm that number before the driver hooks up.

For non-AAA customers, do not accept the first quote. Prices vary significantly between independent operators and chain services. A company dispatched through a third-party app sometimes adds a platform fee on top of standard rates. Ask specifically whether the quoted price is the final out-the-door number, including hookup, mileage, and any storm premium.

## What It Might Cost

**AAA Members:**
- Basic: Free tow up to 5 miles, then $3-5 per additional mile
- Plus: Free tow up to 100 miles
- Premier: Free tow up to 200 miles
- Annual membership: $56-165 depending on level

**Non-AAA Chicago Winter Rates:**
- Base tow: $75-150 hookup fee
- Mileage: $2-8 per mile after first 5-10 miles
- Winter surge: Additional 25-50% during snow emergencies
- Total typical range: $150-400 for local tows

**Distance Examples:**
- Downtown to suburbs (15 miles): AAA Plus free, non-AAA $200-350
- Highway to nearest exit (5 miles): AAA Basic free, non-AAA $150-250

One edge case to watch: if your vehicle needs to be winched out of a ditch or pulled from a snowbank before it can even be loaded onto the truck, that winch-out service is often billed separately. Expect an additional $50-150 on top of standard tow rates. AAA does cover basic winching for members, but confirm with the dispatcher before the truck rolls. For non-AAA customers, ask specifically whether your situation requires a winch, because that word changes the quote immediately.

Also worth noting: if your destination shop is closed when you arrive, some tow companies charge a storage fee starting at around $25-50 per day. In winter, with weekend storms and holiday closures, cars can sit two or three days before a shop reopens. Ask the driver about after-hours drop policies before choosing your destination.

## Common Questions

**Q: Can I use my AAA membership to tow someone else's car, not mine?**
A: Yes, AAA membership covers the member, not the vehicle, so you can use your benefits on any car you are with when the breakdown happens. The member needs to be present at the scene when the truck arrives.

**Q: Do Chicago tow companies actually charge more during a snowstorm, or is that just a scare tactic?**
A: It is real. Many independent operators treat declared snow emergencies as grounds for surge pricing, typically 25-50% above their posted rate. Some larger chain services hold their rates steady but add a winter road conditions fee of $30-75. Always ask for the total before you agree.

**Q: What if I can not afford the tow bill on the spot?**
A: Most tow companies require payment before releasing your vehicle, so you need a card or cash ready. If cost is an issue, call your auto insurance company first since many policies include roadside assistance that reimburses tow costs, or check whether your credit card offers roadside benefits before you call a private tow service.

## Stay Safe

- Keep your phone charged and car charger handy
- Stay in your vehicle if safely off the road
- Turn on hazard lights and keep warm clothes in winter kit
- Have cash or card ready, many tow drivers accept both
- Take photos of your location and send to family before service arrives
- Verify tow truck credentials and get receipt with total charges
